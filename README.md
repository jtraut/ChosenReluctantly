# Chosen, Reluctantly

A 3D isometric action RPG where your skills are cards: real-time combat driven by typed card slots, each with its own shuffled mini-deck. Built around deep class/element build combinations and roguelite runs with persistent between-run progression. Currently a placeholder-visuals prototype: two playable classes (a Fire Rogue and a Fire Wizard, picked at the start of each run), a seeded run of 30 rooms in three acts (MAP3: acts 1 and 2 end in a mini-boss, act 3 in the boss; the run saves at room boundaries and has a Continue button), a branching map you route through (combat, elite, relic chest, rest, merchant, mini-boss, boss; MAP1, MAP2, MAP3) with card rewards and gold after fights, a merchant that sells relics, cards and card removal for that gold (MAP2), a deck-clean choice in the rest room, and a three-phase final boss, The Overly Enthusiastic Guardian (placeholder name). Cards carry tags and keywords, and some card effects have conditions ("if the target is Burning", "if you used Movement in the last 2 s") so cards can set each other up (BD1). Runs also hand out relics (BD2): run-long passives with triggers, offered after the elite room and in a relic room, shown as a chip row on the HUD. BD3a adds a physical (Bleed) Rogue build: Bleed that can stack, a detonate effect that cashes it in, 8 new Rogue cards and 6 relics, and card offers that lean toward the statuses your deck applies. Acts 2 and 3 start with a heal. See `docs/GDD.md` for the design and `docs/ROADMAP.md` for status.

## Running the project
Requires Godot 4.7.x (developed on 4.7.2 stable). Open `project.godot` in the editor and press Play, or use the command line:

```
godot --path . --quit   # smoke-check with no window (verified: exits 0)
godot --path .          # play it: starts a run
```

A normal launch shows the **class pick** (Rogue or Wizard; 1/2 or the arrows highlight, Enter or a click confirms; the HUD is hidden behind it; when a saved run exists a **Continue your run** entry also shows, see "Continue and the save file" below), then starts a **run**. Since MAP3 the run is **three acts of 10 rooms (30 rooms)**, each act its own **branching map** (see "The map" below), generated from the seed; you pick each next room on the map screen, and a short Act screen opens between acts. The paragraph below describes the straight run (`--no-map`) and the screens common to both. `--no-map` gives the older straight run (10 rooms in a row: combat, 1 elite, 1 rest, boss last, and from BD2 one of the combat rooms is a **relic room** with no fight, on on-screen rooms 3 to 9), which the rest of this paragraph describes; see `data/run.json` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 2. The room indicator at the top right shows "Room X of Y - Type" with a shape per type (circle combat, diamond elite, plus rest, star boss; the relic room's plate reads "Relics"). After a won combat room you pick a reward card (see below); after the elite room you pick a relic instead (see Relics below); a rest room offers heal or remove a card; any other room shows a "Room cleared" panel with Continue. The last room is the boss (`data/bosses.json`): its HP bar shows at the top centre with its name, phase notches and the attack it is winding up. Killing it ends with a short death beat, then the stats screen; Game Over shows "Reached room X of Y". Try again and New run start a new seed. `scenes/main/Main.tscn` is the endless arena, still used by tests, scenarios, the wave sim and VpCapture without `--run`.

### Launch options
Game args go after `--`, for example `godot --path . -- --run-seed=7 --run-room=10`. Options marked "run" are read only by a normal run launch (RunMain) and are ignored with `--scenario` or in the endless arena. "First run" means Try again and New run do not repeat it; "whole launch" means they do. Parsed in `src/run/RunController.gd` (`_apply_launch_args`), `src/run/DebugDecks.gd` (also `--class`), `src/ui/ClassPickScreen.gd`, `src/ui/RunPromptScreen.gd` and `src/debug/CaptureArgs.gd`.

| Option | What it does | Example |

| `--run-room=K` | Run: start at room K (1-based), first run only. On a three-act map run K counts over the whole run, 1 to 30 (14 is act 2's room 4; 30 is the final boss); anything else exits 2 with "Launch refused: ..." (BD3a; verified for 31, 0, `1.5`, `abc` and a bare `--run-room`, and for `--acts=1 --run-room=11`). With the map on, the path is laid along the first route (lowest column) in every act before it, and no gold is paid for the skipped rooms. With `--no-map` or `--acts=1` the run has 10 rooms. | `godot --path . -- --run-seed=1 --run-room=30` (final boss), `-- --run-seed=1 --acts=1 --run-room=10` (the one-act boss) |

Notes:
- `--deck` errors exit 2 with "Launch refused: ..." on stderr: unknown name, `--deck` given twice, a bare `--deck`, or a preset that is invalid (each verified except the invalid-preset case, which is covered by tests). There is no rule bypass: both lists of a preset must pass the deck rules.
- `--class` also exits 2 with "Launch refused: ..." for a bare `--class`, `--class` twice, an unknown class, or a class that disagrees with the `--deck` preset (each verified; only the "Launch refused: ..." line is printed, with no stack trace). A preset names its class (`"class"` in the file; `--deck` alone sets the class). `--class` is ignored in the arena (the arena is always the Rogue). With `--scenario` it plays the scenario as that class (a scenario can also set its own `character`, which sets the body look). A bare, repeated or unknown `--class` with a scenario, or one that disagrees with the scenario's own `character`, exits 2 with "Launch refused: ..." (BD1-B; a plain `--scenario` with no `--class` is unaffected).
- `--relics`, `--relic-offer` and `--relic-cap` exit 2 with "Launch refused: ..." for an unknown relic id, an id listed twice, an offer longer than `relics.offer_count`, or a cap that is not a whole number from 1 (each verified). They are read by the run (`RunController._apply_relic_args`, `src/run/RelicArgs.gd`) and are ignored in the arena and with `--scenario`; a scenario that needs relics lists them under its own `relics` key.
- The class pick screen opens on every run, including Try again and New run, and pauses the room behind it. It is skipped when `--class` or `--deck` is given, in headless runs, and in the arena. The last pick is preselected and, in windowed play only, remembered in `user://last_class.json` (`src/run/CharacterPick.gd`). Because that file persists, always pass `--class` in captures.
- `--deck` is ignored with `--scenario` and in the arena, because only the run reads it. `--list-decks` needs the run too (a plain launch).
- Preset files (`data/debug_decks/<name>.json`): `name` (equals the file name), `description`, `class` (`rogue` or `wizard`; a preset names its class), `full` (card ids, copies allowed) and `starter` (a sub-multiset of `full`). Adding a file needs no code. Shipped: `fire-heavy-rogue`, `physical-rogue` (no Fire card at all since BD3a; the deck rules need none), `mixed-rogue`, `fire-heavy-wizard`, `arcane-heavy-wizard` (the fewest Fire cards the rules allow) and `mixed-wizard`, plus the BD1 archetype decks `rogue-kindling`, `rogue-hit-and-run`, `wizard-controlled-burn` and `wizard-departure-lounge` and the two BD3a archetype decks `rogue-bleed-out` and `rogue-grudge-brawler` (each a legal 15 or 16 card deck whose `starter` and `full` lists are the same); `--list-decks` prints the current set. The `mixed-*` decks' `full` list is every card of the class's pools (the whole pool: 38 Rogue, 28 Wizard); their `starter` is the normal 15-card starter. The `fire-heavy-*` presets hold only the Fire cards that existed before BD1. A preset is only the starting deck: rewards and removals change it during the run.
- `tools/VpCapture.tscn` has its own args: `--size=WxH` (required) and `--run` (host the run instead of the arena). Everything else is the capture args above. `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1` builds these for you.
- To jump straight to the final boss: `-- --run-seed=1 --run-room=30` (since MAP3; room 10 is now act 1's mini-boss), or capture it with `-Preset final_boss_act3`. The one-act boss: add `--acts=1` and use `--run-room=10`.
- `--scenario=...` always keeps the endless arena, even on a normal launch.

### The map (MAP1, MAP2, MAP3)
- **What it is:** the run is three acts (`data/map.json` `acts`, 3), each a branching map of 10 rooms, 30 rooms in all (`enabled` true; plan in `docs/MAP.md`). Each act's map is generated from the run seed and the act number. Gold, HP, deck, relics and rejects carry from act to act, and since BD3a acts 2 and 3 start with a heal of 30% of max HP (D45 = B; `data/run.json` `acts.start_heal`, 0 turns it off; the act screen says how much you recovered and shows low HP in red); every route has at least one rest. Room 10 of acts 1 and 2 is the **mini-boss**; room 10 of act 3 is the boss. A short **Act screen** (Enter or Continue) opens between acts. The HUD shows "Act 2 of 3" and "Room 14 of 30 - Type" on a multi-act run. It is generated from the run seed and checked against every path rule (fights, elites, at least 1 rest per route since D40, a relic route). You pick each next room on the map screen with the arrow keys or mouse and Enter; the pause screen has a view-only map. Default focus is a plain fight.
- **Node types:** combat (card offer + gold), elite (relic offer + more gold), relic stage (a treasure chest with no fight: small, medium or large, which sets the relic odds; rooms 3 to 8), rest (heal or remove a card), the merchant (MAP2, see below, rooms 4 to 9, at most 1 on any route, so some routes pass none) and the mini-boss (room 10 of acts 1 and 2; two encounters in `data/run.json` `rooms.mini_boss`; interim reward until MAP3b: gold, a relic offer, then a card offer) and the boss on room 10 of the last act. Curse and buff nodes exist in the schema with weight 0 and are not generated yet. Act scaling (enemy HP, damage, gold per act) is `data/run.json` `acts`; acts 2 and 3 have their own wave lists (`acts` field on each list). Weights, windows, path rules and chest odds are in `data/map.json`; gold amounts are `data/run.json` `gold`.
- **Gold** is earned from fights and spent at the merchant. It shows on the HUD and the map screen, with a "+N" when a fight pays. The map shows a "spend your gold at the merchant" line while a merchant can still be reached.
- **Print a map** (verified: exit 0): `powershell -File tools\run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 7 -Route` prints rooms, columns, edges, wave ids, chest sizes, node ids per non-combat type, path-rule scores and the first route. `-Sweep 1000` generates seeds 1 to 1000 and prints failures and timing (exit 1 on any failure). Other switches (MAP2): `-From N` starts the sweep at seed N, `-Routes` (with `-Sweep`) prints per-route medians for the relic estimate and node mix, `-Merchant` applies `data/merchant_on.json` first (a no-op now that the merchant ships on) and `-Set "types.rest.weight=20"` overrides one config value for the run (repeatable, applied after `-Merchant`). MAP3: `-Act N` prints act N's map (the mini-boss shows as `MB` on room 10 of acts 1 and 2; verified with `-Seed 7 -Act 2`, exit 0) and `-Acts M` sets how many acts the run has (default `data/map.json` `acts`); with `-Sweep` they sweep that act's rules (qa: `-Sweep 1000 -Act 1`, `-Act 2` and `-Act 3` each 0 failures).

### The merchant (MAP2)
- **On by default:** `data/map.json` `types.merchant.weight` is above 0. The four values that make it "on" (merchant weight, `path_rules.relic_route`, `estimate.merchant_prices`, `data/run.json` `relics.prices`) live in `data/merchant_on.json` as a patch and must flip together (`MerchantSwitch.shipped_problems`, checked by a test).
- **What it sells** (numbers: `data/run.json` `merchant` and `relics.prices`): 3 relics priced by tier (2 normal, plus a third slot that holds only shop-only relics when any is eligible), 3 cards at one price, and card removal at a base price plus a step for each removal bought at the merchant (rest-room removals do not raise it). Removal obeys the deck minimums. Boss-tier relics are never sold. Stock is seeded from the run seed and node id.
- **Buying:** any number of items in any order, then Leave. Gold never goes negative. At the relic cap, buying a relic asks which owned relic to give up first (Cancel charges nothing). You can save mid-visit and get the same stock back. Free rerolls of the unsold stock come only from the relic Request for Quotation.
- **Prices (D41 = B, MAP3):** the director kept the higher relic prices and asked for more gold income instead. `relics.prices` in `data/run.json` is per tier and set for three acts; gold income per room type is `data/run.json` `gold`. Card prices are one flat number until MAP3b makes them depend on rarity. The numbers and their history are in `docs/CHANGELOG.md` 2026-11-09.
- **Try it:** `powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Preset merchant_shop` (needs a renderer), or `godot --path . -- --run-seed=54 --acts=1 --run-node=33 --run-gold=200` (seed 54's act 1 has its merchant at node 33 only with `--acts=1` (verified: `run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 54 -Acts 1` lists it; the three-act act 1 map of seed 54 has none); re-check with `run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 54 -Acts 1` if the generator changes).
- **Straight run:** `-- --no-map` gives the pre-MAP1 run unchanged. Tests about the straight run pin it with `RunSession.map_override = 0`.

### Continue and the save file (MAP3)
- A map run saves to `user://run_save.json` (one slot, in Godot's per-project user data folder; its exact location on this machine was not checked). It is written when the map screen opens and when a room starts, never before the first room of act 1, and deleted when the run is won or lost. Straight (`--no-map`) runs do not save.
- On the class pick screen, **Continue your run** shows when a usable save exists (it names the class, act and room, and starts focused). Quitting mid-fight and continuing restarts that room. Starting a new run from the class cards replaces the save at that run's first room start.
- A bad, old or unreadable save is ignored with a warning and left on disk; it never crashes the launch.
- **Never saves, reads or deletes:** headless runs, `-s` scripts, and any launch with a debug flag (`--run-room`, `--run-node`, `--run-gold`, `--run-clear-after`, `--run-offer`, `--full-deck`, `--deck`, `--relics`, `--relic-offer`, `--relic-cap`, `--acts`, `--scenario`, `--sim-*`). So the headless tools, sims and the test suite cannot touch a player's save. A windowed capture with no debug flag is not suppressed, so it can write or delete the real save. `--no-map` is not on the debug list, but since BD3a a windowed straight run that is won or lost deletes the save only when it owns a map run (it used to delete a saved map run too; fixed in f2e54d1, covered by `bd3a_housekeeping_test`, not run by hand here). A save with `hp` at or under 0, `hp` above max, `rejects_remaining` out of range or a bad `removed_cards` entry is refused like any bad save. Not run by hand in this pass: the Continue button itself (qa tests it; `UxCapture` `--mode=continue_pick` captures it).

### Relics (BD2)
- 19 relics in `data/relics/`, one per file. BD2: Pyre Insurance, Smoldering Grudge, Lingering Embers, Second Wind (Unwillingly), Spite Knuckles (a Bleed relic since BD3a: every Nth light hit that lands sets the target Bleeding), Heavy Sigh. BD3a (non-Fire, physical Rogue): Serrated Excuse, Salt in the Wound, Per My Last Email, Chasing Paper, Muscle Memory, Overtime (Unpaid). MAP2 pool relics: ASAP Notice, Severance Package, Paperwork Fire, Grading Curve. MAP2 shop-only relics (`sources` exactly `["merchant"]`): Liquidate Assets, Request for Quotation, As Per the Attached Document. A relic is a run-long passive (stat modifiers and/or triggers on combat events); numbers and rules are in the files. Exit Strategy slipped to BD3b (it is not among the BD3a relics; `docs/ROADMAP.md` BD3).
- **Where they come from:** the elite room offers relics instead of cards (pick 1 of 3, or take the free Skip; skipping costs no reject), the merchant sells them (above), and the **relic room** (straight run only, since MAP1: once per run, never beside the rest room or the elite, on-screen rooms 3 to 9; on the map it is a relic stage on rooms 3 to 8, see below) offers the same with no fight. If no relic is eligible the room gives a card offer instead. Rest rooms and card rewards never offer relics.
- **Rules:** cap 20 (`relics.max_owned` in `data/run.json`). At the cap, taking a relic first asks which owned relic to give up; Cancel is focused by default and keeps everything; boss-tier relics can't be given up. No duplicates, and a relic you gave up is never offered again that run. Tier odds per act are `relics.tier_weights` (the straight run uses act 1). The block also holds `enabled`: set it false and the run is exactly the pre-BD2 run (no relic room, a card offer after the elite).
- **On screen:** a chip row under the dodge pips (a trigger lights the chip and shows its name; rapid repeats stay lit with an "xN" count), relic offer cards with a free Skip tile, the give-one-up screen, and the owned and given-up relics on the pause screen.
- Counters (Spite Knuckles' every-Nth-hit count) and internal cooldowns restart each room.

### Rewards and deck-clean (Milestone 3b)
- After each won **combat** room (never the last; with `relics.enabled` false also the elite room, as before BD2): 3 card offers (`rewards.offer_size` in `data/run.json`). Pick one, or reject all (3 rejects per run, `rejects_per_run`). Offers are seeded, drawn only from the character's pools, and cards you do not own are weighted 4:1 over owned ones. A card may carry an optional `requires` filter. **The status lean (BD3a, D43 = B):** an unowned card that applies Bleed or Burn weighs more for each card you have added that applies the same status, up to a cap (`rewards.status_lean`, `status_lean_max` in `data/run.json`; D46 = B: `status_lean_skip_starter` true means the starting deck does not count). **Removed cards stay gone (BD3a, D47 = B):** a card you remove is never offered again that run, in card offers or the merchant's stock (`RunState.removed_cards`, saved). `tools\run_offer_report.ps1` measures both (see "Offer report").
- **Rest room:** Heal 30% of max HP, or remove a card. A card whose removal would break a deck minimum is greyed out with the reason; if none can go, the remove tile is disabled.
- **Starter deck:** 15 cards, 3 per slot, per class (`starting_deck` in `data/characters.json`; it used to be in `data/run.json`). It was the full 24-card Rogue pool before 3b. The deck minimum is 3 unique per slot, so with the plain starter the removal menu is disabled until picks add cards.
- The choice screen shows card tiles, a reject tile with pips, and a "Next: Room N of 10 - Type" line (on a three-act run: "Room N of 30"). Arrow keys move the highlight, Enter or a click confirms. Options ignore input for 0.4 s after opening (`prompt_option_arm_delay` in `data/ui.json`), and Space is swallowed (the Continue and New run buttons have the same delay, and Space no longer confirms them; the Game Over screen has the same 0.4 s delay and swallows Space, and its hint reads "Enter"). The offer panel shrinks to fit the window and never scrolls (tested from 1024x600 up); only the removal grid scrolls. Card tiles show "In slot: A / B / C" (the `slot_now` template in `data/run.json`; at most `slot_now_max` names, then "+N more"; PL3 removed the "<Slot> (<key>) now:" wording and the number-key hints). A reload mid-reward resumes the same offer.

Controls (from `data/input_bindings.json`): WASD move, mouse aim, left click light attack, right click heavy attack, Q, E, F, Ctrl, R use card slots 1 to 5 in order (Movement is Ctrl, Utility is F, the fifth is Recovery), **Space is the dodge**, and Shift plus the same key swaps a dead card (Movement swap is Shift+Ctrl). The slot keys still cast while Ctrl is held (Ctrl+Q casts Q's slot); the swap works whichever of Shift and Ctrl goes down first (`Player._swap_by_late_modifier`). The dodge is on (`dodge.enabled` is true in `data/player.json`; set it false to switch it off). The dodge does not count as a Movement cast for card conditions such as "if you used Movement in the last 2 s" (D39 = A); `dodge.counts_as_movement_cast` in `data/player.json` (false) is the one switch to change that. Windows caveats, not fixed: Ctrl+Shift is the layout-switch hotkey on multi-layout machines, and Ctrl+Space toggles the IME on some CJK setups. The dodge is a pool of charges that come back one at a time (see Player below). Every key shown in the UI (HUD slot hints, attack meters, dodge label, reward and removal screens, pause hint) is read live from `data/input_bindings.json` through `src/core/InputLabels.gd`, so a rebind there changes the text. No UI text may hardcode a key, and two actions with the same key and modifiers log an error at startup (`InputBindings.last_conflicts`). History: the keys were 1-5 until 24530e4 (Q, E, 1, 2, 3), slot 5 moved from 3 to R in K1, and in H2 Movement moved 2 to Space and Utility 1 to F (the dodge lost Space); in H3 the dodge came back on Space and Movement moved to Ctrl. Also: Esc pause/settings, F3 debug overlay. Movement cards (dashes, blinks, leaps) pass through enemies while they move; the dodge does not (a dodge in the post-dash ghost window may). The ghost look on the player appears only after `ghost_show_delay` in `data/vfx.json`. Holding a slot key shows the card's hit area and releasing casts it (change with the `cast_trigger` setting in the pause screen).

**Card cues on the HUD (BD1).** Each slot shows up to 3 tag chips under the card name: keywords (Keystone, Primer, Payoff) as filled chips, derived tags (Projectile, Area, Burn, ...) as outlined ones; when they don't fit, derived tags drop first (about 2 chips at 1024x600). A slot whose card has a condition that is true right now glows yellow (rings, corner brackets and a LIVE pill; it shows at reduced strength while the slot cools, and is eased so it doesn't flicker). A slot with a primed buff shows a cyan bolt ("xN" for several charges) and a drain bar. Reward offers and the removal grid show the same chips, a condition line (after the cooldown) on the card, and a keyword's rule text as a tooltip. The card slot is 142 px high (it was 134 before the chip row). Look values: `data/ui.json` `hud`; the data comes from `src/cards/SlotSynergyView.gd`.

`godot` must be on PATH, or set `GODOT_BIN` to the full path of the Godot executable, or call it directly: `"C:\path\to\Godot.exe" --path .`. All `tools\*.ps1` scripts resolve the binary from `GODOT_BIN` first, then `godot_console`/`godot` on PATH, and prefer a `*_console.exe` sibling.

## Running tests
Tests use [gdUnit4](https://github.com/godot-gdunit-labs/gdUnit4), vendored in `addons/gdUnit4`. Run the full suite headless:

```
powershell -File tools\run_tests.ps1
```

This runs every suite under `/tests` and exits non-zero on any failure. Last run: see "Last verification" below (2083 test cases, 157 suites, 0 skipped, 0 orphans; about 15 min). Reports go to `reports/` (gitignored). The script uses the console-subsystem `*_console.exe` build when one sits next to the resolved binary, because PowerShell cannot reliably wait on or read the windowed build, which made failures look like passes (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`).

> After adding a new global class (`class_name`) the plain headless run may fail with "Could not find type X in current scope". Run `godot --headless --editor --quit --path .` once to rebuild the class cache (verified: exits 0), then re-run the tests.

Tests and tools that press a card or attack key must send an `InputEventAction` (see `tests/support/test_input.gd`), because `Input.action_press` emits no event and the player reads input events.

Other helpers in `tests/support/`: `body_flag.gd` forces `body.collide_with_enemies` on or off for a test (tests that pin non-solid geometry turn it off), `test_caster.gd` is a test double for a damage dealer (so a test controls every hit), `test_clock.gd` (`TestClock.wait_real`) waits real seconds without the ignore-time-scale timer bug. The test log prints `ERROR:` lines from bad-data fixtures on purpose, so do not use "grep ERROR" as a pass/fail gate; the suite's exit code is the gate.

## Game data worth knowing
All content and tuning is JSON under `data/` (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 6 for the full table).
- **Deck rules** (`data/deck_rules.json`, read by `src/cards/DeckRules.gd`): `min_unique_per_slot` (3 today), optional per-slot overrides under `slots` (for example `{"utility": {"min_unique": 5}}`) and `slot_groups` (a named set of slots with `min_unique_total`). Only different cards count; copies never do. At run start a deck that misses the minimums is blocked: an error is logged, the spawner stays off and `Main.run_start_blocked` is emitted. The F3 debug overlay shows each slot's unique count against its minimum and marks shortfalls `SHORT`. The shipped deck passes with the default rules.
- **Characters** (`data/characters.json`, read by `src/core/CharacterDatabase.gd`): per class id, `display_name`, `description`, `pools`, `max_hp`, `body_color`, `attacks` and the 15-card `starting_deck`, plus two optional per-class tuning keys: `dodge_charges` (the class's dodge charge count, inside the `data/player.json` min and max) and `slot_base_cooldowns` (slot type -> seconds, overriding `data/slot_types.json` `base_cooldown` for that class; today only the Wizard's Recovery). The Wizard has 22 HP against the Rogue's 30 (W1b tuned it to 22, then put it back to 26; the director decided D20 = B on 2026-11-02 and H2 set it to 22, see `docs/RESOLVED.md`); its pool `wizard` holds 10 cards (8 before BD1, plus Parting Gift and Well, Actually) and its starter is built around them (Ward, Technically is in the pool but not the starter); the Wizard's light attack is an auto-firing bolt (`light_variants.wizard` in `data/attacks.json`).
- **Player** (`data/player.json`): the `dodge` block (`enabled` = the on/off switch (H2 trial, shipped true since H3; optional, a missing value reads as the built-in default, true); speed, duration, `cooldown` = the recharge time of one charge, `charges` = the default count, `min_charges` and `max_charges` = hard limits applied after stat modifiers, `block_ticks_after_pause`) and `body.collide_with_enemies` (true: player and enemies are solid to each other; false restores the old walk-through behaviour).
- **Card frame colours** (`data/pools.json`, `frame_color` on every pool, validated on load): a card's frame is the colour of its pool (fire, class pools, neutral). Read through `PoolDatabase.frame_color_for_card(card_id)` / `frame_color_for_pool(pool_id)`. Drawn on the HUD slots (active card and next-up swatch), reward offers and removal cells. Sizes and slot plate colours are `data/ui.json` `hud` (`slot_frame_*`, `slot_plate_*`) and `run_ui` (`option_frame_width`).
- **Melee reach cue** (`src/ui/MeleeReachCue.gd`, `data/ui.json` `melee_cue`): a faint arc at the light attack's reach, shown only when nothing is in reach, brighter on a whiffed click. It appears only for a light attack of shape `arc` (the Rogue; the Wizard's bolt never shows it).
- **Keywords** (`data/keywords.json`, read by `src/cards/KeywordDatabase.gd`; BD1): the authored card keywords Primer, Payoff and Keystone, each with a `display_name` and a `rule_text`. A card lists them in its optional `keywords` array; derived tags (Projectile, Area, Burn, ...) come from its effects (`src/cards/CardTags.gd`). A card effect is `{template, params, condition?}`; condition types are in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 4.
- **Relics** (`data/relics/<id>.json`, read by `src/relics/RelicDatabase.gd`, validated by `RelicSchema`; BD2): per relic `id` (= file name), `name`, `pool`, `rarity` (`tier1`, `tier2`, `tier3`, `boss`), `sources` (which rooms may offer it), `keywords`, `modifiers`, `triggers` (event, filter, effects), a one-line `rule_text` and `flavor_text`. Adding one needs no code unless it uses a new stat, event or effect template. The `relics` block in `data/run.json` holds `enabled` (the master switch), `max_owned` (the cap), `offer_count`, `relic_room {count, first, last}` (0-based room indexes), `tier_weights` per act and (MAP2) `prices` by tier; the merchant's own block is `merchant` in the same file (`MerchantConfig`). Relic modifiers may also scale with a run stat (`per_run_stat` plus `per`) and `triggers` on `hit_landed` accept `tag` and (BD3a) `slot_type` filters. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 4 and "A relic" in section 8.
- **Zones** (`data/zones.json`): the `max_live` cap on ground zones (for example the Fire Wall) and their look.

## Screenshots and scripted scenarios
Capture is built into the game and runs only when user args follow `--` (nothing runs in normal play). It needs a renderer, so it must run windowed; `--headless` cannot capture. The game quits itself after the last shot. Output goes under `screenshots\` by default, which is gitignored.

```
powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1                                  # hp_bars scenario, 1 shot at 3.0 s -> screenshots\shot.png
powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario attacks -After "0.75,2.4,2.7" -Out screenshots\atk.png   # several shots -> atk_0.png, atk_1.png, ...
godot --path . -- --screenshot-after=3.0 --screenshot-out=screenshots/a.png --scenario=hp_bars
godot --path . -- --screenshot-count=3 --screenshot-interval=2.0 --screenshot-out=screenshots/c.png   # shots at 2 s, 4 s, 6 s
godot --path . -- --scenario=hp_bars                                          # play a scenario by hand, no capture
```

`capture_screenshot.ps1` parameters: `-After` (seconds, comma list, default "3.0"), `-Out` (default `screenshots\shot.png`), `-Scenario` (default `hp_bars`; `-Scenario ""` captures the normal run), `-Resolution` (default `1280x720`), `-Extra` (more game args, comma-split, for example `-Extra "--run-seed=7,--run-room=10"`), `-Preset <name>` (BD1: a named capture from `tools/capture_presets.json`, table below; any parameter you give explicitly overrides the preset; the default output is `screenshots\<name>.png`), `-Class rogue|wizard` (`--class=`, now also for scenarios), `-Actions "0.5:dodge,0.9:dodge"` (`--press=`) and `-AtBossAttack <id>` (`--at-boss-attack=`). ### True-size capture (`-TrueSize`)

powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario c1_overlay -After "1.5" -TrueSize 2560x1440 -Out screenshots/t.png   # verified: saved one PNG, exit 0

## Headless bot-sim
A small crash/NaN check on the card and deck system (not a balance tool). It drives N "rooms" with random card use and swaps against an empty room and reports NaN in cooldowns or health.

```
powershell -File tools\run_bot_sim.ps1 -Rooms 20 -Seed 3      # verified: prints "Bot-sim OK" and a checksum, exit 0

## Wave density sim
Plays the real Main scene (spawner, enemies, player) with a bot that uses the real input actions, then reports crashes, NaN, cap violations, enemies or player leaving the arena, stalls, damage taken, alive counts, spacing, and rough CPU frame cost. It runs as a scene (so autoloads exist), headless. `-Seconds` is game time, `-Scale` is `Engine.time_scale` (1 = real time and most faithful, default 3), `-God` keeps the player alive to exercise the whole wave table, `-Idle` is a player who never moves, `-Deck` (`standard` default, or the name of a preset in `data/debug_decks/`; passed to the game as `--sim-deck=`) starts every run with that legal preset deck, so armor comparisons are like for like. A preset is only the starting deck: rewards and removals change it as the run goes on. `-Class rogue|wizard` plays whole runs (`-Run`) as that class (passed as `--sim-class=`; without `-Run` the arena is always the Rogue and the script prints a note; a `-Deck` preset of the other class, or an unknown class, exits 2). `-LightHeavyOnly` is a bot policy, not a deck: the bot never presses any card slot (since H1; it used to skip only slots 1 and 2), so only light attacks, heavy attacks and dodging are used. Three flags are for `-Run` balance work (W1b): `-WizardNoKite` makes a ranged class (the Wizard) walk in like the Rogue bot instead of holding a stand-off, `-DodgePellets` makes the bot also step out of and roll through enemy projectiles in flight (off by default, because it changes results and the older baselines assume it off), and `-BossLog` prints, per boss attack, how often it was thrown and hit and the HP it cost in the boss room (a line per run and a summary). `-BossLog` and `-DodgePellets` need `-Run`. `-StartRoom N` (PL3, needs `-Run`; passed as `--start-room=`) starts every run at room N of the first act (`rooms.size()` is one act's rooms, so 10 is act 1's last room: the mini-boss on a three-act sim run, the boss with `-Acts 1`; from reading `tools/wave_sim.gd`, unverified by a run) with the class's starter deck and full HP; a room past the end prints a plain "note:" (BD1-B). `-Plan` (BD1; passed as `--plan`) lets the plan-aware policy `PlanPolicy` play the card slots (it casts a payoff when its condition is live, swaps a card that can't pay off soon, spends Movement to set up a combo), in the arena and with `-Run`; without it the card presses are the old seeded random ones. BD2 options (all but `-NoRelics` need `-Run`): `-Relics "a+b"` (passed as `--sim-relics=`) starts every run holding those relics, `-RelicCap N` (`--sim-relic-cap=`) lowers the relic cap so a short run reaches the give-one-up step, `-NoRelics` (`--sim-no-relics`) sets `relics.enabled` false for that process (the pre-BD2 run: no relic room, a card offer after the elite) and `-Swap "old=new"` (`--sim-swap=`) replaces one copy of a card in the starting deck (checked against the deck rules; combine with `-Deck`). `-Run` also prints the damage the player's side dealt by source (light, heavy, each slot type, each status, each relic), the fingerprint spread, relic pick/skip/swap counts, a `BOSS TIMES` line (timed-out boss fights are listed apart) and a soft-lock detail line. `-Acts N` (MAP3, `--sim-acts=`; with `-Run` and the map on: the runs have N acts, default `data/map.json` `acts` = 3; `-Acts 1` is the pre-MAP3 one-act run and its numbers; with more acts the report adds an `ACT` line per act (clear rate, time, HP at the end, relics, gold) and an `ACTS TOTAL` line) selects the act count. `-Run` output streams as it arrives (BD3a; it used to be buffered, which made a 12-run three-act sim of 12 to 15 minutes look like a hang). `-Runs` repeats with consecutive seeds (verified: `-Seed 20 -Runs 2` ran seeds 20 and 21), `-Seed` sets the first seed. `-Run` plays whole seeded runs through `RunMain` instead (see below). The script launches Godot with `--fixed-fps 60` (one 60 Hz physics tick per frame) and turns hit-stop off in the sim; `-Scale` defaults to 1, and a `-Scale` above 1 drops `--fixed-fps` and the run no longer repeats.

```
powershell -File tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 1 -Seconds 150 -Runs 3
powershell -File tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 1 -Seconds 120 -God
powershell -File tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 20 -Seconds 60 -Runs 3 -Idle -Scale 1
powershell -File tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 1 -Seconds 20 -God -Deck fire-heavy-rogue    # verified: WAVE SIM OK, exit 0
powershell -File tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 1 -Seconds 20 -God -Deck physical-rogue -LightHeavyOnly    # verified: WAVE SIM OK, exit 0
```

```
`-Run`: `-Seed` is the first run seed, `-Runs` the number of runs, `-RoomTimeout` (sim seconds, default 240, minimum 10) is how long a room may last before it counts as a soft lock. The bot also plays the choices with a seeded policy (rejects 25% of the time while any are left, else a random unowned card; it removes a card in rest rooms when allowed). It reports per room time and lowest HP, and for the boss room a `boss fight (spawn to death)` line (seconds, whether the boss died, the player's lowest HP; the bot steps out of wind-up areas and dodges whenever a charge is ready, throttled to the roll length), and fails on a soft lock, an empty deck or a stuck cooldown. Per run it prints `HP at each room end`, the final deck size and per-slot composition, and picks, rejects used, removals and rest heals; the summary adds `mean HP at room end, as a share of max` per room and `final deck sizes`. Card pick-rate outliers and never-cast cards are printed as a NOTE, not a failure (pick rates count all database cards, so cards outside the 15-card start show as never cast unless picked). A small `-RoomTimeout` (60 was tried) flags the boss room as a soft lock; that is the check working, not a bug.

All forms run and end with `WAVE SIM OK` (verified with shorter `-Seconds`; the 150 s and 120 s forms use the same code path). The wave table and the simultaneous-enemy cap live in `data/waves.json` (validated by `src/enemies/WaveConfig.gd`; enemy ids and scenes in `data/enemies.json`). Headless frame times exclude rendering.

**Seeds repeat exactly** on the same machine and build. The same `-Seed` (at `-Scale` 1) gives the same `result:` line: HP lost, kills, casts and a `state_hash` of final positions (BD3a: `-Seed 1 -Seconds 30` gives `state_hash` 3172486861 and the 90 s run 916264160; `-Plan -Seed 1 -Seconds 30` gives 1317880580 (bare `-Plan` defaults to 90 s and is not pinned). In BD1 they moved because the 8 new cards fill the arena slots (were 2333565009 and 1212800). The 90 s baseline moved in PL3 (was 819020947). Baselines moved in H3 (speed-up, dodge on; were 2816045921 and 3667433295), in H1 (enemy `pressure.speed_scale` 1.0 -> 1.25, were 2484586390 and 3255493562) and earlier in P-dodge, see `docs/CHANGELOG.md`). A windowed run, a `-Scale` above 1, or another machine or Godot version can differ. Besides the `result:` line the sim prints a `pinned:` line (frames where the bot was commanded to move but the player barely moved, and the longest stretch) and a `climb:` line (the player's maximum feet height), which watch for solid enemies pinning or lifting the player.
