# Chosen, Reluctantly

A 3D isometric action RPG where your skills are cards: real-time combat driven by typed card slots, each with its own shuffled mini-deck. Built around deep class/element build combinations and roguelite runs with persistent between-run progression. Currently a placeholder-visuals prototype: two playable classes (a Fire Rogue and a Fire Wizard, picked at the start of each run), a seeded run of 30 rooms in three acts (MAP3: acts 1 and 2 end in a mini-boss, act 3 in the boss; the run saves at room boundaries and has a Continue button), a branching map you route through (combat, elite, relic chest, rest, merchant, mini-boss, boss; MAP1, MAP2, MAP3) with card rewards and gold after fights, a merchant that sells relics, cards and card removal for that gold (MAP2), a deck-clean choice in the rest room, and a three-phase final boss, The Overly Enthusiastic Guardian (placeholder name). Cards carry tags and keywords, and some card effects have conditions ("if the target is Burning", "if you used Movement in the last 2 s") so cards can set each other up (BD1). Runs also hand out relics (BD2): run-long passives with triggers, offered after the elite room and in a relic room, shown as a chip row on the HUD. BD3a adds a physical (Bleed) Rogue build: Bleed that can stack, a detonate effect that cashes it in, 8 new Rogue cards and 6 relics, and card offers that lean toward the statuses your deck applies. Acts 2 and 3 start with a heal. MAP4 adds events: a "?" node on the three-act map that offers a choice with a cost and a gain, plus curse cards, sacrifice relics (a relic you pay for by giving up others) and a rest-room Dig. Slice 3d adds **loadouts**: the game counts finished runs (won or lost); run 1 starts straight away with the class's preset deck, and from run 2 a **start screen** lets you start with the preset or a saved loadout (a deck, a plan of reward cards to look out for, and a starter relic; 5 slots per class) or edit one in a **loadout editor**. Slice BD3b adds Wizard depth: 7 non-Fire Wizard cards (a Charge status that stacks and is spent by Thesis Defense, and Spiteful Ward, a buff that strikes back), 5 relics and the HUD readouts for them. See `docs/GDD.md` for the design and `docs/ROADMAP.md` for status.

## Running the project
Requires Godot 4.7.x (developed on 4.7.2 stable). Open `project.godot` in the editor and press Play, or use the command line:

```
godot --path . --quit   # smoke-check with no window (verified: exits 0)
godot --path .          # play it: starts a run
```

A normal launch shows the **class pick** (Rogue or Wizard; 1/2 or the arrows highlight, Enter or a click confirms; the HUD is hidden behind it; when a saved run exists a **Continue your run** entry also shows, see "Continue and the save file" below), then (from run 2, 3d) a **start screen** (preset deck, saved loadouts or the editor; see "Loadouts and the start screen"), then starts a **run**. Since MAP3 the run is **three acts of 10 rooms (30 rooms)**, each act its own **branching map** (see "The map" below), generated from the seed; you pick each next room on the map screen, and a short Act screen opens between acts. The paragraph below describes the straight run (`--no-map`) and the screens common to both. `--no-map` gives the older straight run (10 rooms in a row: combat, 1 elite, 1 rest, boss last, and from BD2 one of the combat rooms is a **relic room** with no fight, on on-screen rooms 3 to 9), which the rest of this paragraph describes; see `data/run.json` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 2. The room indicator at the top right shows "Room X of Y - Type" with a shape per type (circle combat, diamond elite, plus rest, star boss; the relic room's plate reads "Relics"). After a won combat room you pick a reward card (see below); after the elite room you pick a relic instead (see Relics below); a rest room offers heal or remove a card; any other room shows a "Room cleared" panel with Continue. The last room is the boss (`data/bosses.json`): its HP bar shows at the top centre with its name, phase notches and the attack it is winding up. Killing it ends with a short death beat, then the stats screen; Game Over shows "Reached room X of Y". Try again and New run start a new seed. `scenes/main/Main.tscn` is the endless arena, still used by tests, scenarios, the wave sim and VpCapture without `--run`.

### Launch options
Game args go after `--`, for example `godot --path . -- --run-seed=7 --run-room=10`. Options marked "run" are read only by a normal run launch (RunMain) and are ignored with `--scenario` or in the endless arena. "First run" means Try again and New run do not repeat it; "whole launch" means they do. Parsed in `src/run/RunController.gd` (`_apply_launch_args`), `src/run/DebugDecks.gd` (also `--class`), `src/ui/ClassPickScreen.gd`, `src/ui/RunPromptScreen.gd` and `src/debug/CaptureArgs.gd`, and (3d) `src/loadouts/LoadoutArgs.gd`.

Notes:
- `--deck` errors exit 2 with "Launch refused: ..." on stderr: unknown name, `--deck` given twice, a bare `--deck`, or a preset that is invalid (each verified except the invalid-preset case, which is covered by tests). There is no rule bypass: both lists of a preset must pass the deck rules.
- `--class` also exits 2 with "Launch refused: ..." for a bare `--class`, `--class` twice, an unknown class, or a class that disagrees with the `--deck` preset (each verified; only the "Launch refused: ..." line is printed, with no stack trace). A preset names its class (`"class"` in the file; `--deck` alone sets the class). `--class` is ignored in the arena (the arena is always the Rogue). With `--scenario` it plays the scenario as that class (a scenario can also set its own `character`, which sets the body look). A bare, repeated or unknown `--class` with a scenario, or one that disagrees with the scenario's own `character`, exits 2 with "Launch refused: ..." (BD1-B; a plain `--scenario` with no `--class` is unaffected).
- `--relics`, `--relic-offer` and `--relic-cap` exit 2 with "Launch refused: ..." for an unknown relic id, an id listed twice, an offer longer than `relics.offer_count`, or a cap that is not a whole number from 1 (each verified). They are read by the run (`RunController._apply_relic_args`, `src/run/RelicArgs.gd`) and are ignored in the arena and with `--scenario`; a scenario that needs relics lists them under its own `relics` key.
- The class pick screen opens on every run, including Try again and New run, and pauses the room behind it. It is skipped when `--class` or `--deck` is given, in headless runs, and in the arena. The last pick is preselected and, in windowed play only, remembered in `user://last_class.json` (`src/run/CharacterPick.gd`). Because that file persists, always pass `--class` in captures.
- `--deck` is ignored with `--scenario` and in the arena, because only the run reads it. `--list-decks` needs the run too (a plain launch).
- Preset files (`data/debug_decks/<name>.json`): `name` (equals the file name), `description`, `class` (`rogue` or `wizard`; a preset names its class), `full` (card ids, copies allowed) and `starter` (a sub-multiset of `full`). Adding a file needs no code. Shipped: `fire-heavy-rogue`, `physical-rogue` (no Fire card at all since BD3a; the deck rules need none), `mixed-rogue`, `fire-heavy-wizard`, `arcane-heavy-wizard` (the fewest Fire cards the rules allow) and `mixed-wizard`, plus the BD1 archetype decks `rogue-kindling`, `rogue-hit-and-run`, `wizard-controlled-burn` and `wizard-departure-lounge`, the two BD3a archetype decks `rogue-bleed-out` and `rogue-grudge-brawler` and the two BD3b archetype decks `wizard-thesis` (W-B) and `wizard-ward-battlemage` (W-D; informational, it fails its own balance checks, D54 = A) (each a legal 15 or 16 card deck whose `starter` and `full` lists are the same); `--list-decks` prints the current set (14 presets; verified, exit 0). The `mixed-*` decks' `full` list is every card of the class's pools (the whole pool of non-curse cards: 49 Rogue; 46 Wizard since BD3b, 39 at MAP3b); their `starter` is the normal 15-card starter. The `fire-heavy-*` presets hold only the Fire cards that existed before BD1. A preset is only the starting deck: rewards and removals change it during the run.
- `tools/VpCapture.tscn` has its own args: `--size=WxH` (required) and `--run` (host the run instead of the arena). Everything else is the capture args above. `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1` builds these for you.
- To jump straight to the final boss: `-- --run-seed=1 --run-room=30` (since MAP3; room 10 is now act 1's mini-boss), or capture it with `-Preset final_boss_act3`. The one-act boss: add `--acts=1` and use `--run-room=10`.
- `--scenario=...` always keeps the endless arena, even on a normal launch.

### The map (MAP1, MAP2, MAP3)
- **What it is:** the run is three acts (`data/map.json` `acts`, 3), each a branching map of 10 rooms, 30 rooms in all (`enabled` true; plan in `docs/MAP.md`). Each act's map is generated from the run seed and the act number. Gold, HP, deck, relics and rejects carry from act to act, and since BD3a acts 2 and 3 start with a heal of 30% of max HP (D45 = B; `data/run.json` `acts.start_heal`, 0 turns it off; the act screen shows "+N HP (act heal 30%)" (N is the heal applied, clamped to max; the percent is read from data; hidden at full HP) and shows low HP in red); every route has at least one rest. Room 10 of acts 1 and 2 is the **mini-boss**; room 10 of act 3 is the boss. A short **Act screen** (Enter or Continue) opens between acts. The HUD shows "Act 2 of 3" and "Room 14 of 30 - Type" on a multi-act run. It is generated from the run seed and checked against every path rule (fights, elites, at least 1 rest per route since D40, a relic route). You pick each next room on the map screen with the arrow keys or mouse and Enter; the pause screen has a view-only map. Default focus is a plain fight. **Single-option beat (UX1b):** when exactly one node is reachable, the map stays up for 1 s (`data/ui.json` `map_screen.auto_advance_seconds`) with that node highlighted and a line saying so, then takes it; any key or click skips the wait, and pausing holds it. It runs only for a human session (`MapScreen.human_session()`); bots, SIM and watch mode set `MapScreen.automated_session` in `tools/wave_sim.gd` and never see it. Probe: `godot --path . res://tools/ProbeHumanSession.tscn` (windowed) prints `PROBE ... human_session=true` and then `false` after the opt-out (qa's repro; not run in this pass).
- **Node types:** combat (card offer + gold), elite (relic offer + more gold), relic stage (a treasure chest with no fight: small, medium or large, which sets the relic odds; rooms 3 to 8), rest (heal or remove a card), the merchant (MAP2, see below, rooms 4 to 9, at most 1 on any route, so some routes pass none) and the mini-boss (room 10 of acts 1 and 2; since MB1 a real boss fight: Head of Facilities in act 1 and The Corporate Raider in act 2, defined in `data/bosses.json` with `mini_boss: true` and placed by `data/run.json` `rooms.mini_boss`; its death ends the act; reward since MAP3b: gold, a boss relic pick (1 of 3 boss relics, no Skip; at the relic cap, Cancel on the give-one-up step returns to the same offer), then a card offer of fixed rarity, rare after act 1 and legendary after act 2, `data/run.json` `rewards.mini_boss_rarity`. A quit inside this reward resumes at the mini-boss room's start and replays the fight; a boss relic already taken is not kept, and the replay gives the same offers) and the boss on room 10 of the last act. The **event** node (MAP4, a speech bubble with a "?", rooms 3 to 9, `no_repeat`) replaced the old unused curse and buff node types; it is generated with weight 28 on the three-act run only (`data/map.json` `multi_act.types`, which also raises the relic stage to 18) and has weight 0 in the one-act and straight runs (see "Events and curses (MAP4)" below). Act scaling (enemy HP, damage, gold per act) is `data/run.json` `acts`; acts 2 and 3 have their own wave lists (`acts` field on each list). Weights, windows, path rules and chest odds are in `data/map.json`; gold amounts are `data/run.json` `gold`.
- **Gold** is earned from fights and spent at the merchant. It shows on the HUD and the map screen, with a "+N" when a fight pays. The map shows a "spend your gold at the merchant" line while a merchant can still be reached.
- **Print a map** (verified: exit 0): `powershell -File tools\run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 7 -Route` prints rooms, columns, edges, wave ids, chest sizes, node ids per non-combat type, path-rule scores and the first route. `-Sweep 1000` generates seeds 1 to 1000 and prints failures and timing (exit 1 on any failure). Other switches (MAP2): `-From N` starts the sweep at seed N, `-Routes` (with `-Sweep`) prints per-route medians for the relic estimate and node mix (MAP4 added an events column; verified with `-Sweep 40 -Routes`, exit 0), `-Merchant` applies `data/merchant_on.json` first (a no-op now that the merchant ships on) and `-Set "types.rest.weight=20"` overrides one config value for the run (repeatable, applied after `-Merchant`). MAP3: `-Act N` prints act N's map (the mini-boss shows as `MB` on room 10 of acts 1 and 2; verified with `-Seed 7 -Act 2`, exit 0) and `-Acts M` sets how many acts the run has (default `data/map.json` `acts`); with `-Sweep` they sweep that act's rules (qa: `-Sweep 1000 -Act 1`, `-Act 2` and `-Act 3` each 0 failures).

### The merchant (MAP2)
- **On by default:** `data/map.json` `types.merchant.weight` is above 0. The four values that make it "on" (merchant weight, `path_rules.relic_route`, `estimate.merchant_prices`, `data/run.json` `relics.prices`) live in `data/merchant_on.json` as a patch and must flip together (`MerchantSwitch.shipped_problems`, checked by a test).
- **What it sells** (numbers: `data/run.json` `merchant` and `relics.prices`): 3 relics priced by tier (2 normal, plus a third slot that holds only shop-only relics when any is eligible), 3 cards at one price, and card removal at a base price plus a step for each removal bought at the merchant (rest-room removals do not raise it). Removal obeys the deck minimums. Boss-tier relics are never sold. Stock is seeded from the run seed and node id.
- **Buying:** any number of items in any order, then Leave. Gold never goes negative. At the relic cap, buying a relic asks which owned relic to give up first (Cancel charges nothing). You can save mid-visit and get the same stock back. Free rerolls of the unsold stock come only from the relic Request for Quotation.
- **Prices (D41 = B, MAP3):** the director kept the higher relic prices and asked for more gold income instead. `relics.prices` in `data/run.json` is per tier and set for three acts; gold income per room type is `data/run.json` `gold`. Card prices depend on rarity since MAP3b: `data/run.json` `merchant.card_prices` {common, rare, legendary} (the old flat `card_price` key is refused); each must stay below the relic tier of the same rank, and both run lengths use the same table. Large relic-stage chests start at tier 2 (`min_tier`).
- **Card rarity (MAP3b):** each card has `rarity` (`common` default, `rare`, `legendary`). Every card-offer slot rolls a rarity from `data/run.json` `rewards.rarity_weights` for the current act (act 1 common only; act 2 adds rare; act 3 adds legendary), falling down a rarity if that rarity has no eligible card left. The merchant stock follows the same rolls. Starting decks and pool-filled decks hold only common cards; `--full-deck` still gives every card. 17 rare and legendary cards (9 rare, 8 legendary) and 8 boss relics were added; see `data/cards/` and `data/relics/`. The numbers and their history are in `docs/CHANGELOG.md` 2026-11-09.
- **Try it:** `powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Preset merchant_shop` (needs a renderer), or `godot --path . -- --run-seed=54 --acts=1 --run-node=33 --run-gold=200` (seed 54's act 1 has its merchant at node 33 only with `--acts=1` (verified: `run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 54 -Acts 1` lists it; the three-act act 1 map of seed 54 has none); re-check with `run_map_print.ps1 -Seed 54 -Acts 1` if the generator changes).
- **Straight run:** `-- --no-map` gives the pre-MAP1 run unchanged. Tests about the straight run pin it with `RunSession.map_override = 0`.

### Events and curses (MAP4)
- **Events** (`data/events/<id>.json`, one per file, read by `src/events/EventDatabase.gd`, validated by `src/events/EventSchema.gd`): `id` (= file name), `name`, `flavor`, `acts` (which acts it can appear in), optional `weight` (default 1; the Loyalty Pledge is 0.5) and 2 or 3 `choices`, each `{label, requires?, outcomes[]}`. `requires` has exactly one key: `gold_at_least`, `has_curse` or `owns_relic`. Outcomes (`EventSchema.OUTCOMES`): `lose_hp`, `lose_max_hp`, `heal`, `gain_gold`, `lose_gold`, `gain_relic`, `gain_curse`, `remove_curse`, `remove_card`, `sacrifice_relic`, `start_fight` (an elite), `chance` (`p`, `win`, `lose`), `stat_mod` and `merchant_discount`; params are validated in the schema. HP amounts are fractions of max HP. The screen's cost and gain lines are generated from the outcomes. A choice that cannot be paid is greyed with the reason (`data/run.json` `ui.event.reasons`).
- **Shipped events (8):** Broken Vending Machine, Complacency Trap, Mandatory Exit Interview, Internal Audit, Loyalty Pledge, Performance Evaluation Altar, Misplaced Supply Closet, Sympathetic Coworker. Values are in the files, not here.
- **When they appear:** the `event` node is on the three-act map only (weight in `data/map.json` `multi_act.types`); the run draws one event per node, seeded from the run seed and room, and prefers events you have not seen yet this run (a repeat is possible once every eligible event has been seen). `data/run.json` `events.min_max_hp` is the floor for max HP losses.
- **Curse cards** (`data/cards/curse_*.json`, 3 shipped): an ordinary card with `"curse": true`. It sits in its slot, can be cast and is swapped like any card, but has no or a harmful effect. It never appears in offers or in `--full-deck`, counts toward no deck minimum and never blocks a removal. The cleanse limit is `data/run.json` `curses.max_cleanses` (-1 = no limit; removing a curse is not a prune). Gained through events (and `--run-curses`); removed through events (`remove_curse`), a rest or the merchant.
- **Sacrifice relics:** a relic with `sacrifice_cost` N (4 shipped, tier 3) is taken by giving up N other relics, on a multi-select step with Confirm and Cancel (nothing is lost until Confirm). They are left out of offers while you cannot pay.
- **Event-only relics (6):** `sources` is `["event"]`; an event's relic offer reserves one slot for one of them when the offer has 2 or more slots. One of them, Gravedigger's Spade (stat `rest_dig`), adds the Dig to a rest.
- **Event fights:** an event can start an elite fight (`start_fight`); its rewards wait for the win. Run modifiers from `stat_mod` and a waiting merchant discount are listed on the pause screen.
- Code map and the add-an-event checklist: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "Events (MAP4)" and section 8.

### Continue and the save file (MAP3)
- A map run saves to `user://run_save.json` (one slot, in Godot's per-project user data folder; its exact location on this machine was not checked). It is written when the map screen opens and when a room starts, never before the first room of act 1, and deleted when the run is won or lost. Since MAP4 it is also written on an event's choice screen, on the relic offer that choice opened, on the give-up step (an event's and a sacrifice relic's), on an event's card-removal pick and at the start of an event fight (`RunSave.choice_is_savable`), so quitting after paying a cost cannot refund it. The rest-room Dig is not a boundary: a reload redraws the same seeded offer. Straight (`--no-map`) runs do not save.
- On the class pick screen, **Continue your run** shows when a usable save exists (it names the class, act and room, and starts focused). Quitting mid-fight and continuing restarts that room. Starting a new run from the class cards replaces the save at that run's first room start.
- A bad, old or unreadable save is ignored with a warning and left on disk; it never crashes the launch.
- **Never saves, reads or deletes:** headless runs, `-s` scripts, and any launch with a debug flag (`--run-room`, `--run-node`, `--run-gold`, `--run-clear-after`, `--run-offer`, `--full-deck`, `--deck`, `--relics`, `--relic-offer`, `--relic-cap`, `--event`, `--merchant`, `--run-curses`, `--run-curse-first`, `--run-auto-choose`, `--run-discount`, `--acts`, `--press` (TL1), `--scenario`, `--sim-*`, and (3d) `--runs-finished`, `--profile-path`, `--loadouts-path`, `--loadout`; the list is `RunSave.DEBUG_FLAG_PREFIXES`, which matches by prefix; `--merchant`, `--run-curses` and `--run-discount` joined it in DF1, 6be42da and 5da7427). So the headless tools, sims and the test suite cannot touch a player's save. The same rule keeps them off `user://profile.json` and `user://loadouts.json` (3d, see "Loadouts and the start screen"): `src/loadouts/LoadoutSession.gd` gives such a launch an in-memory profile and loadouts, unless it names a scratch file with `--profile-path=` or `--loadouts-path=`. A windowed capture with no debug flag is not suppressed, so it can write or delete the real save, and (3d) a windowed launch with only `--class` that finishes a run counts it into the real `user://profile.json`; pass a debug flag or scratch `--profile-path` in captures. `--run-curse-first` and `--run-auto-choose` joined the list in 3d (dc39f28). `--no-map` is not on the debug list, but since BD3a a windowed straight run that is won or lost deletes the save only when it owns a map run (it used to delete a saved map run too; fixed in f2e54d1, covered by `bd3a_housekeeping_test`, not run by hand here). A save with `hp` at or under 0, `hp` above max, `rejects_remaining` out of range or a bad `removed_cards` entry is refused like any bad save. Not run by hand in this pass: the Continue button itself (qa tests it; `UxCapture` `--mode=continue_pick` captures it).

### Loadouts and the start screen (3d)
- **What it is:** a loadout is a saved starting setup for one class: a name, a deck (only cards whose `availability` is `starter`), a **plan** (`reward` cards you hope to find; a card offer, mini-boss offer or merchant card on the plan that you do not own yet shows an "On your plan" pill) and an optional starter relic. Limits (slots per class, copies, deck size, name length) are `data/loadouts.json`; the deck must pass the same deck rules as any deck (`data/deck_rules.json`). Card `availability` is a field in each card file (D52 = A: common cards are `starter`, rare and legendary are `reward`, curses have none). Starter relics are the relics that list `starter` in `sources`.
- **Run count:** `user://profile.json` counts finished runs (won or lost, once per run). Run 1 (count 0) skips the start screen and starts with the preset deck. Loadouts are saved in `user://loadouts.json`. The default files are written only by a real windowed launch with no debug flag (a debug launch may write only the scratch files it names); a bad or old file is ignored (kept as `<file>.bak` before the next write), never a crash.
- **Start screen (from run 2):** it opens after the class pick, as a list: the preset deck, each saved loadout (card count, plan size, starter relic), skipped loadouts with the checker's reason (not selectable), and "Edit or create a loadout". The last used row is preselected with a "Last used" pill, so one Enter starts the run. Skipped for `--class` alone, in headless runs, scenarios, and with `--deck`, `--full-deck` or `--loadout`.
- **How to see run 2's start screen:** use scratch files, so the real profile is never touched: `godot --path . -- --class=rogue --runs-finished=1 --profile-path=<scratch>/profile.json --loadouts-path=<scratch>/loadouts.json`. Start screen capture (verified in this pass: saved a PNG, exit 0): the same plus `--screenshot-after=2.5 --screenshot-out=<png>`. Without `--runs-finished`, a real windowed launch with no `--class` reaches it only after one run has been finished into the real profile.
- **Start screen keys:** Up/Down move the highlight, Enter or a click starts (hover moves the highlight). **Editor keys:** Up/Down move through cards; Enter or a click adds a card; Delete, Backspace, Minus or a right-click removes one; Left/Right switch panels (and, inside the starter relic row, step between the relic buttons, which sit side by side since BD3b-0); Esc goes back; Ctrl+S saves (this key is not shown on screen, because the K1 rule keeps key hints out of data). Leaving or switching slots with unsaved edits, and Delete, ask once and go through on the second press. Esc in the editor does not open the pause menu. Save is blocked while the deck breaks a rule; the editor shows the checker's reasons and live counts.
- **Capture recipes** (need a renderer; each verified in this pass, a PNG saved, exit 0): the editor with a legal deck, the editor with a blocked deck, and a real act 2 shop whose plan holds rare and legendary cards:
  ```
  godot --path . res://tools/UxCapture.tscn -- --size=1024x600 --class=rogue --mode=loadout_editor_legal --out=<png>
  godot --path . res://tools/UxCapture.tscn -- --size=1024x600 --class=rogue --mode=loadout_editor_blocked --out=<png>
  godot --path . res://tools/UxCapture.tscn -- --size=1024x600 --class=rogue --mode=shop_plan_act2 --out=<png>
  ```
  A run that starts from a loadout: add `--loadouts-path=<scratch> --loadout=1` to a run launch, with a saved loadout in that scratch file (the playtester did this; its launch was not re-run here). The code map is in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "Loadouts (3d)".
- **Curse swap (3d):** swapping away a curse card costs a longer cooldown than an ordinary dead-card swap: `data/run.json` `curses.curse_swap_penalty` and `curses.swap_penalty` (see the data file for the values; changelog 2026-11-18 has the change).

### Relics (BD2)
- 57 relics in `data/relics/`, one per file (BD3b added 5, listed at the end of this bullet; MAP3b added 15 tier 1 to 3 relics and 8 boss relics, rarity `boss`, which are offered only by the mini-boss and can't be given up; MAP4 added 6 event-only relics and 4 sacrifice relics, see "Events and curses (MAP4)"; the list below is the older 19). BD2: Pyre Insurance, Smoldering Grudge, Lingering Embers, Second Wind (Unwillingly), Spite Knuckles (a Bleed relic since BD3a: every Nth light hit that lands sets the target Bleeding), Heavy Sigh. BD3a (non-Fire, physical Rogue): Serrated Excuse, Salt in the Wound, Per My Last Email, Chasing Paper, Muscle Memory, Overtime (Unpaid). MAP2 pool relics: ASAP Notice, Severance Package, Paperwork Fire, Grading Curve. MAP2 shop-only relics (`sources` exactly `["merchant"]`): Liquidate Assets, Request for Quotation, As Per the Attached Document. A relic is a run-long passive (stat modifiers and/or triggers on combat events); numbers and rules are in the files. The BD3b relics (all sourced from elite, relic room and merchant): Exit Strategy (Movement cards leave a line of fire), Thorned Boundary (with bonus armor, melee attackers are stunned and hit back), Reading List (Charge stacks higher), Filing Cabinet (a dead Utility or Movement card swaps free) and Hot Streak (Fire casts reset Attack: AoE). Numbers: the files. Starter relics (Chasing Paper, Spite Knuckles, Ergonomic Chair, Paperwork Fire) may also drop as ordinary relics (D53 = B).
- **Where they come from:** the elite room offers relics instead of cards (pick 1 of 3, or take the free Skip; skipping costs no reject), the merchant sells them (above), and the **relic room** (straight run only, since MAP1: once per run, never beside the rest room or the elite, on-screen rooms 3 to 9; on the map it is a relic stage on rooms 3 to 8, see below) offers the same with no fight. If no relic is eligible the room gives a card offer instead. Rest rooms and card rewards never offer relics (MAP4: the Dig, from the relic Gravedigger's Spade, is a rest action that offers 1 of 3 relics; events can offer relics too).
- **Rules:** cap 20 (`relics.max_owned` in `data/run.json`). At the cap, taking a relic first asks which owned relic to give up; Cancel is focused by default and keeps everything; boss-tier relics can't be given up. No duplicates, and a relic you gave up is never offered again that run. Tier odds per act are `relics.tier_weights` (the straight run uses act 1). The block also holds `enabled`: set it false and the run is exactly the pre-BD2 run (no relic room, a card offer after the elite).
- **On screen:** a chip row under the dodge pips (a trigger lights the chip and shows its name; rapid repeats stay lit with an "xN" count), relic offer cards with a free Skip tile, the give-one-up screen, and the owned and given-up relics on the pause screen.
- Counters (Spite Knuckles' every-Nth-hit count) and internal cooldowns restart each room.

### Rewards and deck-clean (Milestone 3b)
- After each won **combat** room (never the last; with `relics.enabled` false also the elite room, as before BD2): 3 card offers (`rewards.offer_size` in `data/run.json`). Pick one, or reject all (3 rejects per run, `rejects_per_run`). Offers are seeded, drawn only from the character's pools, and cards you do not own are weighted 4:1 over owned ones. A card may carry an optional `requires` filter. **The status lean (BD3a, D43 = B):** an unowned card that applies Bleed or Burn weighs more for each card you have added that applies the same status, up to a cap (`rewards.status_lean`, `status_lean_max` in `data/run.json`; D46 = B: `status_lean_skip_starter` true means the starting deck does not count). **Removed cards stay gone (BD3a, D47 = B):** a card you remove is never offered again that run, in card offers or the merchant's stock (`RunState.removed_cards`, saved). `tools\run_offer_report.ps1` measures both (see "Offer report").
- **Rest room:** Heal 30% of max HP, or remove a card (MAP4: with the relic Gravedigger's Spade, a third action, Dig, replaces both: pick 1 of 3 relics or skip). A card whose removal would break a deck minimum is greyed out with the reason; if none can go, the remove tile is disabled.
- **Starter deck:** 15 cards, 3 per slot, per class (`starting_deck` in `data/characters.json`; it used to be in `data/run.json`). It was the full 24-card Rogue pool before 3b. The deck minimum is 3 unique per slot, so with the plain starter the removal menu is disabled until picks add cards.
- The choice screen shows card tiles, a reject tile with pips, and a "Next: Room N of 10 - Type" line (on a three-act run: "Room N of 30"). Arrow keys move the highlight, Enter or a click confirms. Options ignore input for 0.4 s after opening (`prompt_option_arm_delay` in `data/ui.json`), and Space is swallowed (the Continue and New run buttons have the same delay, and Space no longer confirms them; the Game Over screen has the same 0.4 s delay and swallows Space, and its hint reads "Enter"). The offer panel shrinks to fit the window and never scrolls (tested from 1024x600 up); only the removal grid scrolls. Card tiles show "In slot: A / B / C" (the `slot_now` template in `data/run.json`; at most `slot_now_max` names, then "+N more"; PL3 removed the "<Slot> (<key>) now:" wording and the number-key hints). A reload mid-reward resumes the same offer.

Controls (from `data/input_bindings.json`): WASD move, mouse aim, left click light attack, right click heavy attack, Q, E, F, Ctrl, R use card slots 1 to 5 in order (Movement is Ctrl, Utility is F, the fifth is Recovery), **Space is the dodge**, and Shift plus the same key swaps a dead card (Movement swap is Shift+Ctrl). The slot keys still cast while Ctrl is held (Ctrl+Q casts Q's slot); the swap works whichever of Shift and Ctrl goes down first (`Player._swap_by_late_modifier`). The dodge is on (`dodge.enabled` is true in `data/player.json`; set it false to switch it off). The dodge does not count as a Movement cast for card conditions such as "if you used Movement in the last 2 s" (D39 = A); `dodge.counts_as_movement_cast` in `data/player.json` (false) is the one switch to change that. Windows caveats, not fixed: Ctrl+Shift is the layout-switch hotkey on multi-layout machines, and Ctrl+Space toggles the IME on some CJK setups. The dodge is a pool of charges that come back one at a time (see Player below). Every key shown in the UI (HUD slot hints, attack meters, dodge label, reward and removal screens, pause hint) is read live from `data/input_bindings.json` through `src/core/InputLabels.gd`, so a rebind there changes the text. No UI text may hardcode a key, and two actions with the same key and modifiers log an error at startup (`InputBindings.last_conflicts`). History: the keys were 1-5 until 24530e4 (Q, E, 1, 2, 3), slot 5 moved from 3 to R in K1, and in H2 Movement moved 2 to Space and Utility 1 to F (the dodge lost Space); in H3 the dodge came back on Space and Movement moved to Ctrl. Also: Esc pause/settings, F3 debug overlay. Movement cards (dashes, blinks, leaps) pass through enemies while they move; the dodge does not (a dodge in the post-dash ghost window may). The ghost look on the player appears only after `ghost_show_delay` in `data/vfx.json`. Holding a slot key shows the card's hit area and releasing casts it (change with the `cast_trigger` setting in the pause screen).

**Card cues on the HUD (BD1).** Each slot shows up to 3 tag chips under the card name: keywords (Keystone, Primer, Payoff) as filled chips, derived tags (Projectile, Area, Burn, ...) as outlined ones; when they don't fit, derived tags drop first (about 2 chips at 1024x600). A slot whose card has a condition that is true right now glows yellow (rings, corner brackets and a LIVE pill; it shows at reduced strength while the slot cools, and is eased so it doesn't flicker). A slot with a primed buff shows a cyan bolt ("xN" for several charges) and a drain bar. Reward offers and the removal grid show the same chips, a condition line (after the cooldown) on the card, and a keyword's rule text as a tooltip. The card slot is 142 px high (it was 134 before the chip row). Look values: `data/ui.json` `hud`; the data comes from `src/cards/SlotSynergyView.gd`.

`godot` must be on PATH, or set `GODOT_BIN` to the full path of the Godot executable, or call it directly: `"C:\path\to\Godot.exe" --path .`. All `tools\*.ps1` scripts resolve the binary from `GODOT_BIN` first, then `godot_console`/`godot` on PATH, and prefer a `*_console.exe` sibling.

## Running tests
Tests use [gdUnit4](https://github.com/godot-gdunit-labs/gdUnit4), vendored in `addons/gdUnit4`. Run the full suite headless:

```
powershell -File tools\run_tests.ps1
```

This runs every suite under `/tests` and exits non-zero on any failure. Last run: see "Last verification" below (2869 measured by gameplay-dev at HP1, 2875 expected with qa's 6 cases, unverified here; 2851 at UX1b, from qa; TL2, from game-lead's handoff: 2813 test cases, 0 failures; other counts not given in the handoff). A full run takes about 23 minutes on a quiet machine and about 90 minutes under heavy sim load (qa measured both). Reports go to `reports/` (gitignored). The script uses the console-subsystem `*_console.exe` build when one sits next to the resolved binary, because PowerShell cannot reliably wait on or read the windowed build, which made failures look like passes (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`).

**Engine logs (TL3a).** Every tool runner (`tools/sim_log.ps1` `Get-ToolLogArgs`) and every test that spawns Godot (`tools/tool_log.gd` `ToolLog.args`) passes `--log-file`, so the log goes to `logs\sim\<name>-<time>-<pid>.log` (gitignored; newest 60 kept) and never to `user://logs`. A new `OS.execute` in a test that launches Godot must pass `ToolLog.args(...)`. Child-process logs from tests have fixed names (`child-<name>.log`).

> After adding a new global class (`class_name`) the plain headless run may fail with "Could not find type X in current scope". Run `godot --headless --editor --quit --path .` (or `godot --headless --path . --import`, TL3a; after pulling it picks up `ToolLog` and `WatchText`) once to rebuild the class cache (verified: exits 0), then re-run the tests.

Tests and tools that press a card or attack key must send an `InputEventAction` (see `tests/support/test_input.gd`), because `Input.action_press` emits no event and the player reads input events.

Other helpers in `tests/support/`: `body_flag.gd` forces `body.collide_with_enemies` on or off for a test (tests that pin non-solid geometry turn it off), `test_caster.gd` is a test double for a damage dealer (so a test controls every hit), `test_clock.gd` (`TestClock.wait_real`) waits real seconds without the ignore-time-scale timer bug. The test log prints `ERROR:` lines from bad-data fixtures on purpose, so do not use "grep ERROR" as a pass/fail gate; the suite's exit code is the gate.

## Game data worth knowing
All content and tuning is JSON under `data/` (see `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 6 for the full table).
- **Deck rules** (`data/deck_rules.json`, read by `src/cards/DeckRules.gd`): `min_unique_per_slot` (3 today), optional per-slot overrides under `slots` (for example `{"utility": {"min_unique": 5}}`) and `slot_groups` (a named set of slots with `min_unique_total`). Only different cards count; copies never do. At run start a deck that misses the minimums is blocked: an error is logged, the spawner stays off and `Main.run_start_blocked` is emitted. The F3 debug overlay shows each slot's unique count against its minimum and marks shortfalls `SHORT`. The shipped deck passes with the default rules.
- **Characters** (`data/characters.json`, read by `src/core/CharacterDatabase.gd`): per class id, `display_name`, `description`, `pools`, `max_hp`, `body_color`, `attacks` and the 15-card `starting_deck`, plus two optional per-class tuning keys: `dodge_charges` (the class's dodge charge count, inside the `data/player.json` min and max) and `slot_base_cooldowns` (slot type -> seconds, overriding `data/slot_types.json` `base_cooldown` for that class; today only the Wizard's Recovery). The Wizard has 22 HP against the Rogue's 30 (W1b tuned it to 22, then put it back to 26; the director decided D20 = B on 2026-11-02 and H2 set it to 22, see `docs/RESOLVED.md`); its pool `wizard` holds 23 cards (counted from `data/cards/`; 7 of them BD3b's, the rest earlier, including rare and legendary ones) and its starter is built around the early ones (Ward, Technically is in the pool but not the starter); the Wizard's light attack is an auto-firing bolt (`light_variants.wizard` in `data/attacks.json`).
- **Player** (`data/player.json`): the `dodge` block (`enabled` = the on/off switch (H2 trial, shipped true since H3; optional, a missing value reads as the built-in default, true); speed, duration, `cooldown` = the recharge time of one charge, `charges` = the default count, `min_charges` and `max_charges` = hard limits applied after stat modifiers, `block_ticks_after_pause`) and `body.collide_with_enemies` (true: player and enemies are solid to each other; false restores the old walk-through behaviour).
- **Card frame colours** (`data/pools.json`, `frame_color` on every pool, validated on load): a card's frame is the colour of its pool (fire, class pools, neutral). Read through `PoolDatabase.frame_color_for_card(card_id)` / `frame_color_for_pool(pool_id)`. Drawn on the HUD slots (active card and next-up swatch), reward offers and removal cells. Sizes and slot plate colours are `data/ui.json` `hud` (`slot_frame_*`, `slot_plate_*`) and `run_ui` (`option_frame_width`).
- **Melee reach cue** (`src/ui/MeleeReachCue.gd`, `data/ui.json` `melee_cue`): a faint arc at the light attack's reach, shown only when nothing is in reach, brighter on a whiffed click. It appears only for a light attack of shape `arc` (the Rogue; the Wizard's bolt never shows it).
- **Keywords** (`data/keywords.json`, read by `src/cards/KeywordDatabase.gd`; BD1): the authored card keywords Primer, Payoff and Keystone, each with a `display_name` and a `rule_text`. A card lists them in its optional `keywords` array; derived tags (Projectile, Area, Burn, ...) come from its effects (`src/cards/CardTags.gd`). A card effect is `{template, params, condition?}`; condition types are in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 4.
- **Relics** (`data/relics/<id>.json`, read by `src/relics/RelicDatabase.gd`, validated by `RelicSchema`; BD2): per relic `id` (= file name), `name`, `pool`, `rarity` (`tier1`, `tier2`, `tier3`, `boss`), `sources` (which rooms may offer it), `keywords`, `modifiers`, `triggers` (event, filter, effects), a one-line `rule_text` and `flavor_text`. Adding one needs no code unless it uses a new stat, event or effect template. The `relics` block in `data/run.json` holds `enabled` (the master switch), `max_owned` (the cap), `offer_count`, `relic_room {count, first, last}` (0-based room indexes), `tier_weights` per act and (MAP2) `prices` by tier; the merchant's own block is `merchant` in the same file (`MerchantConfig`). Relic modifiers may also scale with a run stat (`per_run_stat` plus `per`) and `triggers` on `hit_landed` accept `tag` and (BD3a) `slot_type` filters. See `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` section 4 and "A relic" in section 8.
- **Zones** (`data/zones.json`): the `max_live` cap on ground zones (for example the Fire Wall) and their look.

## Screenshots and scripted scenarios
Capture is built into the game and runs only when user args follow `--` (nothing runs in normal play). It needs a renderer, so it must run windowed; `--headless` cannot capture. The game quits itself after the last shot. Output goes under `screenshots\` by default, which is gitignored.

```
powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1                                  # hp_bars scenario, 1 shot at 3.0 s -> screenshots\shot.png
powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario attacks -After "0.75,2.4,2.7" -Out screenshots\atk.png   # several shots -> atk_0.png, atk_1.png, ...
godot --path . -- --screenshot-after=3.0 --screenshot-out=screenshots/a.png --scenario=hp_bars
godot --path . -- --screenshot-count=3 --screenshot-interval=2.0 --screenshot-out=screenshots/c.png   # shots at 2 s, 4 s, 6 s
godot --path . -- --scenario=hp_bars                                          # play a scenario by hand, no capture
```

`capture_screenshot.ps1` parameters: `-After` (seconds, comma list, default "3.0"), `-Out` (default `screenshots\shot.png`), `-Scenario` (default `hp_bars`; `-Scenario ""` captures the normal run), `-Resolution` (default `1280x720`), `-Extra` (more game args, comma-split, for example `-Extra "--run-seed=7,--run-room=10"`), `-Preset <name>` (BD1: a named capture from `tools/capture_presets.json`, table below; any parameter you give explicitly overrides the preset; the default output is `screenshots\<name>.png`), `-Class rogue|wizard` (`--class=`, now also for scenarios), `-Actions "0.5:dodge,0.9:dodge"` (`--press=`) and `-AtBossAttack <id or name>` (`--at-boss-attack=`; TL3b: the attack id from `data/bosses.json` or its label as a snake_case name, so `ground_pound` is `slam`; a bad name exits 2 and lists the valid ones, verified; resolved by `tools/boss_attack.ps1`). Without `--screenshot-out` the game writes to `user://screenshot.png`. Run-screen examples (both verified, each saved a PNG, exit 0; `-Extra` also accepts several quoted args):

### True-size capture (`-TrueSize`)
The OS window is clamped to the monitor, so `-Resolution` cannot exceed it. `-TrueSize WxH` instead runs the real Main scene (with `-Scenario ""`, RunMain, via `--run`) inside a SubViewport of exactly that size (`tools/VpCapture.tscn`; Main registers with `src/core/WorldRef.gd` because `current_scene` is null there). Same scenarios and `-After` times as above. It needs a renderer (windowed), like all capture.

```
powershell -File tools\capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario c1_overlay -After "1.5" -TrueSize 2560x1440 -Out screenshots/t.png   # verified: saved one PNG, exit 0
godot --path . res://tools/VpCapture.tscn -- --size=1920x1080 --scenario=hp_bars --screenshot-after=2 --screenshot-out=screenshots/vp.png   # verified
```
Use forward slashes in `-Out`: backslashes and `$` get mangled when the command is typed in bash.

## Wave density sim
Plays the real Main scene (spawner, enemies, player) with a bot that uses the real input actions, then reports crashes, NaN, cap violations, enemies or player leaving the arena, stalls, damage taken, alive counts, spacing, and rough CPU frame cost. It runs as a scene (so autoloads exist), headless. `-Seconds` is game time, `-Scale` is `Engine.time_scale` (1 = real time and most faithful, default 3), `-God` keeps the player alive to exercise the whole wave table, `-Idle` is a player who never moves, `-Deck` (`standard` default, or the name of a preset in `data/debug_decks/`; passed to the game as `--sim-deck=`) starts every run with that legal preset deck, so armor comparisons are like for like. A preset is only the starting deck: rewards and removals change it as the run goes on. `-Class rogue|wizard` plays whole runs (`-Run`) as that class (passed as `--sim-class=`; without `-Run` the arena is always the Rogue and the script prints a note; a `-Deck` preset of the other class, or an unknown class, exits 2). `-LightHeavyOnly` is a bot policy, not a deck: the bot never presses any card slot (since H1; it used to skip only slots 1 and 2), so only light attacks, heavy attacks and dodging are used. Three flags are for `-Run` balance work (W1b): `-WizardNoKite` makes a ranged class (the Wizard) walk in like the Rogue bot instead of holding a stand-off, `-DodgePellets` makes the bot also step out of and roll through enemy projectiles in flight (off by default, because it changes results and the older baselines assume it off), and `-BossLog` prints, per boss attack, how often it was thrown and hit and the HP it cost in the boss room (a line per run and a summary). `-BossLog` and `-DodgePellets` need `-Run`. Since 3d, `-BossLog` matches a boss's hits through the act's `damage_scale` (it used to label the mini-boss's hits "unknown"), and the "HP at each room end" report compares each room with that room's own max HP (an event can cut max HP mid-run). `-StartRoom N` (PL3, needs `-Run`; passed as `--start-room=`) starts every run at room N of the first act (`rooms.size()` is one act's rooms, so 10 is act 1's last room: the mini-boss on a three-act sim run, the boss with `-Acts 1`; from reading `tools/wave_sim.gd`, unverified by a run) with the class's starter deck and full HP; a room past the end prints a plain "note:" (BD1-B). `-Plan` (BD1; passed as `--plan`) lets the plan-aware policy `PlanPolicy` play the card slots (it casts a payoff when its condition is live, swaps a card that can't pay off soon, spends Movement to set up a combo), in the arena and with `-Run`; without it the card presses are the old seeded random ones. BD2 options (all but `-NoRelics` need `-Run`): `-Relics "a+b"` (passed as `--sim-relics=`) starts every run holding those relics, `-RelicCap N` (`--sim-relic-cap=`) lowers the relic cap so a short run reaches the give-one-up step (the MAP3b qa finding at `-RelicCap 1` with a boss relic held is fixed in MAP4 sitting A, b2ae51b; the regression test in `map3b_qa_test.gd` now runs), `-NoRelics` (`--sim-no-relics`) sets `relics.enabled` false for that process (the pre-BD2 run: no relic room, a card offer after the elite) and `-Swap "old=new"` (`--sim-swap=`) replaces one copy of a card in the starting deck (checked against the deck rules; combine with `-Deck`). `-Run` also prints the damage the player's side dealt by source (light, heavy, each slot type, each status, each relic), the fingerprint spread, relic pick/skip/swap counts, a `BOSS TIMES` line (timed-out boss fights are listed apart) and a soft-lock detail line. `-Acts N` (MAP3, `--sim-acts=`; with `-Run` and the map on: the runs have N acts, default `data/map.json` `acts` = 3; `-Acts 1` is the pre-MAP3 one-act run and its numbers; with more acts the report adds an `ACT` line per act (clear rate, time, HP at the end, relics, gold) and an `ACTS TOTAL` line) selects the act count. `-Run` output streams as it arrives (BD3a; it used to be buffered, which made a 12-run three-act sim of 12 to 15 minutes look like a hang). `-Runs` repeats with consecutive seeds (verified: `-Seed 20 -Runs 2` ran seeds 20 and 21), `-Seed` sets the first seed. `-Run` plays whole seeded runs through `RunMain` instead (see below). The script launches Godot with `--fixed-fps 60` (one 60 Hz physics tick per frame) and turns hit-stop off in the sim; `-Scale` defaults to 1, and a `-Scale` above 1 drops `--fixed-fps` and the run no longer repeats.
