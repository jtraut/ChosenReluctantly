# Playtest: Slice A1 Armor (2026-10-29)

## Build and setup
HEAD c46e4e5, Godot 4.7.2 console build, Windows, RTX 3080. Windowed captures through `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1` and fight-length data from `tools/run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -Seed 1 -Runs 4 -Deck <family>`. **I did not play with a live keyboard and mouse.** Everything below comes from captures (which I viewed), scripted scenarios and the bot sim. Feel (hit-stop, shake, responsiveness) is not judged here. Screenshots are in `screenshots/a1/` (gitignored).

## Smoke check: PASS, no blocker
- `godot --path . --quit` exits 0 with no errors or warnings (only the engine banner and the Vulkan line).
- Every capture launched windowed, saved a PNG and quit itself with no console errors.
- World visible in all shots: floor, blue player, enemies, shadows, a camera that frames the player. HUD shows HP 9999/9999 (the scenarios give god HP), LMB/RMB bars, five card slots with names and "Next:" lines.
- Not verified: live movement, aim, dodge and slots 1-5 by hand. The `armor_numbers` scenario did cast slot 1 cards (slot 1 went into cooldown), so casting works.

## Findings, ranked

### BLOCKER
None.

### Should-fix
1. **Physical builds still take longer than fire at the boss in my sample.** Bot sim, 4 seeded runs per family, boss fight spawn to death: fire 50.5, 58.4, 61.6, 58.4 s (mean 57); physical 60.1, 76.0, 84.4, 57.3 s (mean 69); standard 63.3, 62.9, 86.7, 71.7 s (mean 71). Physical is about 1.2x fire, with big seed-to-seed spread (57 to 84 s inside one family). The ROADMAP quotes fire 0.93x and physical 1.04x of standard from another sample; mine gives about 0.80x and 0.98x. Small, bot-driven sample, so read it as "same band, fire a bit quicker", not as proof. A human would probably feel fire as the faster build at the boss. Suggest QA re-run with 10+ seeds before anyone retunes. The `lightheavy` family (no attack-slot cards) took 201 s and 238 s, and in the second run the boss did not die before the sim timeout (sim printed WAVE SIM FAILED). I take that as an expected stress case, but it means a player with no damage cards can be stuck on the boss for 3 to 4 minutes. See director question 2.
2. **The numbers are hard to tell apart in a crowd.** In `armor_numbers` the "[9]" armor number, the "<4>" resisted number and the big "12" overlap each other and the elite pips when four enemies stand together. Each style is distinct on its own (brackets vs angle brackets vs plain), but stacked and small at the moment of impact I could not tell at a glance which hit was which. The muted styles would also vanish among Burn ticks in a real fight. Suggest a slightly larger muted size or a short vertical stagger so stacked numbers separate.
3. **A new player is not told what "Armor" vs "Resists" means.** The bar says "Armor 50% - Resists all elements 50%", but nothing tells me which of my cards are physical and which elemental, so I cannot act on it ("is my Pointy Stick physical?"). The card slots in these shots carry no damage-type cue. The info is accurate but not yet actionable. Suggest a damage-type tag on cards (even a coloured pip) or a one-time tooltip at the first elite.

### Nice-to-have
4. **Elite pips read fine alone, are small in a clump.** The shield pip left of the bar and the orange diamond right of it are clear on a lone elite at 1280x720. In the 4-enemy clump, one elite's shield is partly hidden behind another enemy's bar and number. Pips only say "has armor", not how much; F3 covers that.
5. **Boss bar at 1280x720:** the right-aligned "Resists all elements 50%" text sits over the boss's head in `boss720_0.png`. Still readable thanks to the outline, a little noisy. 1024x768 is cleaner because the line wraps below the bar.
6. **F3 overlay is large and covers the arena centre.** Debug tool, so low priority, but it hid the elites in `elite_f3.png` while I was trying to read their pips. A compact mode for the defense lines would help.
7. **The glyphs at the two ends of the boss bar (diamond left, shield right) have no labels.** They echo the elite pips, but the player has to infer the meaning.

## Fun, clarity and difficulty notes
- **Clarity:** "Armor 50%" and "Resists 50%" being equal at the boss is clean and easy to remember. The elite split (25% armor, 50% resist for the golem) is visible only in F3.
- **Decisions:** with boss armor equal to boss resistance, fire and physical are designed to be a wash. That is the intended parity, but it means the boss gives no reason to prefer one family, and the bar says so in plain sight. Whether that feels fair or flat is a director call.
- **Difficulty and pacing:** sim boss fights landed at 50 to 87 s (target about 60 s) and whole runs at about 250 to 280 s of game time with the bot. The bot won every run; I cannot judge human difficulty. Player lowest HP in the boss fight ranged from 10 to 30 of 30 in the sim, so no obvious spike.
- **Feel:** untestable without live input. The cues are quiet by design, which suits "unobtrusive in a crowd" but works against "explain why that hit was small".

## What I could not test
- Live hands-on play: movement, aim, dodge, slots 1-5, hit feel.
- A human-slotted fire loadout vs physical loadout fight with `--full-deck --run-room=K`. I used the sim's deck families instead. The elite room for seed 1 is room 6 (from the sim output); other seeds range over rooms 4 to 7.
- Armor cues in a live wave crowd (the scenarios use idle clumps).
- Comprehension by an actual new player (my read is a judgement from screenshots).
- Sim numbers are bot-driven and small-sample.

## Questions for the director
1. Is "fire a bit faster than physical at the boss" acceptable, or should parity be tuned to within about 10%?
2. Should a build with no damage cards (the `lightheavy` stress case, 200 s or more at the boss) get any safety net, or is that the player's own problem?
3. Do you want a damage-type cue on cards (physical vs element) before the T-decks slice, so players can use the armor information?

## Suggested next playtest focus
A live, hands-on fire vs class loadout elite and boss fight once T-decks gives named presets. Also check damage-number readability during a real wave (Burn ticks, bleed and armor numbers together).
