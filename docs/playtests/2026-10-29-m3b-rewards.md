# Playtest: Milestone 3b, rewards and deck-clean

Date: 2026-10-29. Build: HEAD of main (294b2ce). Playtester.

## Build and setup
I cannot press keys or click. Evidence is windowed captures of the real launch (`capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario ""`, 1280x720, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 true-size) and the real-HP whole-run sim (`run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -Scale 1`, seeds 3, 5, 9, 11, one bot run each; qa ran 12 runs at scale 8). Combat feel and real card decisions are NOT judged by hand; the bot picks cards by its own rules. Screenshots (gitignored): `screenshots/m3b/` (`smoke.png`, `reward720.png`, `reward1080.png`, `reward1440.png`, `rest720.png`, `remove720.png`).

## Smoke check: PASS
- Normal launch: version banner only, zero error or warning lines in all 6 capture launches.
- World, blue player, red chaser, "HP 30 / 30", LMB and RMB meters, five card slots with "Next:" names, and "Room 1 of 8 - Combat" top centre (`smoke.png`).
- Not tested by hand: real WASD, dodge, clicks. The sim bot uses the real actions and clears all 8 rooms. No blocker.

## Clarity verdict
**Reward screen: clear. A new player can answer all four questions without being told.**
- What a card does: name, effect line ("9.0 fire damage (projectile). Cooldown 1.2 s"), flavor line (`reward720.png`).
- Which slot: a "Slot 5 / Recovery" pill using the HUD's slot numbers and names.
- What rejecting costs: a pink tile "Reject the offer / Spends one reject / Rejects left: 3 of 3" with three pips.
- What is next: "Next: Room 2 of 8 - Combat" with the shape icon at the bottom of the panel.
- Fit: 720p and 1080p fit with no clipping. 1440p fits and reads well; the panel top slightly overlaps the dimmed HP label (cosmetic, qa already logged it).
- Gaps (not blockers):
  1. The card tile does not show what you already have in that slot or what you would be adding to. A player offered "Four Quick Stabs" (slot 1) can't judge it without remembering the deck. "You own N" only appears for owned cards.
  2. Effect lines are terse: "Leech 7.0 s" (what does leech do?), "dash 6.5" (no unit), "2.0 x4" (no total).
  3. The Reject title wraps to two lines, uneven beside the cards.
  4. Nothing tells a first-timer that a card joins that slot's rotation.

**Rest room: heal vs remove is clear; the removal block feels like a wall.**
- At full HP: "Rest and heal: HP is already full" (fixes the old "Healed +0"), and "Remove a card" greyed with "Unavailable: Every card is needed to meet the deck minimums" (`rest720.png`).
- The wording is understandable but not actionable. It doesn't say "own a 4th card in a slot first". With the 15-card starter the removal option is a locked door in early rest rooms.
- With `--full-deck` the removal grid reads well: five slot columns, headings, greyed Utility cards with "Utility needs 3 unique cards, deck would have 2", scroll, Back (`remove720.png`). The reasons are specific. The greyed cells' orange reason text is louder than the available cells.

- **Deck growth:** 15 to 18-19 cards. Six reward rooms were offered (picks plus rejects = 6); the bot rejected 2 of 6 every time. Slot mixes differ (seed 3 ended with 6 single attacks, seed 9 with 3), so runs do diverge a little.
- **Attrition is real now:** mean room-end HP is about 85% (range 47% to 100%), with in-room lows of 14-18 HP in rooms 2-5. Better than 3a (always 30). Room 8 shows 30/30 in all four runs, probably the win-state reset; I do not read it as real.
- **Removals:** 1 in 3 of 4 runs, 0 in seed 3. Rest heals used in 1 of 4 runs. The rest room is mostly "heal or nothing", as predicted.
- **Run length:** 253-293 s for a bot, same as 3a. By hand, with card reading, perhaps 8-12 minutes. Far under the 15-25 min proposal and the GDD 30-60.
- **Where runs fail:** qa saw 5 of 6 losses at the placeholder boss (room 8 takes 42-116 s, ends at 1-4 HP). All four of my real-time runs won; the boss room is 25-33% of the run (72-94 s).
- **Roguelite arc?** Partly. There is a shape (fight, pick, rest, elite, boss) and a growing deck. Missing: the starter is the same every run and there is no class, element or subclass choice, so "no two runs alike" is lightly met.

## Top 3 things that work
1. The reward panel: option cards with slot pill, effect line, flavor, "You own N" and a separate Reject tile with pips read cleanly at all three resolutions. Flavor ("Four stabs, one sigh.", "Hit things, feel better. Ethics pending.") fits the tone.
2. The rest screen: two big tiles, honest "HP is already full", and specific deck-rule reasons in the grid.
3. Reject tension exists on paper: the bot spends 2 of 3 rejects across 6 offers, so rejecting is neither free nor useless.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **Offers are decided blind.** The screen doesn't show what is already in that slot. Suggestion: add "Slot 1 now: A, B, C" to each tile. This is 3b's main decision surface.
2. **The removal door is locked most of the run.** The reason isn't actionable and removals only happen after duplicates arrive. Suggestion: reword to "Own a 4th Utility card first", and consider 4 per slot in the starter or allowing removal when you hold a duplicate (already so) plus a visible hint.
3. **Boss room is a third of the run and the main failure spot.** 72-94 s, 1-4 HP in lost runs. 3c replaces it; judge 3b balance with that in mind.
4. **Weak attrition-vs-power tension.** About 85% HP at room end, and rest heals mattered in 1 of 4 runs. Re-measure after 3c; if still near full, trim Recovery in the starter or cut rest heal.
5. **Effect text reads like debug output.** Suggest plain phrasing from `run.json ui.effect`, including units and totals; fix the Reject title wrap.

## Other observations
- 3 rejects against about 6 offers: by room 6 offers are a forced pick. Watch in hand play.
- Input safety (0.4 s arm delay, number keys only highlight) is a good call (qa verified synthetically).
- I did not capture an elite reward screen (same panel).

## Questions for the director
1. Should the reward card show what is already in its slot? (I say yes, cheap.)
2. Is a locked removal in early rest rooms acceptable, or should the starter be 4 per slot?
3. Is 3 rejects against about 6 offers the intended tension, or should rejects scale with run length?
4. Is 8 rooms final, given a 4.5-5 bot-minute run?

## Suggested next playtest focus
3c boss and elite, then ideally a hand-played run: note where the first picks confused the player, whether they rejected, and whether they tried to remove a card.
