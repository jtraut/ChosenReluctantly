Date: 2026-10-29. Build: HEAD of main (e5dc3d1). Playtester.

## Smoke check: PASS (no blocker)
- Normal launch, the three boss scenarios, real room 8 (`--run-seed=1 --run-room=8`), a reward screen and a rest screen: every launch printed only the Godot and Vulkan banner. Zero error or warning lines in 8 launches.
- Room 1 shows the world, "HP 30 / 30", LMB and RMB meters, five card slots with "Next:" names, and the room indicator top right (`smoke.png`).
- In real room 8 the boss bar appears top centre and the room indicator ("Room 8 of 8 - Boss", star) top right, with no overlap. Standing still, the player was dead in under 14 s (HP 30, then 16 at 9 s, then "Well. That went badly."). An idle player is punished fast, which seems right.
- Not tested by hand: WASD, dodge, clicks. The sim bot clears all 8 rooms with real actions.

## Boss readability (observed in screenshots)
The shared language is consistent. The boss body goes from purple (idle) to orange (winding up) and a pink ground shape appears. The aim is locked at the start of the wind-up (read in `Boss.gd`), so the shape stays true for the whole wind-up. A bar label and a small draining strip under the HP bar name the attack: "Overhead smash / Ground pound / Fan of regrets / Reluctant stampede".

| Attack | Telegraph | Impact | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overhead smash (cone 3.4 m, 8 dmg) | An outlined fan from the boss that fills in as the wind-up runs (`sheet_a` #0 to #1). | Fan turns solid pink, number "8" (`sheet_p3` #5). | Clear: "get out of the wedge". The wedge is wide (130 degrees), so a sidestep is not enough; you must back away or go round. |
| Ground pound (disc r 3.2 m, 9 dmg) | A pink disc on the player's own spot with an inner ring (`sheet_a` #5, `sheet_b` #6). | A "9" on the player (they stood still). | The clearest attack. The disc is locked where you stood, so one step out is enough. Almost too easy, which is fine as a teaching attack. |
| Fan of regrets (volley, 5 lanes, 5 dmg each) | Five translucent lanes fanning out from the boss (`sheet_b` #10). | Pink dots fly down the lanes (`sheet_c` #12 to #14). | Readable. The shots are small and the lanes run off screen (problem 5). |
| Reluctant stampede (charge, 11 m lane, 10 dmg) | A long red rectangle from the boss through the player (`sheet_c` #15). | The boss ends on the far side, "10" on the player (`sheet_c` #16). | Clear, and the biggest threat. The lane is long enough to fill a third of the screen, which reads as big and dangerous. |

- **Phase change: obvious.** At the threshold (`sheet_edge` #0) the boss turns purple, a yellow ring expands on the floor, and the bar fills with red-and-white diagonal stripes labelled "Invulnerable". Phases 2 and 3 appear as "Phase N of 3: name". The phase names ("Warming up", "Getting into it", "Far too into it") carry the tone better than anything else on screen.
- **Bar: reads well.** Name, a ghost trail, notches at 66% and 33%, the phase line, "Resists all elements 50%" and the attack label with its timer. Resistance is stated up front. The gaps are in problem 4.
- **Adds: they clutter.** In phase 2 two red chasers run into the boss's own cone (`sheet_edge` #2, #4). They stand on top of the pink wedge, so its edge is harder to see, and they body-block you toward it. In phase 3 I did not see the shooters fire (the capture ends too early), so I could not judge them.

## Fairness and difficulty
- **Verdict: no attack looks unfair on a screenshot.** Every telegraph is visible for at least 0.77 s. The player walks at 6 m/s with a 5 m dodge, and the boss walks at 2.6 to 3.4 m/s. The aim lock means you can circle it, then leave the shape. I found nothing hidden behind the body, because the telegraph is drawn outward from the boss and the camera looks down at an angle.
- **Not judged:** adds plus a charge at once, and phase 3 with 0.8 s gaps between attacks, as a human. The bot uses the boss's own `escape_direction`, so its 8 of 8 only proves the attacks are escapable, not that a human will find the escape. Please hand-play phase 3.
- **Sim numbers (real HP):** seed 1 won, boss fight 62.0 s, lowest HP 13 of 30; seed 2 won, 56.1 s, lowest HP 22; seed 3 won, 63.4 s, lowest HP 26. All match qa. About 60 s is a good length for a dodge-heavy fight. A human who reads more and misses more will take longer, and since the boss never staggers, a long fight only feels like progress if the bar visibly moves.
- **Do player hits feel like they do anything?** Unknown by eye. By code: a hit flash, knockback 0, stun 0, so the feedback is the flash, the damage number and the bar's ghost trail. Fire cards deal half (a 9 damage Ember lands as 4.5). The sim's casts favour the physical rogue singles (Ember, Overhand, Quick each over 2x their slot mean), so a physical build probably clears faster than a fire build.
- **Run arc (sim, 3 seeds, bot):** all won in 230 to 256 s. The boss room is 59.7 to 67 s, about 26% of the run. HP at the end of room 7 was 30, 22 and 30, so the boss starts near full HP and is the only real HP test. Lowest HP per room was mostly 14 to 30 of 30. The rest rooms healed 0 or 1 time (seed 1 was at full HP). Decks ended at 17 to 19 cards.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The phase transition.** Purple tint, floor ring, striped "Invulnerable" bar and a named phase line make the roar impossible to miss, and explain why your hits do nothing.
2. **Telegraph consistency.** Four attack shapes, one pink language, one orange "winding up" tint, aim locked, visuals matching the hit areas (qa probed edges at 0.05 m). The ground pound with its inner ring is a model telegraph.
3. **The boss bar and its tone.** It answers "which phase, how much, what resists, what is next" in one place, and the phase names and attack labels ("Fan of regrets", "Reluctant stampede") land the humour without getting in the way.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **The adds clutter the one thing you must read, the cone.** Chasers walk into the smash wedge and sit on top of it (`sheet_edge` #2, #4). Suggestion: draw the telegraph above the adds (outline pass or higher alpha), give the adds a clearly different tint, or have the boss not smash while an add is in its cone.
2. **Thin feedback for a 60 s fight with no stagger.** Only a flash, a number and the bar. Fire builds halve their damage with no explanation. Suggestion: a small bar jolt or ghost burst per hit, a tiny shake on big hits, and mark resisted damage numbers (a tint or "resisted") so 9 becoming 4.5 makes sense.
3. **Comma-separated "Slot N now" is unreadable.** It is the main decision surface of the reward screen. Use one name per line, or a separator that is not a comma.
4. **The attack label is small and sits under the bar.** At 1280x720 it is about 10 px tall, and when the boss stands at the top of the screen its body is behind the bar and the "Resists" text (`boss_0.png`). The ground shape is the real telegraph, so this is secondary. Suggestion: a larger label or a one-line banner at wind-up start.
5. **The boss and its lanes can be partly off screen.** In real room 8 the boss starts in the top-right corner, cut off by the frame (`sheet_room8` #0), and volley lanes run past the screen edge (`sheet_b` #10). Suggestion: spawn it nearer the centre and cap the visible lane length.

## Proposals
- Capture tooling: an input script (hold a key, click) and a "kill boss now" arg so the playtester can screenshot the death beat and win screen.
- Fix the rest-tile removal-lock text to match the grid's actionable wording.
- Explain Leech in plain words in the effect line.

## Questions for the director
1. Equal 50% resistance to every element makes fire strictly weaker than physical against the boss, with only one element in the game. Intended? (GDD section 7 says equal; the question is whether it feels fair today.)
2. Should the boss never stagger? It is a readability win, but the player's cards never change the fight's rhythm. Is a short stagger at a phase threshold worth it?
3. Run length: the bot clears 8 rooms in 230 to 256 s. The GDD target is 30 to 60 min. Is 8 rooms a deliberate milestone-3 shortcut?
4. The boss name is placeholder, while the phase names and attack labels already carry the voice. Is a one-line phase quip wanted before story work starts?
