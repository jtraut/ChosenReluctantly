## Smoke check: PASS (no blocker)
- Rogue and Wizard run captures (room 1, room 10) launched with no errors or warnings in the engine output. Floor, lit player, enemies, HUD (HP 30/30 and 26/26), five card slots, "Room N of 10" are all visible. Wave sim ended "WAVE SIM OK" for both classes (it fails on any ERROR/WARNING).
- Not checked by me: live movement, aim, dodge and slot keys. Only through bot and scripted scenario.

## 10-room run (bot sim)
- Rogue: 3 of 3 won, 10 of 10 rooms. Combat rooms 18-40 s, elite 42-46 s, rest 0 s (instant), boss 68-84 s. Whole run is about 5.0-5.5 min of sim time for a competent bot. That is far under the 30-60 min target; a human will be slower but this is a lot shorter than the target. (Question below.)
- HP drops: Rogue mean HP at room end 82-100% for rooms 1-8, 64% in room 9 (seed 23 hit 6 HP there). Dips come in the combat rooms just before the boss, not in the elite. The bot arrives at the boss at full HP every time (the room-9 to room-10 gap is refilled to 100% in the sim's "HP at last room clear"), and the boss costs only about half its HP (lowest 14-16 of 30).
- Wizard: HP is 100% in almost every room (lowest HP 21-26 of 26), then the boss is a cliff. Seed 21 lost at full HP 26: dead in 6.4 s of boss time, boss untouched. Seed 22 won with lowest HP 11.1. So the Wizard is trivial for 9 rooms and then either nothing or a near instant death. This is a real spike (see problem 1).
- Dead stretch: rooms 1 to 8 for the Wizard (never threatened). Rogue rooms 4-8 hover around 85-90% HP with no pressure either.

## Movement cards as escapes (capture and tests)
- Reluctant Lunge through a line: the preview is now a full rectangle that runs through all the enemies in the line, and after the cast two enemies show "8" damage numbers and the player lands in the middle of the crowd. It reads right and the damage numbers are a nice "oh nice" beat.
- Nope, Leaping Away (back leap): the cast ring and arc show it leaving the crowd, and the slot greys out on cooldown with "Next: Walk Through Everyone". Reads fine.
- Not seen in a still frame: whether passing through bodies feels like an escape instead of a bump. qa's tests say all six dash cards cover full distance with no overlap at the end. I did not see Ugh Fine Moving, Blaze and Bail, Sideways Blink or Tactical Hover move live. Bot sim: Blaze and Bail 1.4x and Ugh Fine Moving 1.4x are cast a lot, so the bot likes them. Sideways Blink 1.34x, Tactical Hover 0.25x (OUTLIER, rarely cast). Shadow Step never cast in this run (and the Lunge 0.91x is fine).
- After a lunge or dash the player's body sits overlapping enemies (capture: blue body inside grey and red). A player may read that as "stuck" for a moment; qa says a ghost look only appears after 0.35 s. Worth a hand check.

## Wall of Mild Fire 7.5 x 1.8 m
- The preview is now a big strip, visibly larger than a player and about two golems wide. Burn-down visual (burning strip with a bright centre line) matches the preview outline. Size reads well.

## Boss room 10 at 1024x600
Both Rogue and Wizard captures (seed 7): the boss is fully on screen, its head (top about y=130) is clear of the HUD, boss bar and phase text. The "Fan of regrets" telegraph covers a large part of the left half and runs off screen on the left edge. Clear and readable. Pass.

## Top 3 things that work
1. The boss framing fix: big boss, bar, phase and telegraph name all readable at 1024x600.
2. The Wall's new size and the Lunge preview both show exactly what will be hit; "Room 10 of 10" fits.
3. Rogue runs flow: 10 of 10 reached every time, no stalls, no errors, varied elites and rests (positions spread over rooms 3-8).

## Top 5 problems
1. **Wizard difficulty is flat then a cliff.** Rooms 1 to 9 cost the Wizard almost no HP, then the boss killed a full-HP Wizard in 6.4 s (seed 21) or nearly did (11 of 26 on seed 22). A player sees nothing to learn from then dies fast. Suggest W1b tuning looks at mid-run pressure and a boss opening that is not a 6 s kill, or a first-phase telegraph that gives time to react.
2. **Run is short.** About 5 minutes of bot time against a 30-60 min target (even a slow human is likely 15-25 min). Rest rooms take 0 s of fight. Pacing suggestion: more enemies per room, or longer rooms, before adding rooms. Director call.
3. **Combat rooms 4-8 have no pressure for the Rogue.** HP is 85-90% at every room end and the boss takes only about half the HP. Late rooms do not escalate.
4. **Wall and Shadow Step are not being cast by the Rogue bot, and I could not check the Wall catching walkers.** Not a player finding yet, but the buff is unproven. Needs a walking-enemy scenario.
5. **Post-dash overlap.** The player ends inside enemies after Lunge or a pass-through dash; I could not feel whether it reads as an escape. Also qa's pre-existing launch quirk (exact-concentric restore) is now reachable by more cards, tiny odds.

## Questions for the director
- Is about 5 minutes of bot clear time acceptable as the 10-room length, given the 30-60 min goal? Is more content per room or more rooms the answer?
- Wizard boss: should the first seconds be survivable by design (W1b)?
- qa's open question: reword GDD section 3 about the dodge in the post-dash window, or hard-block it.
