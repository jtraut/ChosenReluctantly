# PL2 short playtest (2026-10-30)

## Build and setup
HEAD b39810c (qa PASS). Windowed Godot 4.7.2, RTX 3080, via `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1`. Captures are in `screenshots/pt_pl2/` (gitignored). I cannot use hands, so this is stills only: no feel judgement of live input, and the idle bot died in room 2 (expected). Observed facts are marked; the rest is opinion.

## Smoke check: PASS, no blocker
- Launches, no console errors in any capture run.
- Rogue and Wizard: floor, lit world, player, HUD (HP 30/30 and 26/26, LMB/RMB meters, five slots, room indicator, boss bar) all visible, camera frames the player.
- Not verifiable here: live input response (move, aim, dodge, slots 1-5). Covered by qa tests, not by me.

## Findings by scope item
1. **Boss room (observed):** boss is fully on screen in all four captures (Rogue seeds 1 and 7 at 1024x600, Wizard seed 7, Rogue 1280x720). It is a big purple capsule at roughly 250-300 px from the player, readable. The bar, name, phase 1 of 3, "Armor 50% - Resists all elements 50%" read clearly. **N1 confirmed:** Rogue seed 7 at 1024x600 puts the boss's head directly behind the RMB meter (about 15 px overlap) and touching the HP panel. It is not clipped and not a play problem at the first frame, but it looks sloppy. The boss is standing still at 2-3 s, so I could not judge the fight itself.
2. **Rogue melee cue (observed):** a thin pale-blue arc in front of the player with nothing in reach. Quiet. Opinion: it reads as "something about range here", and a new player probably guesses melee, but nothing says "swing". In the room-2 crowd capture it is not drawn while enemies are in reach and the swing wedge is the loud thing, so no clutter. Not confused with the heavy wind-up in what I saw, but I did not capture a heavy wind-up with the arc showing. The Wizard has no arc, correctly.
3. **Frame colours (observed):** on the slot strip, reward offers and removal list, orange, light bronze and grey read as three families without any label. Fire vs bronze is distinct side by side (reward screen, removal grid). Opinion: bronze vs grey is the closer pair on the slot strip at 1024x600 (bronze is pale, grey is mid); distinct but I had to look. Ready vs cooling: I did not catch a cooling slot in my stills, so I rely on qa's captures here and did not verify it myself. Reward focus ring on a grey card (N2): the white ring is visible in my capture (Healing Potion card), but it is the weakest pair and has a small white notch artefact at its left and top edge. Suggest a thicker or brighter ring, or a glow, for focus.
4. **Class pick (observed):** no HP, meters or slots behind it, only the dimmed room indicator. PASS. The copy ("Someone has to go in there...") lands the tone.

## Top 3 things that work
1. Frame colours: instant family read, and fire orange pops, especially the all-fire Wizard strip.
2. Boss now starts in view with a legible bar, phase notches and resist line.
3. Pick screen is clean, and its class descriptions tell the melee vs ranged story better than the in-world cue does.

## Top 5 problems
1. **Melee cue is subtle** (mild). A new player may not tell it is the reach of a melee swing. Suggest a brief fade-in swing flash on first light attack, or a tiny "melee" label only in the first room.
2. **Boss head under the HUD meter at 1024x600** (N1). Widen the spawn box's top margin.
3. **Bronze vs grey is the closest frame pair** on the slot strip. Consider a slightly more saturated bronze (e.g. #d9a066) if confusion shows up. Check with a live player first.
4. **Focus ring on grey reward card (N2)** is visible but weakest, with a notch artefact.
5. **Not checked: boss fight itself and cooling slots**, because stills only. Not a defect, a coverage gap.

## Questions for the director
- Is a "subtle arc" the right level for the melee cue, or should a first-run hint teach it explicitly?
- Is bronze vs grey close enough to warrant tuning now, or wait for a human to try it?
