- Godot 4.7.2, commit 5287546. Windowed stills with `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1`, plus `run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run`. I cannot press keys or watch motion here, so feel, hit impact, bolt speed and click-to-speed-up are NOT observed. Anything about motion is theory.
- Screenshots (gitignored) are in `C:\Users\jaket\Workspace\ChosenReluctantly\screenshots\w1a\`: `pick_1024.png`, `pick_1280.png`, `w_r1_0..2.png` (room 1), `w_room3..6.png`, `w_boss_0..2.png` (room 8), `w_bolt_0..2.png`, `w_aoe_0..2.png`, `sim.txt` (sim output).

## Smoke check: PASS, no blocker
- Launches with no errors or warnings (I grepped the capture output for error and warn: nothing).
- The pick screen shows with `--show-class-pick`. World, player, HUD and the card row show in room 1, room 4 and the boss room. Wizard HP reads 26 / 26 and the body is purple.
- Not checked: live input (movement, dodge, slots 1 to 5, Enter and click on the pick screen).

## Findings

### BLOCKER
None.

### Should-fix
1. **The `wizard_bolt` and `targeted_aoe` scenarios show a blue Rogue body** (`w_bolt_1.png`, `w_aoe_1.png`), even with `--class=wizard` and `"character": "wizard"` in the scenario data. The bolt is the right cyan one, so the scenario seems to set the attack set only. Anyone judging a Wizard capture from these gets the wrong picture. Possible bug or intended; not diagnosed.
2. **The Wizard's starter has several close-range-sounding cards.** Slot 1 shows "Pointy Stick" (neutral), and the deck also has Shrug Bomb, Hot Head and Deep Sigh. The class pitch is "keeps its distance", but some starting slots read as Rogue-style. I could not play them to see whether they work at range.
3. **The boss is mostly off-screen at the start** (`w_boss_0.png`, `w_boss_1.png`): about a quarter of the orange body shows at the top-right edge. A ranged class fights from far away, so it matters more here: you cannot see the boss that is hurting you. Worth a hand check of the camera.
4. **The pick screen backdrop shows "HP 30 / 30"** while Wizard is highlighted (`pick_1280.png`). It is dim, but a new player can read it as the Wizard's HP. Small.

### Nice-to-have
- The pick screen tone works: "Someone has to go in there. It might as well be one of these two."
- A one-line playstyle tag on each card ("Forgiving", "Careful") if one class proves harder.
- In `w_r1_1.png` a pink fan sits at the Wizard's feet with enemies adjacent. I could not tell whether it is an enemy attack telegraph or something of the player's. If it is the player's own cone, the Wizard has a melee cue it should not need. Check by hand.
- The Rogue melee cue (planned for W1b) still matters.

## Pick screen (item 2)
- At 1024x600 and 1280x720 it is clear and nothing is clipped. The highlighted card has a bright outline, and the prompt changes to "Enter: play as Wizard". The "1 / 2 or the arrow keys. Enter or a click to start." hint is readable at 1024.
- It tells a new player the key difference in plain words: "Melee. Hits what is in front of it, up close." against "Ranged. Fires a bolt at the cursor and keeps its distance." The attack icons (green fan against dart-and-dot), HP 30 against 26 and the blue and purple swatches all help. That directly answers the confusion the outside tester had.
- It says nothing about the Wizard's heavy being a targeted blast. That is fine for a first pick.

## The Wizard in play
Observed from stills:
- **Bolt direction and legibility** (`w_bolt_1.png`): the cyan bolt trail runs from the player along the aim line toward the far enemies. Enemy shots are hot pink with a longer fade and clearly different. Cyan against pink reads well at a glance, and the dotted aim line makes direction obvious. The bolt goes where the reticle points: yes, in stills.
- **Heavy telegraph at range** (`w_aoe_1.png`): a large lavender ring with an inner disc lands on the enemy cluster 5 or more metres away. It is high contrast and readable. The meter says "CAST", which fits. The enemies in that scenario are idle, so I did not see it against moving crowds.
- **Different from the Rogue?** By data and look yes (purple, ranged bolt, 12 m, targeted heavy, 26 HP). By feel: untested.
- **The original complaint (left click hits what is under the reticle):** by design yes. Caveat: the bolt flies along a line and hits the first thing on it, so a target under the reticle behind a nearer enemy may not be hit. Test by hand.
- In `w_room4.png` the enemies were around 8 m away and no bolt was visible. I could not tell whether auto-fire needs a target in line or fires regardless.

## Difficulty (sim, `-Run`, 1 run per seed, bot plays)
| Class, seed | Result | Boss fight (spawn to death) | Boss lowest HP | Room-end HP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wizard 1 | WON | 45.2 s | 12 / 26 | full (26) in all 8 rooms |
| Wizard 2 | WON | 52.2 s | 21 / 26 | full except room 3 (23.2) |
| Wizard 3 | WON | 62.6 s | 13.4 / 26 | full in all 8 rooms |
| Wizard 4 | WON | 58.1 s | 17 / 26 | full in all 8 rooms |
| Rogue 1 | WON | 63.3 s | 10 / 30 | below full in rooms 1, 2, 8 |
| Rogue 2 | WON | 62.9 s | 26 / 30 | below full in rooms 1 to 4 and 7 |
| Rogue 3 | WON | 86.7 s | 22 / 30 | below full in rooms 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |

- Boss time: Wizard average 54.5 s, Rogue average 71.0 s, so the Wizard is at about 77% of the Rogue's. That is inside the W1b target of 70 to 130%, at the fast end, even with 80% light DPS.
- Rogue seed 3's room-end HP was 19 to 28 in four rooms and its mid-room lows were 14 to 15; the Wizard's lows in the same sort of rooms were mostly 26 (it was never hit).
- The Wizard ended 31 of 32 rooms at full HP (4 seeds x 8 rooms, rest rooms included; the one exception was seed 2 room 3). It is not trivial at the boss: lows of 12 to 21 of 26.
- **Likely cause (my opinion; the bot is not a human):** range plus kiting plus auto-fire. The bot can shoot from 12 m while backing away, most enemies are melee chasers that must close in, and auto-fire means no attack input is needed while dodging. The starter has 4 movement cards (Blink, Backward Leap, Blaze and Bail, plus one more) and 3 to 4 recovery cards (Potion, Leech, Regeneration). A human is likely to kite worse than the bot, so this is the ceiling, not the typical player.
- Verdict: a bit too safe in ordinary rooms and fine at the boss. A note for W1b, not a blocker. Levers: bolt range, enemy speed or ranged-enemy counts, the starter's recovery count.
- Limits: 4 Wizard seeds and 3 Rogue seeds, one run each.

## Fun, clarity and pacing notes (theory plus stills)
- Wizard combat rooms take 14 to 28 s and elites 28 to 36 s; the Rogue's 17 to 35 s and 39 to 54 s. A whole run is about 3 to 3.5 sim minutes, well under the GDD's 30 to 60 minute target. This is not new to W1a but it is the largest pacing gap in the game.
- Card names (Sideways Blink, Borrowed Focus, Footwork Allegedly, Tactical Hover) match the tone.
- The "Next:" line under each card helps a newcomer; in the stills the card text is small but readable at 1280x720.

## Gaps a player would notice
- **Only 4 Wizard cards and none is an attack.** The Wizard's attack slots are all Fire and Neutral cards, so apart from the bolt and heavy a Wizard run plays much like a Rogue run. Two Wizard runs will differ mostly by rewards drawn from a small pool. That undercuts "no two runs alike" until W1b.
- Rewards come from a small Wizard, Fire and Neutral pool, so offers may repeat quickly. Not observed.
- Enemies are the same for both classes. Melee chasers feel weak against a ranged hero and enemy shooters feel stronger.
- No preview of the starting deck at the pick screen.

## Questions for the director
1. Is a Wizard that mostly clears rooms untouched fine as the "easier" class, or should W1b close the gap with the Rogue's risk? (The boss-time target is met; HP risk is not.)
2. Should the pick screen label playstyle or difficulty, or are "Melee" and "Ranged" enough?
3. Should the Wizard's starter swap its melee-sounding neutral cards for caster-flavoured ones?
4. Run length is about 3 minutes in the sim, far from the GDD's 30 to 60 minutes. Does the director want that addressed before more classes?
5. Should the pick backdrop reflect the highlighted class (HP and body), or stay generic?
