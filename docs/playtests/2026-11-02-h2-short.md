# Playtest: H2 dodge-off trial (short)

**NO BLOCKER.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at d0afb31, Godot 4.7.2 console build, Windows, RTX 3080 (real renderer).
- **What I could do:** launch real runs and capture frames (`capture_screenshot.ps1`, `-Scenario ""`), and play whole 10-room runs with the wave-sim bot (`run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -Seed 2 -Runs 3 -Class <c> -BossLog`). Captures: `docs/playtests/img/h2-pt-*.png`.
- **What I could not do:** press keys myself. I did not feel Space under a real hand, hold-to-preview, or react to a telegraph. Everything about "feel" below is read from frames and bot numbers. The bot presses cards at random and cannot choose Space on purpose, so it is pessimistic (and its Movement use is not a human's).
- **No-card run:** no tool plays one live. I used (a) a captured Rogue run left idle with cards: dead in room 1 by 14 s (`h2-pt-rogue-run_1.png`), and (b) qa's no-card bot table (every class dies in room 1 on the shipped build, was rooms 1-8). This is not a real no-card playthrough.

## Smoke check: PASS
- Run launches, no ERROR or WARNING lines in the capture logs (3 launches).
- World visible: floor, player, enemies, aim reticle and dotted aim line, boss with a cone telegraph and a phase bar.
- HUD: HP 30/30 Rogue and 22/22 Wizard, LMB/RMB bars, five-card strip reading **Q E F Space R**. No dodge row, no gap where it was. Room counter top right.
- Game over screen: "Well. That went badly." with a "Try again" button and an "Enter" hint (not Space).

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The HUD changed cleanly.** Q E F Space R matches the cards (Movement is the 4th card, labeled "Space"; Utility "F"). Nothing on screen is left asking for a dodge key.
2. **Telegraphs are big and readable.** The boss Overhead smash shows a pink cone on the floor plus a named, filling bar ("Overhead smash"). If you eat it, it reads as your mistake.
3. **Game-over is now deliberate.** The 0.4 s arm delay and the Enter hint mean a Space-spamming player does not skip their own death screen. The dry death line still lands the tone.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **The run now collapses in the early-middle, which reads as a spike, not a curve (bot data, not human).** My 6 bot runs: Rogue lost 3/3 (rooms 2, 10, 10), Wizard lost 2/3 (rooms 7 and 8), one Wizard win. Rogue room 2 on seed 4 went 30 HP to 2 HP lowest. qa's 16-seed table has the same shape: deaths cluster rooms 2-10 and every no-card bot dies in room 1. A new player with a 5-card starter and no dodge has very little margin in the first three fights. Suggestion: if the director keeps the dodge off, watch rooms 1-3 first; the trial question is whether a human survives them without a panic button.
2. **A full boss volley is a one-shot for the Wizard, and it is the largest slice of boss damage.** Boss log: pellets are 5 each, 5 pellets, so a volley is 25 against 22 HP. In the one Wizard boss clear, volleys were 63% of the damage taken (15 of 24). Rogue boss: smash 42%, charge 33% (charge 10 per hit, smash 8, slam 9). The D20 reasoning ("a full volley is your fault") holds only if the lanes are readable. In the frames I got I could not capture a volley mid-flight, so **I cannot confirm lane readability.** Next playtest should target that. Fairness note: the bot was inside a lane at fire time in just 1 of 6 volleys yet 5 of 30 pellets hit, so some damage comes from lanes after the fire moment. Worth a human check that the lane stays dangerous after the telegraph and shows it.
3. **Movement as the escape depends on luck of the draw.** Each slot is a shuffled mini-deck, so Space may hold "Tactical Hover" (a slow hover in the boss screenshot) or "Blaze and Bail" or "Backward Leap". With the Movement card on cooldown the player has nothing. The strip's "Next: ..." line helps, but it does not tell you when the card is ready as clearly as a dodge pip did. Suggestion: judge this live; if it hurts, the director's call is whether Movement cards should be shorter-cooldown while the dodge is off (a retune, which the brief says not to do now).
4. **The bot cannot answer "is Space comfortable".** The Rogue cast mix shows Movement cards used as random presses (Shadow Step 0.26x, Lunge 0.44x). Nothing here says whether a human can find Space under pressure while aiming with the mouse and holding WASD (thumb on Space, ring finger on F). Not a defect, a gap: the director's hands-on session is the real test.
5. **Wizard 22 HP is thin against 8-10 damage hits.** Smash 8, charge 10, slam 9: a charge plus a smash is 18 of 22. Rogue has 30. With no dodge there is no second chance after a mistake, so Wizard feels one error away. Suggestion: none yet (trial). Note it for the "stay off?" decision.

## Key feel
- Space reads as Movement on screen: the 4th card is labeled "Space" (`h2-pt-boss.png`, `h2-pt-rogue-run_0.png`). F reads as Utility ("Hot Head" on F). Both labels fit the key box.
- **Remaining "dodge" ideas:** no visible one. HUD text, game-over screen and card strip are clean. Still in data/code and harmless: the `dodge_*` UI config entries and `DodgePips`, plus the Fireball flavor "Slow enough to dodge. Enemies never do." (a joke, and ironic now).
- The "Hot Head" Utility card now sits next to Movement on adjacent keys (F, Space). Unverified how that feels by hand.

## Questions for the director
1. Rogue clears 19% and the Wizard 44-50% in bot runs, and the bot is pessimistic. What clear rate do you want for a human on the standard deck? That sets whether the dodge stays off, or comes back with fewer charges.
2. D20: is a full volley killing the Wizard (25 vs 22 HP) the intended "it is your fault" moment, or should the Wizard survive one full volley? Today it does not.
3. If Movement is the only escape, should its cooldowns be looked at, or is "sometimes you have no escape" the point?
4. Space also confirms the pause screen's Resume button. If you mash Space when you open the pause, it may close at once (qa's note). Do you want it armed like Game Over?

## Suggested next playtest focus
A human session (director) with cards and without: rooms 1-3 survival, Space under pressure, and a boss volley, stopping to note when you felt "that was cheap". For tooling: a way to capture a mid-volley frame (a boss scenario with a fixed attack) would let me judge lane readability without a hands-on session.
