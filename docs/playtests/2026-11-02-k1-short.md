# Playtest: K1 (key labels follow the bindings) - short

Date: 2026-11-02. HEAD a7f8b6f (qa PASS). Tester: playtester, no hands: windowed captures via `tools\capture_screenshot.ps1` (real run, 1280x720, RTX 3080) and the scripted `cast_preview`, `settings_open` and `game_over` scenarios. Not played by a person at a window. Captures: `docs/playtests/img/k1-pt-*.png`.

## Smoke check: PASS
- Rogue and Wizard run launches (`--class=rogue|wizard --run-seed=3 --full-deck`): console shows only the Godot/Vulkan banner, no ERROR or WARNING.
- Floor, player, 4 enemies, lighting and a camera framing the player are visible. HP 25/30 (Rogue, after a hit) and 21/26 (Wizard), not zero. Damage numbers and the slash cone show, so combat is live.
- Scripted input responds: slot_1, slot_2, slot_4 and slot_5 held and released cast (scenario log and captures), pause key opens the settings panel, a scripted kill opens Game Over.
- Not verified (no tool): live mouse and keyboard movement, aim, real dodge, Shift+key swap, Space mash. Blocker: none.

## What I checked
- HUD strip (Rogue and Wizard): keys read Q, E, 1, 2, R left to right. Labels are centred at the top of each card and nothing clips. LMB, RMB and "Space" labels sit under the HP bar. The longest card text ("Next: Fireball, Incoming", "Next: Footwork, Allegedly") fills the card edge to edge on Wizard but still fits.
- Hold R: the scenario holds slot_5 (Healing Potion) at 6.51 s and releases at 7.40 s. Capture at 6.9 s shows the preview ring under the player and no heal (HP 9939); after release HP is 9955, a green heal ring shows, and the R card goes dark and cycles to "Heal By Hurting". PASS: hold previews, release casts Recovery.
- Pause: "Escape also resumes" shows; the strip behind it still shows Q E 1 2 R, dimmed. Fits.
- Game Over: strip dimmed behind, hint reads "Enter or Space".
- Reward (Rogue and Wizard): "Key R" / "Key Q" / "Key 1" / "Key E" chips and "Recovery (R) now: ...", "Attack: Single (Q) now: ..." lines present. Nothing overlaps or clips. Wizard option 3 wraps its title to two lines and still sits clear of the chip.
- Removal grid (ux capture `k1-remove.png`; I could not reach it myself, `--run-auto-choose=remove_menu` did not open it for me): headers "Attack: Single (Q)", "Attack: AoE (E)", "Utility (1)", "Movement (2)", "Recovery (R)", footer "Keys 1-5 jump to a column, Enter confirms".
- Not scriptable here: Shift+R swap-without-cast. I did not see it. `tests/unit/cast_input_6a_test.gd` covers Shift swaps; treat as qa-verified, director to eyeball.

## Top 3 things that work
1. The strip is clear: key on top, card name in the middle, "Next:" under it. In the capture I could tell Q casts the first card, R the heal, without being told.
2. Reward text now says what the slot is, not a number. "Recovery (R) now: Eventual Healing / Healing Potion (Lukewarm) / Nap Time (Brief)" tells me what I would replace and where it lives. The "Key R" chip is easy to spot.
3. Recovery on R is a good move. Hold-to-preview then release-to-heal worked on the new key, with a visible preview ring and a heal ring on release. R is far from the 1/2 movement and utility keys, so a panic heal does not collide with them.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **Reward screen has two meanings for the digits 1 to 4.** Option cards are numbered 1, 2, 3, 4 (highlight keys), and a chip on the same card says "Key 1" (Wizard capture: option 1 "Deep Sigh", chip "Key 1 Utility"; option 3 "Three Bolts", chip "Key Q"). In the Rogue capture option 1 carries "Key R" and option 4 is the reject. A new player will read "1" as "press 1 to take this" and "Key 1" as the same thing, or press 1 expecting it to cast. Before K1 the slot numbers matched the options loosely; now it is two unrelated number systems on one card. Suggestion: change the highlight badges to something that cannot be a slot key (A, B, C, D is wrong, as Q/E/R are slot keys too; use a plain "Pick 1" or drop number badges and use left/right arrows plus Enter), or make the chip text "Casts on 1" and give it a different look from the option number.
2. **Removal grid: "Keys 1-5 jump to a column" beside "Utility (1)" and "Recovery (R)".** Headers now show slot keys, and the hint right under them says digits 1 to 5 jump to columns, but digit 1 jumps to Attack: Single (header "Q") and digit 3 jumps to Utility (header "1"). Digit 5 jumps to the R column. I read this as a bug on first look: the header says (1), the hint says 1 jumps, and they disagree. Suggestion: either drop the header keys on this screen only (they do not help here), or reword the hint to "Press 1 to 5 for the first to fifth column" and stop printing slot keys in headers on this screen.
3. **Game Over hint "Enter or Space" is still a hardcoded key string** in `src/ui/UiConfig.gd` (`DEFAULT_GAME_OVER.hint`). K1's own goal was "no UI text hardcodes a key". It also makes Space (dodge) the confirm key for the arena Game Over, which I could not mash-test. The run's prompt screen swallows Space and has a 0.4 s arm delay; I found no equivalent in `src/ui/GameOverScreen.gd`, so a dodge-mash on death in the endless arena would very likely restart instantly. Theory only: not tested. In the run itself Game Over goes through the prompt screen (guarded). Needs a look by qa or gameplay-dev.
4. **Card strip is cramped on Wizard.** "Next: Footwork, Allegedly" fills the card width with no margin, and two-line card names ("Walk Through Everyone", "Three Bolts, Reluctantly") push the name close to the "Next:" bar. Not clipped, but one longer name will be. Minor, and not a K1 regression.
5. **Slot-name words on the reward screen are a mouthful.** Rogue "Attack: Single (Q) now: Ember, Whatever / Overhand, Whatever / Quick, I Guess" runs to four lines on a card and buries the card's own description; the card flavour text is pushed below. It is useful for a veteran and heavy for a new player. Suggestion: show the "now:" line only for the card being replaced, or cap it at two names plus "+N".

## Questions for the director
1. Reward option numbers vs "Key N" chips (problem 1): which fix do you want? Different badge style, or dropping number-key highlight on reward screens?
2. Removal grid: keep slot keys in the column headers (problem 2) or keep digit jump? Digit jump is positional, so headers like "Utility (1)" will always disagree with it.
3. Is "Enter or Space" on Game Over meant to stay (Space doubles as dodge)? GDD says no hardcoded key labels.

## Suggested next playtest focus
A person at a window: type Shift+R, Shift+Q on a dead card and confirm no cast; mash Space on death in the arena and in a run; mash Q/E/R while the reward panel opens; and watch whether a first-time player presses 1 on the reward screen expecting a cast.
