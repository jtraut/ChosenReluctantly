# Playtest: H3, the dodge returns (short)

**Blocker: NO.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at b210aa4, Godot 4.7.2 console build, Windows, real renderer.
- **What I could do:** launch real runs and capture frames (`capture_screenshot.ps1`: the `boss_volley` scenario at 5 times, a Rogue and a Wizard run at 2 s and 6 s), and play whole 10-room runs with the wave-sim bot (`-Run -Seed 2 -Runs 3 -Class rogue|wizard -BossLog`, and `-LightHeavyOnly` for the no-card run). Frames are in `docs/playtests/img/h3-pt-*.png` (local, not committed).
- **What I could not do:** press keys myself. I did not feel Space or Ctrl under a real hand, did not hold Ctrl while steering with WASD, and did not react to a telegraph. All "feel" below is read from frames and bot numbers. The bot presses cards at random and dodges by script, so it is a pessimistic and non-human proxy. Three runs per class is a small sample.
- **No-card run:** the bot with light, heavy and dodge only (Rogue). Not a live playthrough.

## Smoke check: PASS
- Launches with no ERROR or WARNING lines in the capture and sim logs.
- World visible: floor, player, enemies, aim reticle and dotted aim line, melee arc, boss with lanes. The camera frames the player.
- HUD sane: HP 30/30 Rogue, 22/22 Wizard, LMB/RMB bars, a dodge row labeled **Space**, five card slots **Q E F Ctrl R**, room counter top right.
- Rogue shows 2 dodge pips, Wizard shows 1 (confirmed in frames). An idle player took damage (Rogue 5/30, Wizard 7/22 after 6 s standing still), so enemies are live.
- Input: not directly verifiable by me. The bot's dodges and card casts work end to end, and qa's tests cover Ctrl hold with WASD and clicks.

## Answers to the director's questions
1. **Fluid again with the dodge back?** Bot evidence says mostly yes. Rogue won 2 of 3 whole runs (H2 bot: 0 of 3 on similar seeds), lowest HP per room mostly 13-26 of 30, boss cleared with 16-27 HP lowest. The Wizard won 1 of 3 and the boss is where it dies. I cannot judge the feeling in the hand.
2. **Is Ctrl comfortable for Movement while moving with WASD?** Not verified. Reasoning only: WASD, Shift (swaps), Ctrl (Movement hold) and Space (dodge) are all the left hand, so "hold Ctrl to preview, steer, dodge, release to leap" is a lot of chording. The director should try exactly that.
3. **Do faster telegraphs and Fan of regrets read in time?** The volley looks good in frames: five wide pink lanes from the boss, a named bar "Fan of regrets" filling under the boss bar, and the lanes visibly go from dim (about 1.9 s) to bright (about 2.2 s) as the fire moment nears. That brightening is a real help. What I could not check is reaction time: 0.75 s (0.64 s in phase 3) is tight on a keyboard. The gaps between lanes are slivers, so the safe spot is not obvious (problem 3).
4. **Does the single Wizard pip read ready versus recharging (EMPTY)?** Ready reads fine: one lit cyan pip under "Space" (`h3-pt-wizard_1.png`). I did not capture EMPTY this pass (`UxCapture --mode=pips_empty` exists; not re-run), so EMPTY is unverified. The row is small and top left, away from where eyes are in a fight.
5. **Is a no-card run winnable?** Not on the bot. Light and heavy only plus dodge lost in room 1 in 3 of 3 (69 s, 48 s, 53 s; lowest HP 1-4 of 30), same as H2. The bot is a poor stand-in for a human who aims, but the margin is not close. I would treat "cards carry the run" as consistent with the game's premise, not as a defect.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The volley lanes brighten toward the fire moment.** It turns a wall of pink into a countdown, and the named bar says what it is. The best-telegraphed attack I have seen in the game.
2. **The dodge row is back and clean.** Two pips for the Rogue, one for the Wizard, labeled Space; Movement relabeled Ctrl on the card strip. Nothing is left asking for the old key.
3. **The Rogue run has a curve again.** Lowest HP per room bounces (26, 22, 13, 25, 26, 26, 22, 18 in the winning seed 2) and the Rogue clears the boss with HP to spare. H2's early-middle spike is much less visible in the bot.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **The Wizard still dies to the volley; it is all of its boss damage.** Bot boss log: the volley is 100% of boss-room damage, hit in 13 of 13 volleys (20% of pellets), about 30 HP per boss visit against 22 max HP. One Wizard boss win (9.3 HP lowest), one boss loss (3.3 HP), one run lost in room 4. The faster windup (0.9 to 0.75) probably sharpened this. Why it matters: the Wizard's final fight is a coin flip on one attack, unlike the Rogue's. Suggestion (gameplay-dev, director's call on numbers): look at the Wizard's volley damage or pellet count, or a second charge for the Wizard in the boss room.
2. **Possible hits after the fire moment, or a lane versus pellet mismatch (bot data, unconfirmed).** The log says the bot was inside a lane at fire time in 4 of 13 volleys, yet all 13 volleys landed a hit. Some of that may be the bot walking into pellets in flight, but it is the same smell as in H2. Suggestion (qa or gameplay-dev): in `boss_volley`, park a bot between lanes at the fire moment and confirm no damage, and in a lane and confirm damage; log both numbers.
3. **Lane gaps are narrow and the safe spot is hard to find.** In `h3-pt-volley_3.png` the five lanes cover most of the player's side and the gaps are slivers. The player is told where it is dangerous, not where it is safe, and at 0.64 s in phase 3 there is little time to pick a gap. Suggestion (ux): make the gaps readable (a faint safe tint) or make sure the volley is meant to be answered with the dodge roll and say so in the bar text.
4. **The Wizard's single pip is easy to miss; EMPTY is unverified.** With one charge each dodge is a decision, so the state must read at a glance, and the row is tiny and top left. Suggestion (ux): a flash on the player when the charge returns, or a larger pip for 1-charge classes. Capture `pips_empty` and `pips_recharge` for review.
5. **Ctrl for Movement is untested by hand and may be the weak link.** The chord load on the left hand is high (see answer 2). qa's tests cover Ctrl hold with WASD and clicks and left and right Ctrl. Suggestion: the director tries it; if it is uncomfortable, the brief says to stop and ask for a design call, not to pick another key.

## Key feel
- The card strip reads Q E F Ctrl R and the labels fit. Card names such as "Nope, Leaping Away" and "Ugh, Fine, Moving" land the tone without getting in the way.
- The "Next:" line under each card is useful.
- Utility (F) and Movement (Ctrl) are no longer adjacent, and the dodge is now on a separate key again, which reads cleaner than H2's F and Space pair.

## Questions for the director
1. Should the Wizard (22 HP, 1 pip) survive a full volley? Today it cannot reliably, and the volley is most of its boss fight. Intended "Wizard risk" (GDD section 5) or too much?
2. Is a volley windup of 0.75 s (0.64 s in phase 3) the right speed, given the brightening helps but the gaps are narrow?
3. Ctrl for Movement: keep or change after your hands-on run?
4. A no-card run dies in room 1. Fine ("the cards are the game"), or should a no-card run last a few rooms?
