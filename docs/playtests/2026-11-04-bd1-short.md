# Playtest: BD1 synergy core (short)

**Blocker: NO.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at f2ef23b, Godot 4.7.2, Windows, real renderer (RTX 3080).
- Could do: real-renderer run captures of all four decks (`--deck=<id> --run-seed=3 --run-room=2`, with scripted presses of slots 1, 2, 3, 5, 4 and dodge, then more casts), plus the presets `bd1_primed` (1024x600), `bd1_glow_target` and `bd1_reward_keystone_wizard`. I looked at the frames.
- Could not do: press keys by hand, feel a dodge, or watch the glow flicker over time. Scripted presses are blind (they do not react to the glow). Flicker and feel statements are from single frames plus opinion. I did not play the boss room, and I did not read the sim numbers myself; the Controlled Burn / Drive-By comments below are theory only.
- Frames: `docs/playtests/img/bd1pt-kindling.png`, `bd1pt-hitandrun-live.png`, `bd1pt-controlledburn.png`, `bd1pt-lounge.png`, `bd1pt-primed-600.png`, `bd1pt-glow.png`, `bd1pt-reward-wizard.png`.

## Smoke check: PASS
- No ERROR or WARNING lines in any of the 7 runs.
- World visible: floor, player, enemies, aim line, camera on the player. HP 30/30 Rogue, 22/22 Wizard. Slots Q E F Ctrl R all show names, tag chips and a "Next:" line. Casts happen and cool down (slots dim and fill) in all four decks. Dodge pips drop after Space.
- Capture note: `--press` takes `slot_1` to `slot_5` and `dodge`, not `slot1`. My first try errored on that (my mistake, not a bug).

## Per deck
**Rogue, Kindling** (`bd1pt-kindling.png`, `bd1pt-glow.png`)
1. Glow: in `bd1pt-glow.png` Fan the Flames gets a thick yellow frame plus a yellow LIVE pill when burning enemies are in its area. It is the loudest thing in the strip. In the busy room-2 frame no slot was live, so I could not test the glow against heavy clutter, only on a calm scene.
2. Plan: chips say Keystone, Area, Burn, Primer. The condition text says "if the target has Burn". I could work out "burn first, then fan" from the text; the chips alone do not say it.

**Rogue, Hit and Run** (`bd1pt-hitandrun-live.png`, `bd1pt-primed-600.png`)
- Drive-By lights LIVE with Keystone and Payoff chips right after a Ctrl cast, while the rest of the strip is dim. Readable at a glance, even with a pack on screen, pink hit effects and damage numbers (this frame is the best evidence the glow survives clutter).
- Risk: the frame shows 21/30 HP with a pack on top of me after the dash-in. A Ctrl dash does not spend a dodge charge, so the risk is moving into the pack, not the charges. Without playing it I cannot say it feels worth it; it looks like a mild, real risk.
- Space does not count as Movement (D39 = A). A new player will press Space and wonder why Drive-By stays dark. Nothing on screen says so.

**Wizard, Controlled Burn** (`bd1pt-controlledburn.png`)
- Slow Burn (Zone, Burn) is in E and the Zone shows as a red cross on the floor, easy to see. No LIVE slot in this frame, so I did not see Heat Sink light up while the Zone was out. Not verified.

**Wizard, Departure Lounge** (`bd1pt-lounge.png`)
- 3/22 HP in the frame, the Wizard in melee range of four enemies. The bot is not a fair judge, but a Dash + Area deck that ends up in melee is the opposite of the Wizard fantasy. A human should try this one.

## The five questions, short
1. Glow reads: yes in calm and busy frames; flicker not tested.
2. Plan changes keys: yes in theory (cast the primer first); discoverable from card text, not from chips.
3. Hit and Run risk: see above, risk is the pack, not charges.
4. **Primed marker** (`bd1pt-primed-600.png`): a small cyan bolt at top-left of the slot and a thin cyan drain bar on its top edge. At 1024x600 the bolt is about 10 px and the bar about 2 px, easy to miss next to the loud yellow LIVE pill. The bar shrank 0.7 s later, so it does drain. I did not see a "used up" moment. Not verified.
5. **Keystone reward** (`bd1pt-reward-wizard.png`): understandable. The green condition line ("While you have a Zone active: 10 fire damage (area)") reads well. Not exciting: Keystone is a purple chip the same weight as "Area", and Heat Sink carries both Keystone and Payoff, which is two chips for one idea.

## Also checked
- **New cards in normal runs:** I only ran presets, so this is theory. A Rogue who rolls Drive-By into a random slot gets a card that stays dim with no reason shown.
- **1024x600:** the strip fits. Three chips fit on one row, a fourth would not. The Next line wraps to two lines without clipping. The primed bolt is the one item that gets too small.
- **Sim signals:** theory only. Drive-By being weak fits the screen: it needs a Ctrl cast first, and Ctrl is a dash with a cooldown, so it rarely lights.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The LIVE glow.** Thick yellow frame plus pill, the loudest object in the HUD, not confused with the orange element border.
2. **Condition text in green on rewards.** It states the rule in plain words.
3. **Chips on slots.** Area, Zone, Dash and Heal are visible at a glance, and nothing clips at 1024x600.

## Top 3 issues
1. **Primed marker is too small and has no "used up" beat** (owner: ux). Suggest a bigger bolt or a cyan slot outline, and a short flash when the prime is spent.
2. **Dead conditional cards give no reason** (owner: ux, then gameplay-dev for any text). Show the condition line on the HUD slot, dim when not live, and note "Dodge (Space) does not count" on Movement-conditional cards.
3. **Keystone does not feel like a big deal** (owner: ux; game-lead for wording). Suggest a distinct Keystone look (gold border on the tile, or one chip instead of Keystone plus Payoff) and a "build around this" line.

## Questions for the director
1. Should Space get a hint on Movement-conditional cards, or is D39 learned by trying?
2. Should a Keystone feel like a rare, exciting pick? If yes, it needs a stronger look.
3. Is Departure Lounge meant to end up in melee?

## Suggested next playtest focus
A human run with Drive-By and Heat Sink: does the glow light at the right moment, and what happens when a prime is spent? Then a plain Rogue run to see a random BD1 card in the starting slots.
