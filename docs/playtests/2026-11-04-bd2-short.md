# Playtest: BD2 relics (short)

**Blocker: NO.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at e0db92b, Godot 4.7.2, Windows, real renderer (RTX 3080).
- Could do: real-renderer captures of the four presets (`bd2_relic_elite`, `bd2_relic_room`, `bd2_relic_swap`, `bd2_pyre_explosion`), plus my own: a Wizard relic room, a cap-1 give-one-up, the pause screen with three relics, and a Rogue run holding Pyre Insurance, Spite Knuckles and Heavy Sigh with scripted blind presses (light x5, heavy, slots 2/3/4, more lights).
- Could not do: play by hand, watch the chip flash over time (single frames only), see the Spite Knuckles counter tick, press Cancel, or finish a run. The scripted Rogue died at about 14 s because the presses are blind, so it is not a fair judge. I never saw a Heavy Sigh or Spite Knuckles trigger in a frame. Statements about flash noise, pacing and "does it change how I play" are opinion from frames plus the data files.
- Frames in `docs/playtests/img/`: `bd2pt-elite.png`, `bd2pt-room.png`, `bd2pt-swap2.png`, `bd2pt-swap-cap1.png`, `bd2pt-pyre-0.png`, `bd2pt-pyre-1.png`, `bd2pt-pause.png`, `bd2pt-rogue-combo.png`, `bd2pt-wizard-room.png`.

## Smoke check: PASS
- No ERROR or WARNING lines in the 8 launches. The run starts. Floor, player, enemies and camera are fine. HP is 30/30 Rogue and 22/22 Wizard. Dodge pips show (2 Rogue, 1 Wizard). Slots Q E F Ctrl R show names, chips and Next lines.
- Light, heavy and slots 2/3/4 fired in the scripted run (cooldown bars moved, HP dropped from enemy hits). Pause opens and shows the relic list.

## The ten questions
1. **Relic offer vs card offer at a glance: yes.** (`bd2pt-elite.png`) The title says "Relic found" with a gold diamond. Every option has a purple "Relic / Tier N" banner with a hex icon, and the relic room uses a purple hex. I did not put a card offer beside it this pass. The three relic borders follow element colour (orange Fire, grey Neutral), so the banner is what carries "this is a relic", not the border.
2. **Skip is obviously free: yes.** A green card with an arrow, "Take nothing. This does not use a reject." and a green "Free" pill, the same size as a relic. Small issue: "reject" is jargon on a screen that never explains it.
3. **Why did it explode?** (`bd2pt-pyre-0.png`, `-1.png`) Partly. The frame shows a big orange disc, three "4" damage numbers, and the HUD chip "PI" with a yellow frame and a "Pyre Insurance" tooltip at the same moment. If I look at the top-left I get it. A player looks at the fight, where I see an orange blast and no label. The chip is about 30 px, far from the action. With one relic it is not noisy. With three relics (Pyre Insurance fires on every Burn that ends, Heavy Sigh on heavies) I expect the row to flicker a lot, but I cannot test that from a frame. Suggestion: a short floating label ("Pyre Insurance") at the blast, once per burst, or a distinct ring so it is not mistaken for a card's Area.
4. **Give-one-up makes sense: yes.** (`bd2pt-swap2.png`, `bd2pt-swap-cap1.png`) A "Taking:" box shows what you get, the held relics are listed with full rule text, and Cancel is a normal button. I could not press Cancel in a capture, so "Cancel keeps everything" is **not verified by me**. Two points: at cap 1 the line reads "You hold 1 relics" (plural). And the default focus is a held relic ("Enter to give this one up"), not Cancel, so one reflexive Enter loses a relic you were carrying.
5. **Pause relic list: readable, cramped.** (`bd2pt-pause.png`) Each entry has name, tier, pool, the rule line and the flavour line, with a coloured left edge. With three relics only two are visible; the third (Heavy Sigh) needs scrolling in a box about 150 px tall with a thin scrollbar. At 1024x600 it will be worse (not captured). The header reads "Relics (3 of 20 held)". The 20 sits oddly beside the roadmap's hard cap 10.
6. **Does a relic change how I play?** On paper, partly. Heavy Sigh (heavy on a Burning enemy) and Spite Knuckles (count four lights) ask for a play pattern, which is the good kind. Pyre Insurance and Smoldering Grudge are close to silent numbers: they trigger whenever Burn exists, so a Burn Rogue just gets stronger without choosing anything. Second Wind (Unwillingly) is a nice small loop with Movement. From my frames I cannot say any relic changed my moves. Theory only.
7. **Relic room on room 9.** It reads well: "Next: Room 10 of 10 - Boss" is under the cards, so you know the boss is next, and a free pick before the boss is a relief. But a room-9 relic is used for one fight, so it stops being a build decision and becomes "grab the best boss relic". My opinion is that it should land no later than about room 7. Proposal only.
8. **Spite Knuckles counter restarts each room.** I cannot feel it from frames. By the numbers, a fight that ends on hit 3 throws that progress away, and nothing on the chip shows the count, so the reset is invisible and surprising. For a relic about light attacks I would carry the count across rooms, or show "2/4" on the chip.
9. **"After Move" without a dodge note.** (`bd2pt-pyre-0.png`) Drive-By has a small "After Move" label top-left of the slot, the smallest text on the slot (about 9 px). It says the card is conditional, but not that Space does not count. I would still press Space and wonder why Drive-By stays dim. 1024x600 not captured. Suggestion: "After Move (Ctrl)", which names the key and implies Space is out.
10. **BD1 readability fixes.**
    - **Not verified:** the primed marker size and the USED flash at 1024x600. I did not capture them this pass.
    - **Verified:** the Keystone chip on Drive-By is now a gold, starred "Keystone + Payoff" pill, louder than the Area and Burn chips and merged into one chip. That fixes my BD1 complaint.
    - **Not seen:** a dim "Needs Burn"-style line on an unlit slot. No slot I captured showed one.

## Also seen
- **Wizard relic room** (`bd2pt-wizard-room.png`): the offer includes Spite Knuckles, tagged "Rogue" in its pool line. This was a forced offer (`--relic-offer`), so a normal Wizard run may never see it. I did not check. If it can, that is a dead pick.
- **Relic row** (`bd2pt-rogue-combo.png`): three small squares "PI SK HS" under the dodge pips. Two-letter initials are hard to tell apart, and the tooltip only shows on flash.
- **Tone:** "It was already on fire. You just wanted to make a point." and "Nothing personal. Entirely personal." land well and sit under the rule text, so they do not get in the way.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **Offer screen.** Clear relic identity, free Skip, rule text plus flavour, and a "Next:" line.
2. **Give-one-up screen.** Shows what you take, what you lose, with full text, and a visible Cancel.
3. **Flavour lines.** Short, on tone, never hiding the rule.

## Top issues, ranked
1. **A relic firing is hard to attribute** (ux, gameplay-dev). The chip flash is far from the action and the blast looks like a card's Area. Add a floating label or ring at the trigger point, rate-limited.
2. **Give-one-up defaults to a held relic, not Cancel; "You hold 1 relics"** (ux). Default focus to Cancel and fix the plural.
3. **Pause relic list shows 2 of 3** (ux). Make it taller or paged. The cap label says 20 against the roadmap's 10 (game-lead question).
4. **Relic row is initials only, and Spite Knuckles shows no counter** (ux). Use icons or colour, and show the count.
5. **Late relic room, and the per-room Spite counter reset** (game-lead). Room 9 gives one fight of use. The reset throws progress away silently.

## Questions for the director
1. Should the relic room land earlier (about room 7 at the latest) so a relic matters for more than one fight?
2. Should Spite Knuckles' counter carry across rooms (or at least be shown)?
3. The pause list says "of 20 held" while the roadmap says hard cap 10. Which is intended?
4. Should Cancel be the default focus on the give-one-up screen?

## Suggested next playtest focus
A human run per class with Pyre Insurance and Heavy Sigh against a pack: does the flash read in a real fight, and does the row get noisy? Then the primed marker and USED flash at 1024x600, and the dim "Needs Burn" line on a card that is really unlit. Both are still unverified.
