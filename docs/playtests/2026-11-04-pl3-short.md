## Answers to the director's questions
1. **Is Ward visibly "on"?** Yes. `pl3pt-ward.png` (0.8 s after cast, so past the old 0.5 s ring): a translucent blue shell around the Wizard plus a blue ring on the floor. It reads as a shield. Not verified: that it disappears when the buff ends, and how it looks at the edge of the screen in a busy fight.
2. **Do Three Bolts and Overqualified Bolt differ from the auto bolt, and the latter read as heavy?** Partly. Auto bolt is a pink blob with a pink trail (`pl3pt-ward.png`). Three Bolts is a lime-green stream with a cyan ring-trail (`pl3pt-bolts.png`). Overqualified Bolt is cyan-teal with a cyan impact ring (`pl3pt-heavy.png`). All three are clearly different in colour. Heavy does not look heavy in a still: the teal bolt is no bigger than the others, and the trail is thinner than the green one. Colour says "different", size does not say "big hit". The "12" damage number vs "6" does the work.
3. **Do the green safe-gap strips help? Too thin at 8 m (0.41 m)?** They help a lot. `pl3pt-volley.png` shows four green strips between the five pink lanes and it is instantly obvious where to stand; this fixes H3's problem 3. At the player's position the nearest gap is usable on screen but narrower than the player capsule, so standing in it will need precision or a dodge; at 0.41 m I would say they are "readable but not forgiving". The strips are widest near the boss and pinch toward the far end, so the best spot is close to the boss. I could not time a human reaction.
4. **Does the returning dodge pip flash catch the eye?** Not verified. In my scripted dodge frames the pip row after the dodge shows the label "Space" with no pip (the EMPTY state reads, in a way, but only because the pip is gone). I did not catch the return frame, so I cannot say if the flash is visible. It is still top-left, far from the action.
5. **Are reward and removal screens clear without number keys (key caps, "In slot:")?** Not verified. I did not capture a reward or removal screen this pass (no scenario in my list). qa's tests cover the reward keys. Next pass.
6. **Does knockback feel too soft after the fix?** Not verified by feel. I could not trigger a boss smash in capture. Bot data: smash thrown 2 times on Wizard runs and 0 hits, 9 thrown and 0 hits on Rogue, so the bot never took one and I have no number for the shove. Needs a human on the boss smash.
7. **Is the Wizard card strip readable?** Yes. Names fit even on two lines ("Three Bolts, Reluctantly", "Nope, Leaping Away"), the "Next:" line wraps to two lines without clipping, and cooling slots dim and fill. It is dense: it is a lot of text at the bottom of the screen, but nothing clips.

## Runs per class (bot, seed 2-4)
- **Rogue:** 1 won, 2 lost (room 2 and room 8). Winning run boss: 0 HP taken, lowest 27 of 30 over 77 s.
- **Wizard:** 2 won, 1 lost (room 4). Boss: volley is 81% of boss damage (21.7 HP per boss visit against 22 max), 9 of 85 pellets hit, 8 of 17 volleys landed. Charge took the other 19%.
- Both are better than H3 for the Wizard (1 of 3 then, 2 of 3 now). The bot's "inside a lane at fire time 6 of 17" against "hit 8 of 17" is closer than H3's 4 vs 13, which suggests the lane versus hit mismatch is smaller. Still unconfirmed.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **Safe-gap strips on the volley.** The boss attack now tells you where to go, not only where not to.
2. **Ward's shield shell and ring.** A buff you can see on the character.
3. **Card bolts are colour-coded** (pink auto, green three-bolt, teal heavy), and the strip text never clips.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **The Wizard's boss fight is still mostly one attack.** Volley is 81% of boss damage, about 22 HP per visit against 22 max HP. Wins happen, but narrowly. Suggestion (gameplay-dev, director's call on numbers): volley damage or pellet count for the Wizard, or a larger gap on the Wizard's side.
2. **Overqualified Bolt does not look heavy.** Different colour, same size. Suggestion (ux): larger scale and a fatter trail or a thin muzzle flash through `look` params.
3. **Dodge pip is unproven.** Small, top-left, and I could not see the return flash. Suggestion (ux, tooling): a capture-time scenario that presses dodge and snaps at the return moment; consider a flash on the character too.
4. **Gap strips are narrow far from the boss.** Narrower than the player capsule on screen at range. Suggestion (ux): a slightly wider gap tint, or the bar text "stand between lanes, or dodge".
5. **Reward screen, removal screen and boss smash knockback are not captured.** Suggestion (gameplay-dev, tooling): scenarios `reward_screen`, `removal_screen` and `boss_smash`, so the next playtest can answer questions 5 and 6.
