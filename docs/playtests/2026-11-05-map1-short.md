# Playtest: MAP1, the branching map (short)

**Blocker: NO.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at 6d45bb2, Godot 4.7.2, Windows, real renderer (RTX 3080).
- **Did:**
  - Smoke launches.
  - Real-renderer captures of all five `map_*` presets at 1024x600 and 1280x720.
  - A scripted Wizard launch: map, then a reflexive `ui_accept`, then the room. (A Rogue launch ran the same script; I only looked at the Wizard frames.)
  - 48 bot runs: `wave_sim -Run -Seed 100 -Runs 8`, each class on the `first`, `relic` and `safe` routes (seeds 100-107; different from qa's seeds).
  - A read of the earlier ux captures (`ux-map1-*`: relic cue, pips, pause map).
- **Could not do:**
  - Play by hand, or click and arrow around the map. Frames are single images, and scripted input only taps named actions.
  - Watch the relic label animate, see the Spite pips tick, or see the HUD "+N" appear live.
  - Reach the boss through real pick-by-pick input. "One run per class to the boss" is covered only by bot runs, which pick routes by policy and never see the screens. Anything about route feel is opinion from frames plus data.
- Frames: `docs/playtests/img/map1pt-*.png` (map_start, map_mid, map_room9, map_chest_large, map_chest_medium at both sizes, plus `map1pt-flow-wizard-room1.png`). Raw sim output was in the gitignored `screenshots/pt_sims.txt`. Only the first four seeds per cell were in my filtered output, so per-seed route claims below cover seeds 104-107.

## Smoke check: PASS
- Headless `--quit` exits 0. All captures and the 48 sim runs had **0 ERROR or WARNING lines**.
- The run opens on the map ("Choose your path", 0 gold, room-1 nodes ringed). A reflexive Enter at t=4 s started room 1: floor, player, enemies and camera are fine, Wizard HP 22 (the Rogue preset frames show 30/30), slots Q E F Ctrl R show names, and the gold HUD sits under the room plate.
- After a cleared room the map appears with "+15 gold" next to the total (`map1pt-map_mid-*`).
- The unattended Wizard died in room 1 (blind, no input). Expected, not a finding.

## Answers to the seven questions
1. **Is a route choice a decision? Sometimes. Not at the start, and "safe" is often not a real option.**
   - **Room 1:** both start nodes are plain Combat (`map_start`). Either pick plays the same, so the first choice is cosmetic.
   - **Shape:** 40% branching does not look too linear. The mid-run frame shows 4 rows and real forks (elite or fight, chest or fight, rest or fight at rooms 3, 5, 7, 8, 9).
   - **Safe route (seeds 104-107):** seed 104's safe route is the same path as `first` (2 elites, 0 rests, 10 fights). Seed 106's has 9 fights, 0 elites, 0 rests. Only seeds 105 and 107 had a rest at all (2 and 1). The bot's `safe` route lost 1 of 8 (Rogue) and 2 of 8 (Wizard) against 0 of 8 and 1 of 8 on `first`. That is a small sample, but "safe" did not play safer.
   - **Verdict:** the number of forks is fine for a first pass. The weakness is that the options often do not differ in risk or reward (issue 2).
2. **Readable at a glance: yes, with caveats.**
   - **Shapes:** circle, diamond, hexagon, plus and star are distinct at 1024x600. The taken path is a thick peach line, the current node has a double ring, reachable nodes have dashed white rings with a label, and later nodes are dim. In `map_mid` I found "where am I" and "what can I pick" at once.
   - **Chest sizes:** carried by hexagon size and 1/2/3 dots. At 1024x600 the small chest's single dot is tiny. The legend explains it.
   - **Ring colours:** the legend's "Gold ring: you are here" is a peach ring close to the path colour, and "gold" also means the coin. It still reads.
   - **Elite reward:** the map does not show that an elite gives a relic. I could not hover to check the hover text.
   - **Fit:** nothing clips at 1024x600. The legend text is about 11 px there: usable, but small.
3. **Default focus: right.** It lands on a plain Combat node (top node at room 1, a Combat at room 8 in `map_mid`, the lone boss at room 9). A reflexive Enter never takes an elite or a chest by accident. At room 9 there is only the boss, so that screen is a "press Enter" beat. Pause map: Enter, Esc or Back all close it, which is harmless.
4. **Gold: it reads, but it is a number with no use.**
   - **Where it shows:** a coin and total on the HUD, and top right on the map with a "+N" for the last gain (I saw "+15" and "+13"). I did not see the HUD "+N" live.
   - **Not confusing, but dead:** nothing says it will be spendable. A player will wonder what it is for.
   - **Amounts:** the bots ended with 83 to 169 gold by the boss. MAP2's prices should be set against that spread.
5. **Length and difficulty: about the same as before, and still short.**
   - **Win rates (8 runs per cell):**

     | Class | `first` | `relic` | `safe` |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | Rogue | 8 of 8 | 8 of 8 | 7 of 8 |
     | Wizard | 7 of 8 | 8 of 8 | 6 of 8 |

   - **Run length:** bot runs took 176 to 385 sim seconds for all 10 rooms, with boss fights of roughly 50 to 80 s. A human is slower, but this is far from the GDD's 30 to 60 minute target. It is not a MAP1 regression (the no-map baseline is similar), but it is a director-level pacing question.
   - **Wizard and elites:** the Wizard's lowest-HP room dipped to 5.8 to 7 HP on routes with 1 to 2 elites. But the `relic` route (about 2.9 elites, 5 relics held against 2 on the others) won 8 of 8 for both classes. In this sample, elites paid for themselves through relics. The Wizard losses came on `first` and `safe`, which hold fewer relics. I would not nerf elites on this evidence.
   - **Rests:** 3 of the 4 `first` routes I saw (seeds 104-107, Rogue) had no rest. A hurt player may find no rest on any reachable path.
6. **Relic label and Spite pips: label good, pips readable but not seen ticking.**
   - **Label:** `ux-map1-relic-cue-1024x600.png` shows an orange "Pyre Insurance" tag at the blast, a ring, and the HUD chip flashing in the same frame. That fixes my BD2 complaint: I can tell which relic fired without looking top-left. With several relics firing together I cannot judge overlap from a frame.
   - **Pips:** in `ux-map1-relic-pips-1024x600.png` the "SK" chip has small ticks under it. They are visible but tiny, and I did not see them change.
   - **Chips:** bigger, with distinct border colours (tan, orange, grey). They are still two-letter initials.
   - **"After Move (Ctrl)":** sits above the Drive-By slot and does what I asked in BD2.
7. **Chest sizes: different on the map, the same in the room.**
   - **Map:** small, medium and large are clearly different hexagons.
   - **Room:** the offer is titled "Relic room" for all three (`map_chest_large`, `map_chest_medium`). Nothing says "large chest", so the size you walked to has no visible payoff.
   - **Offers I saw:** large (seed 22): Tier 2, Tier 2, Tier 1. Medium (seed 30): Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 1. Small: not seen. Without tier 3 relics, medium and large are nearly the same, as game-lead said.
   - **Weight:** a large chest at 15% is a surprise rather than a goal worth routing toward.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The map screen.** It reads at a glance at both sizes: bright taken path, ringed reachable nodes, clear legend and gold. It is the cleanest new screen so far.
2. **Safe default focus.** A reflexive Enter lands on a plain fight, so the map cannot punish a player mashing through.
3. **Relic floating label.** My top BD2 complaint is addressed: label and ring appear at the trigger point, and the chips are bigger and distinct.

## Top issues, ranked
1. **Chest size has no visible payoff** (design, ux). The map promises small, medium or large, but the offer screen is the same "Relic room" and the odds barely differ. Suggestion: name the size in the offer title, and until tier 3 exists make a large chest give an extra option or pick.
2. **Many route choices are empty** (design). The two room-1 nodes are identical, some seeds give "safe" and "first" the same path, and many routes have no rest. Suggestion: make room 1's two nodes differ (or make room 1 a single forced start), keep a rest reachable from about room 4 on, and make the safe route actually safer.
3. **Gold is shown with no use** (director call, ux). Suggestion: a one-line "for the merchant, coming" note, or hide it until MAP2.
4. **A forced "Choose your path" at room 9** (ux). The only option is the boss. Suggestion: skip it, or show a short "Boss next. Enter" prompt.
5. **Small legend text and a tiny single dot at 1024x600** (ux). Suggestion: larger legend text, and S/M/L labels or bigger dots.

## Possible code or text bugs (not diagnosed)
- The relic offer's Skip card reads "Take nothing. It is free and leaves your card rejects alone." That sentence looks garbled.
- I could not verify the HUD "+N" live. `map_start` shows 0 gold, so there is no "+N" in that frame.

## Questions for the director
1. Should room 1's two start nodes always differ, so the first pick is a real choice?
2. Should the chest size show on the offer screen and carry a real payoff before tier 3 relics exist?
3. Should gold be hidden until MAP2, or shown with a "coming" note?
4. Bot runs are about 3 to 6.5 minutes against a 30 to 60 minute target. Is that a concern for the next slice, or fine for now?
5. Should a rest be guaranteed on every route, or is a rest-free route a deliberate risk?

## Suggested next playtest focus
A human run per class on a relic route and a "safe" route, with the mouse. I want to see whether an elite diamond next to a chest changes my pick, whether the hover text sells the choice, and how the HUD "+N" looks live. Then re-check the Spite Knuckles pips in a real fight.
