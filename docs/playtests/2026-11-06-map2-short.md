# Playtest: MAP2, the merchant (short)

**Blocker: NO.**

## Build and setup
- `main` at 46065c1, Godot 4.7.2, Windows, real renderer (RTX 3080). The merchant is on in shipped data.
- **Did:**
  - Real-renderer captures of `merchant_shop`, `merchant_shop_poor`, `merchant_removal`, `map_start` and `map_mid` at 1024x600 and 1280x720.
  - A read of ux's captures for the swap screen (`shop_giveup`) and the free reroll (`shop_reroll`).
  - Bot runs, `wave_sim -Run -Seed 200 -Runs 8` (seeds differ from qa's seed 1): Rogue on `relic`, `safe` and `first`; Wizard on `relic` and `safe`. Wizard `first` had not finished when I stopped waiting.
  - A read of the new relics' data and rule text.
- **Could not do:**
  - Play by hand, press Enter in the shop, or watch a purchase happen. Frames are single images and scripted input only taps named actions. How buying feels (confirmation, feedback) is not observed.
  - See a new relic fire in a live fight. I read their rule text and qa's trigger counts instead.
  - Reach a merchant through real map clicks. Bots reached it (8 of 8 relic runs), but they never see the screens.
- Frames are in the gitignored `screenshots/pt/` (not committed, per "commit only this path"). Sim output is in `screenshots/pt_sim_*.txt`.

## Smoke check: PASS
- All 10 captures exited 0 and saved a PNG. The 5 finished sim cells (40 runs) had **0 ERROR or WARNING lines** and ended "WAVE SIM OK".
- The map shows ("Choose your path", gold top right, "+15 gold", a square in the legend). The merchant panel shows 3 relics on the left, 3 cards on the right, "Remove a card", Leave, and the gold total twice (panel and HUD). HP 30/30. Nothing clips at 1024x600 or 1280x720.
- I did not hand-drive a normal windowed run to a merchant. The merchant is covered by presets and bots.

## Answers to the questions
1. **Is a relic route a real alternative to a card route? As a route, yes. As a feel, only half.**
   - **Observed (8 runs per cell):**

     | Class | Route | Won | Median relics | Merchant visited |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | Rogue | relic | 7 of 8 | 6 | 8 of 8, 2.9 relics bought, 96 gold spent |
     | Rogue | first | 8 of 8 | 3 | 5 of 8 |
     | Rogue | safe | 8 of 8 | 1 | 5 of 8, removal or card |
     | Wizard | relic | 7 of 8 | 6 | 8 of 8, 2.9 relics bought |
     | Wizard | safe | 7 of 8 | 1 | not read |

   - **What that says:** the relic route reaches 6 relics (the target) through about 2.4 elites plus the merchant. It does not win more than the safe route in this sample (one loss each class, small numbers). It is a different shape (elites and a merchant versus rests and combats), and the deck stays small because it buys no cards and no removals (0.00 each).
   - **Opinion:** a real alternative, but what it buys is mostly cheap tier 1 stat bumps, and the lower-risk route is not clearly worse. The pull to build around relics is weak.
2. **Is the merchant worth the detour? Mostly yes, with two holes.**
   - **No merchant on about half the routes:** `first` and `safe` routes reached it in 5 of 8 runs. In the other 3, gold has no use. The map line "Spend your gold at the merchant (the square)" is true, but a player can reach room 10 holding 100 gold.
   - **Late placement:** in the capture seed the merchant is room 9, one step before the boss, so a relic bought there gets one fight to matter. I cannot say how common that is: the sim prints a position histogram for elites, rests and relic rooms, not the merchant.
3. **Prices: readable yes, fair so-so.**
   - **Readable:** the gold pill sits in the same spot on every row. A dead row says "Need 10 more gold" in orange and the pill goes hollow. In `merchant_shop_poor` (20 gold) I could tell why each row was dead.
   - **A 30 gold relic against a 40 gold card does feel odd.** A permanent passive costs less than a card you may rarely cast.
   - **Cheap overall:** relic-route bots spend about 96 gold on 3 relics and finish with about 37 gold. Nothing makes me choose between two things I want, because most of the shop is affordable.
4. **Rests: not too safe, but I cannot tell about card offers.**
   - Rests per run were about 1.1 (relic) to 1.4 (safe). Mean HP at room end stays 85 to 95% on the relic route. Combat rooms (the card-offer rooms) still number about 6 to 7 per run, so card offers did not obviously shrink.
   - HP that high suggests the bots rarely need the rests. That is more a difficulty question than a rest-count one.
5. **Readable at 1024x600 and 1280x720? Yes.** Everything fits. "Need N more gold" is clear. "No card can go: deck minimums" (removal) is correct but vague: it does not say what the minimum is, and a new player sees it on their first visit.
6. **Do the new relics read clearly when they fire? I can judge only the text.**
   - Rule text is plain and short. Flavor lines ("The curve is on fire", "Please advise") fit the tone and stay out of the way.
   - Paperwork Fire (every 5th card hit) fired 0 to 3 times in qa's bot runs, so a player may never notice it. I do not know whether the MAP1 floating label fires for the new relics.
   - ASAP Notice and Liquidate Assets are quiet stats. No cue is fine for a stat, but Liquidate Assets at 45 gold feels invisible.
7. **Does the legend's merchant square need to match the coin node? Yes, a little.** The node is a square with a coin, the legend shows a plain orange square. Trivial to fix, and the legend is the only explanation.
8. **Does a run last about as long? About the same, and still short.** Bot boss fights are 52 to 65 s. The merchant adds one screen with no combat. The 30 to 60 minute GDD target is still far off (carried from MAP1).

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The shop panel.** Two clear columns, a price pill on every row, a hollow pill plus "Need N more gold" on dead rows, and a "Too many relics" swap screen that says "You pay only when you give one up. Cancel charges nothing." I never had to guess what a click would do.
2. **The "Only sold here" tag.** It explains why an unfamiliar relic is in the third slot and makes it a reason to visit.
3. **The map's gold line and merchant node.** "Spend your gold at the merchant (the square)" answers my MAP1 complaint (gold with no use). The room 8 pick (combat or rest) in `map_mid` reads in one look.

## Top issues, ranked
1. **The shop opens with focus on a purchase, so a reflexive Enter may buy something** (ux; I could not verify whether there is a confirm). In `merchant_shop` the white focus box is on ASAP Notice. In MAP1 I praised the map for defaulting to a safe node. Suggestion: default focus on Leave, or add a confirm.
2. **Gold is dead on routes that skip the merchant, and the merchant can land late** (design). About 3 of 8 non-relic runs never see it, and in the capture seed it is room 9. Suggestion: a merchant guarantee in a mid window like D40's rest, or show the odds on the map.
3. **Prices make the choice too easy** (design, D41). With 30 and 45 gold nothing hurts. Suggestion: the director chooses between the cheap table and something nearer 45/65/90, knowing qa's relic route (median 6, "at the floor") would drop. This also fixes the odd 30 against 40 ordering.
4. **Request for Quotation is in about 79% of shops, and Liquidate Assets is weak** (game-lead; qa's carry-over 2). The free reroll shows so often it stops being special. Liquidate Assets gives 1% damage per 50 gold spent, so even a big shop gives a few percent and the player cannot see the current value. Suggestion: weight shop-only relics equally, and show "now +N%" or raise the rate.
5. **"No card can go: deck minimums" is unhelpful on the starter deck** (ux). Suggestion: "Your deck is at its minimum. Buy a card first." Minor: make the legend's merchant icon match the coin node.

## Possible code or text bugs (not diagnosed)
- I did not check qa's carry-over 7 (a free reroll can show the same unsold items). `shop_reroll_used` exists in ux's captures; I did not compare the before and after stock.
- Wizard `first` did not finish in my session, so it is not in the table.

## Questions for the director
1. **D41 (prices):** keep relics at 30/45/65, or move toward 45/65/90 for a real choice, accepting a relic route under target?
2. Should every route have a merchant (like D40's rest), or is a no-merchant route an acceptable risk?
3. Should the shop's default focus be Leave, so Enter can never spend gold by accident?
4. Should shop-only relics weigh equally, so Request for Quotation does not dominate every shop?
5. Bot runs are 3 to 6.5 minutes against a 30 to 60 minute target: when does that get addressed?
