## Where each brief item landed

1. **`rogue-bleed-out`: stacks, detonate, labels (observed in frames, scenario captures).**
   - The detonate reads well. On the two chasers the "Bleed x5 MAX" labels (pink, above the HP bar) turn into a big white "31!" and a pink "Bleed detonated". It is clearly bigger than the "10" hits before it.
   - On the boss the same: "Bleed x5 MAX", then "[16]!" and "Bleed detonated". The `[ ]` marks armor.
   - **But stacks are not there by default.** Bleed's base max is 1 (`data/statuses.json` has no stacking, D25). In a live room-1 capture with the deck, I saw no "Bleed xN" label at all. Every stacking frame I saw used the relic Serrated Excuse (max 5). Without it, the stack comes from Bad Influence's 6 s buff (card F, +4 max stacks). A player who draws Open a Vein before Bad Influence is up cashes in almost nothing, and nothing on the card says why.
   - In the live capture the cone, the aim dots and the player's facing nub all line up, but the chasers swarmed too fast to see a stack build. I could not confirm "can you build stacks" in real play. See problem 2.
2. **`rogue-grudge-brawler`: a plan? Not judged.** See problem 3. The deck is nearly the standard starter, and its plan lives in four relics that `--deck` does not give you.
3. **A Fire Rogue going physical.** From the offer report (300 seeds, shipped settings), see problem 1. The standard starter has **0 Bleed cards and 4 Fire cards of 15**. Pruned cards stay gone by construction (the report plays D47 = B). I did not watch it happen by hand.
4. **Act screen and mini-boss bar (observed, real wiring).**
   - Run with `--run-clear-after=7`, so the standing player was hurt first. The real act screen showed red "Low HP: HP 10 of 30, 92 gold, relics: 0", then "You recover 9 HP. Rests are on the map.", then the flavor line. HUD behind it read 1/30 before the heal. This is the wiring ux said was unverified: it works.
   - Mini-boss: a named bar "Act 1 Mini-boss", the armor line, and a bigger orange triangle over a taller grey golem. This reads as a boss now. It fixes the MAP3 complaint that the mini-boss looked like an ordinary enemy.
5. **Wizard, acts 2-3 (sim only).** 4 of 6 won on seed 300 (2 lost: rooms 14 and 24). Consistent with qa's 29/36. **The MAP3 carry-over is answered:** the Wizard `-Run` finished (about 10 minutes of wall time with three sims running at once). It is slow, not stuck.
6. **Scenario aim.** In the scenario frames the body's facing nub points at the aim ring and the cone goes where the aim goes. The sim bot's aim fix shows.

## Difficulty check: is a three-act Fire Rogue run clearly harder than at `playtest-2026-11-09-MAP3`?
**No. By data it is not harder, and it is slightly easier.** I cannot give a human number.
- `git diff --stat playtest-2026-11-09-MAP3 HEAD` over `data/enemies`, `waves.json`, `bosses.json`, `player.json`, `attacks.json`, `statuses.json`, `characters.json` and `armor.json` is **empty**. Enemy HP, damage, waves, boss attacks, player HP and the starting deck are unchanged.
- What changed: `run.json` adds `acts.start_heal` 0.3 (**easier**: +30% of max HP at the start of acts 2 and 3; 9 HP of 30 in my frame), the new mini-boss display name and marker, and the status-lean offer weighting. The new Bleed and basic-attack cards and relics only add options.
- Spite Knuckles went from Burn to Bleed. That is a small change for a Fire deck.
- The sim cannot split the two builds. The fixed bot wins about 35/36 on every build, and my 6-run samples were 6/6 for both new decks (boss in about 20 s, against about 30 s standard). The sim says nothing about human difficulty (DECISIONS says the same).
- **What I would need:** one hand-played three-act run by the director at normal skill, with a note of where HP got low. If it felt like MAP3's "felt good mostly", the answer stays no.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The detonate hit reads.** A bigger "!" number, a burst, the pink "Bleed detonated" line. It is clearly different from a normal hit, on small enemies and on the boss.
2. **The act screen tells the truth.** "Low HP" in red, an honest heal line, then the weary flavor line. It answers the MAP3 complaint that no-free-heal was invisible.
3. **The mini-boss is an event now.** Its own name, its own bar, a bigger marker.

## Top 5 problems, ranked

1. **A Fire Rogue still gets few Bleed cards, and Fire keeps showing up (design, high; offer report, not hand-played).**
   - **Observed:** the whole game has **6 Bleed cards** and the standard starter has none. For a bot that picks Bleed and prunes Burn (shipped settings): Bleed share of offers goes 17.7% early, 12.9% mid, 8.5% late. **Fire share goes 29.2%, 32.4%, 31.8%: it does not fall.** The bot ends with 4.8 Bleed cards picked. A neutral bot sees Bleed at about 18% and Fire at about 30% throughout.
   - **The lean barely helps.** Lean 0.25 against 0: Bleed 15.5 -> 17.7% early, 10.2 -> 12.9% mid, 8.5% -> 8.5% late.
   - **Why it matters:** in player terms, a Bleed player's late offers hold roughly four Fire cards for each Bleed card, and the Bleed ones are the 1-2 they already own. This softens the director's complaint ("kept rejecting Fire offers", 3 rejects per run) but does not fix it. D47 helps pruned cards (the "bleed-old" row, the pre-D47 rule, shows Fire 39.4% late), not Fire cards the player never took.
   - **Suggestion:** more Bleed and physical cards (MAP3b can carry them). Consider a lean that also pushes *down* a card whose status the deck has pruned. Or let a reject or a prune remove a whole type from offers for the run.
2. **The stack and detonate fantasy is gated behind a relic or one 6 s buff (design/clarity, high).**
   - **Observed:** base Bleed max stacks is 1. Open a Vein shows only "Keystone + Payoff" (no Bleed chip). I saw no "x" label in a live room.
   - **Why it matters:** a new player who plays the deck from room 1 meets "Cashes in every little cut" and cuts that do not stack. The best moment of this archetype, the 31! frame, needs Serrated Excuse or perfect timing.
   - **Suggestion:** a "LIVE" or "needs stacks" cue on Open a Vein (the glow system exists for conditions). Check that Bad Influence's buff is visible on the slot while it runs, and consider giving the deck a second stack source.
3. **Grudge Brawler is not a physical deck (design, medium-high).**
   - **Observed:** the preset holds 2 Fire cards (`fire_ember_whatever`, `fire_fine_burn_then`), is nearly the standard starter, and its description says it is "only viable with its relics". One of those is **Heavy Sigh, which is Burn-dependent** (it explodes on a heavy hit against a *Burning* enemy). `--deck` gives no relics.
   - **Why it matters:** this is the archetype for the light/heavy player and it still leans on Burn, the thing the director felt trapped by. Launched as listed, it plays like the starter. A bot wins 6/6 with it, which proves little.
   - **Suggestion:** give the preset its relics in the debug flow, and check whether the relics for this plan can come without Burn (a Bleed or no-status heavy payoff in place of Heavy Sigh).
4. **Stack labels and damage numbers collide (clarity, medium; observed).**
   - Two chasers close together: the two "Bleed x5 MAX" labels overlap ("Bleed x5 MAX" printed over another).
   - Boss: "Bleed detonated" prints right over the "[16]!" number, so the number is hard to read.
   - Elites: the label sits on the diamond marker and shield pips (ux said this was unverified).
   - **Suggestion:** offset or fade the label when a damage number is up; stagger labels by enemy.
5. **Mini-boss bar relabels itself under the act screen (probable bug, low; observed).** Behind the Act 2 screen the bar reads "Act **2** Mini-boss" while the plate still says "Act 1 of 3 / Room 10 of 30 - Mini-boss". During the fight it said "Act 1 Mini-boss". Probably the act number moves before the screen. Owner: ux.
