## Smoke check: PASS

## Where am I? (observed, from frames)
The three-act structure reads well:
- The HUD plate says "Act 3 of 3 / Room 30 of 30 - Boss" and "Act 1 of 3 / Room 10 of 30 - Mini-boss". I can tell at a glance where I am and how far there is to go.
- Map titles say "Face the mini-boss - Act 1 of 3" and "Choose your path - Act 2 of 3". Columns are numbered 11-20. The hint line says "Every route ends at the mini-boss".
- The orange triangle is clear. Its hover text ("ends the act. A tough fight, a relic, 90-110 gold") tells me what I get before I commit.
- The final-boss frame (Overly Enthusiastic Guardian, phases, "Ground pound" wind-up bar) is readable.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The map as orientation.** Act title, numbered columns, "Nothing else is left. Pick the mini-boss when you are ready", and the reward promise on the node all tell a new player what to do next.
2. **The Act 2 screen has the right tone and size.** "Act 2 / HP 30 of 30, 92 gold, relics: 0 / Another act. Same hero, same reluctance." fits the weary-hero voice. One button, Enter works.
3. **Acts get longer in a believable way.** Bot act times were 246 / 288 / 251 s. No act is a slog compared with the first.

## Top 5 problems, ranked

1. **Acts 2 and 3 feel like "act 1 again, with more", and the mini-boss does not look like an act-ender (design, high).**
   - **Observed:**
     - `mini_boss_act1` is one golem (scale 1.2, HP x3.5) with two ordinary red chasers. In the frame it is a grey capsule a little taller than the others, with a small orange triangle and a normal enemy HP bar.
     - Act 2 and 3 scaling is HP x1.1 / x1.2 and damage x1.0. Max HP stays 30 in every frame.
     - Bots clear every act at 100% HP on median (sim: 30.0 at the end of all three acts).
   - **Why it matters:** the pillars are "no two runs alike" and a sense of escalation. A player who beats the act 1 mini-boss walks into act 2 with the same screen, the same enemies and a 10 percent bigger health bar. The act 2 and 3 lists add golems and shooters, which helps, but nothing says "the world just changed".
   - **Suggestion (proposal only):**
     - Give each act one visible change: a floor or light tint, a new enemy, or a mini-boss attack. The brief said "no new AI", so this is for a later slice.
     - Make the mini-boss visibly bigger and give it a named boss bar like the final boss.
     - Consider a small damage bump in acts 2 and 3. At x1.0 the same hits hurt the same, so "harder" is only "longer".

2. **No free heal between acts is invisible and untested (design/ux, medium).**
   - **Observed:** the act screen shows "HP 30 of 30" but never says the next act starts at that HP. Bots always arrive at full HP, so the rule has never been stressed. Rests heal 30% and appear on the map.
   - **Why it matters:** a human who just killed a mini-boss at 8 of 30 HP gets no comfort and no warning. That case decides whether the rule feels fair, and nobody has tested it.
   - **Suggestion:**
     - Colour the HP red on the act screen when it is low.
     - Add a line like "No free heal. Rests are on the map."
     - Run one low-HP test at an act boundary before locking the rule.

3. **Gold has no job in acts 2-3 (design, medium).**
   - **Observed (Rogue, first route):** median gold 259 at the end of act 1, 629 at act 2, 960 at act 3. Relics at act end: 3, 6.5, 8.
   - **Why it matters:** the shop frame shows relics at 80 / 80 / 120 and cards at 40. A player who meets one merchant per act cannot spend 960 gold, so gold turns into a score. The mini-boss's "90-110 gold" reads the same way.
   - **Suggestion:** this is MAP3b's gold tuning. Ideas: prices that rise per purchase, a "heal for gold" at the merchant (which also helps problem 2), and one guaranteed merchant per act.

4. **Run length is probably below the 30-45 minute target (my estimate).**
   - **Observed:** won bot runs took a median 806 s of sim time (about 13.5 minutes) over three acts. Only 2 of 6 won on seed 300.
   - **My estimate:** a human adds map picks, reward screens, shops and cautious play. I would guess 20 to 30 minutes for a win. This is a guess, not an observation.
   - **Why it matters:** if the run is short and acts do not escalate (problem 1), it feels like a long first act.
   - **Suggestion:** use the director's clock time from his earlier run. If it was under 30 minutes, add content (a mini-boss phase, more rooms) rather than longer fights.

5. **Win rate swings between seeds (balance, low-medium).**
   - **Observed:** 2 of 6 wins on seed 300, against 9 of 12 on qa's seed 1. Of 6 runs, 5 cleared act 1, 4 cleared act 2 and 2 cleared act 3, all at full HP at each act's start.
   - **Why it matters:** half of act 3 starts losing is a spike. The player cannot see where the danger comes from until they are in it.
   - **Suggestion:** have qa or the sim log which room kills the bot in acts 2 and 3. If it is the elites, a map hint ("Elite: dangerous") may be enough.

## Smaller notes

- **Observed:** `merchant_shop_poor` is a one-act preset ("Room 9 of 10"). "Remove a card - 50 gold. No card can go. Add a card first." now gives a next step. I did not see the 170 relic price.
