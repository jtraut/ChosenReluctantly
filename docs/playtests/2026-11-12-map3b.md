## Build and setup
HEAD f95c15c (MAP3b qa PASS). Godot 4.7.2, windowed Vulkan renderer. **I cannot play with live keyboard and mouse here.** What I did: scripted launches and frame captures through `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1` (`--run-room=10/20/24`, `--run-clear-after`, `--run-auto-choose`, `--deck`), `tools/UxCapture.tscn`, the `bd3a_stacks` scenario, `run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run` (bot) and `run_offer_report.ps1`. ## Smoke check: PASS, no blocker
- Launch exits clean, no console errors or warnings in the capture runs.
- Room 10: world visible (floor, blue player, yellow-cube mini-boss, lit and framed), HUD sane (HP 30/30, cards Q/E/F/Ctrl/R, boss bar with phase ticks, "Act 1 of 3, Room 10 of 30, Mini-boss", gold).
- Act 3 (room 24): sand floor, player, red chasers, orange golem and purple shooter all visible; the idle player dying to six chasers ends in the death screen as expected.
- Live input (move, aim, dodge, slots 1-5) was not exercised by hand (no tool for it).

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The boss-relic screen reads as a different tier.** Orange "Relic / Boss" header band on every tile, the title "Boss relic", and "Pick one. Each one has a real upside and a real cost. Read both." The cost is its own orange line starting "Cost -" under the upside. Three tiles, no Skip (`map3b_pt_smoke_1.png`, `map3b_pt_a2_wiz.png`).
2. **The mini-boss reward has a good two-beat rhythm**: boss relic, then straight into a rare (act 1) or legendary (act 2) card offer. The legendary frame (gold border, gold "Legendary" band, two diamonds) beside the rare (blue band, one diamond) is easy to tell apart at 1024x600 (`map3b_pt_a2_rogue_0.png`). The legendary tiles are exciting: Death by a Thousand Memos, Golden Handshake, Exsanguinate. "Everyone's accounts settled at once. Nobody was consulted." lands the tone.
3. **Merchant rarity bands and prices (55 / 85 / 125) are legible** at 1280x720 and 1024x600: chip, diamonds, gold frame, price pill (`map3b_pt_shop_1024x600.png`). Caveat: that capture mode fabricates the options (stand-in effect lines, empty left column), so it proves the tile, not a real shop.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **Boss relic costs are mostly not costs; this is probably why Non-Compete is 12/12 (theory from the screen text and the sims).** Non-Compete: "each card cast takes 0.5 s off every slot", cost "light and heavy attacks deal half damage". In a game where cards carry the build, auto attacks are what you stop caring about, so the cost is nearly free. The Micromanager (+25% damage, cannot swap a dead card) has the same shape. Corporate Buyout (all card cooldowns 40% longer) is a real cost, so it reads as a decision. For two of three tiles the pick is "which free power", not "which trade". Suggestion: **gameplay-dev** raise Non-Compete's cost (light and heavy deal 0, or lose a dodge charge) or cut the refund; **game-lead** decide whether every boss relic must cost something felt every room, since the screen promises it.
2. **Fire still crowds a Bleed Rogue's offers (director priority).** `run_offer_report.ps1`, Bleed-steering Rogue, lean 0.25: late Fire is **31.3% of card offers against Bleed 11.8%** (early 29.9% vs 17.9%); the Bleed player ends with about 5.9 Bleed cards. The tool omits the mini-boss's fixed rare and legendary offers (a good Bleed source), so real runs are somewhat better, but a Bleed build is still shown about one Fire card in three. Suggestion: **game-lead / director** raise the lean, or let a committed status deck down-weight the other element harder from act 2, or add Bleed commons and rares.
3. **The rare and legendary Bleed cards barely get cast in Bleed bot runs.** 4 runs, `-Run -Plan -Deck rogue-bleed-out -Route first`, all 4 won all three acts (about 470 sim-s each). Casts: Open a Vein 235, Paper Cuts 243, Everyone Calm Down 122, Cut Corners 104, but **Exsanguinate 7, Arterial Spray 19, Thousand Memos 12, Brawl Clause 0, Hemorrhage Clause 3**. The bot is not me and the plan policy may undervalue them, so I cannot confirm by hand; but if a human would also keep the commons, the "oh nice" payoff of rarity is missing for Bleed. Suggestion: **gameplay-dev** check how the plan bot values them; **game-lead** decide if they need a clearer upgrade over Open a Vein.
4. **Bleed stack labels overlap with only 2 enemies** (`map3b_pt_stacks_1.png`): two "Bleed x5 MAX" labels sit on top of each other with both damage numbers, so you read one smudge. The 3+ enemy dimming is meant for a worse case and I could not test it: no scenario has 3 or more Bleed targets and I may not edit `data/scenarios.json`. Suggestion: **ux** add a 4-enemy Bleed scenario; stagger label heights or show labels for the nearest 2 only.
5. **Act 3 sand lowers contrast for the orange golem and for telegraphs (mild).** Red chasers and the purple shooter read fine on tan. The pink telegraph beam is visible but muted on sand (`map3b_pt_a3_bleed_1.png`), and the orange golem sits near the floor hue. I did not catch a golem swing mid-wind-up, so this is partial. Suggestion: **ux** check golem wind-up and shooter line on sand, maybe a darker outline on the orange.

Also logged, not ranked: Knuckle Sandwich reads "Take 60 s off your Heavy attack cooldown", which looks like a unit mistake or a clumsy "reset it" (**gameplay-dev / ux**). Burnout Syndrome appeared in both boss offers I read that listed it (Rogue act 1, Wizard act 2); for a Rogue on Bleed it is a dead choice, and its cost (Recovery slot 50% longer) lands on a slot many decks barely lean on (**game-lead**).

## Answers to items 2-6
**2. Mini-boss reward (Rogue and Wizard, acts 1 and 2).** Boss relic: clearly a different tier. Upside and cost are both readable as separate lines. Burnout Syndrome reached and checked: "Burn ticks twice as fast and stacks to 5. Cost - Your Recovery slot takes 50% longer. Running hot. Running out." Reads well. The rare offer (act 1) and legendary offer (act 2) follow directly with the reject counter. Both classes worked, no errors. Combat against the mini-boss itself was not played by hand.
**3. Bleed Rogue into act 3.** 4 of 4 Bleed-plan runs and 4 of 4 generic relic-route runs won all three acts, with the bot at about full HP at each act end (a bot, not a human). Enough Bleed late? By share about 12% of late card offers are Bleed, with Fire at 31%, so a human has to work for it; Bleed relics (Serrated Excuse, Blood Oath, Salt in the Wound) showed up in nearly every Bleed run. Fire crowding: yes, still, see problem 2. Bot unspent gold was 628 median in the Bleed runs, so gold still has little to do.
**4. Visual items.** Open a Vein slot glow lit in play: **not verified**; the scenario casts it at 1.9 s and I could not tell the glow state from my frames. Stack-label dimming with 3+ enemies: **not verified** (no scenario). Act 3 telegraphs: partly readable, see problem 5. Act 3 merchant with a legendary: **not reached**; the tile render is fine.
**5. Balance feel (theory).** Non-Compete: not a trivial-run switch on its face, but it is the most free power on offer, and 12/12 for both classes agrees; see problem 1. Exsanguinate with stacked Bleed: qa measured up to 1332 against boss health of 120-195. That is absurd, and **fun-absurd only if it needs real setup** (10 stacks, several pieces); I would leave it, it is the build's payoff, but watch for a one-cast boss kill in a hands-on run. Mini-bosses: with Raider HP 120 and armor and resist lines (31% and 17%) they are no longer pushovers on paper, but bots lose none of rooms 10 or 20 (qa), and I did not fight them by hand, so "still soft?" stays open.
**6. Rarity bands.** Reward (1280x720 and 1024x600), legendary offer and merchant are readable. The rare's blue band is the weakest (thin, light blue on a dark tile). I did not capture the removal tile.

## Ratings (theory-weighted; no hands-on session)

- Clarity: 4/5. Cost lines are the win; stack labels overlap.
