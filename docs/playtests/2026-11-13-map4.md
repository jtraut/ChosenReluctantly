# Playtest: MAP4, events (2026-11-13)

## Build and setup
HEAD 3307c3c (MAP4 qa PASS), Godot 4.7.2, windowed Vulkan. **I cannot play with a live keyboard and mouse here.** What I did, so you can separate fact from theory:
- **Windowed frame captures** of the real game through `tools/capture_screenshot.ps1`: `--event=<id>` on a real map node, `--run-curses`, `--relics`, `--relic-offer`, `--run-auto-choose` (to reach the second screen of a choice), the scenarios `map4ux_bleed4` and `map4ux_sand_telegraphs`, and the map at seed 3. I read every frame myself.
- **Bot runs** (`run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -Plan -Route relic -Merchant -BotPreset human`, seeds 3-4, 2 runs per class, three full acts, plus `-StartRoom 20`, seeds 1-3, 4 runs per class). A bot, not a person: it picks events by a fixed policy and never took a curse in these runs.
- Anything about how a choice *feels* in hands is **theory from the screens and numbers, and labelled so**.
- Not covered: the real merchant discount screen (my `--merchant` capture opened the map instead), event E5 and E6 as rendered screens (I read their data only), hands-on combat against the Raider.

Screenshots (all `docs/playtests/img/`): `2026-11-13-map4-event_screen_requires.png`, `-vend.png`, `-sacrifice.png`, `-sacrifice_pick.png`, `-coworker_remove.png`, `-dig_pick.png`, `-curse_hud.png`, `-bleed4_0.png`, `-sand.png`.

## Smoke check: PASS, no blocker
- Every capture launched and exited clean. No ERROR or WARNING lines in any windowed run.
- The map shows teal "?" bubble nodes in a three-act run (seed 3 act 1 has three: rooms 3, 7, 7), with an "Event" legend entry. Room 4 of act 2 and room 7 also carry them.
- Event screens open and resolve: title, flavor line, 2-3 choices, cost and gain lines, "Enter to choose". Choices that resolve into a relic pick, a give-up step, a card removal and the Dig pick all opened.
- HUD sane (HP 30/30, five slots, gold, relic chips). World framed and lit in the room captures.
- Live movement, aim, dodge and slots 1-5 were not exercised by hand (no tool for it).
- Note, tool only: `run_wave_sim.ps1 ... -BossLog` ends with `SCRIPT ERROR: Invalid access to property or key 'volley' on a base object of type 'Dictionary'` at `tools/wave_sim.gd:1512` (`_boss_summary`), after the runs finish; the script then exits 1. Logged under "Code bugs".

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The event screen reads at a glance, before you click.** Red "- Lose 40 gold", green "+ Gain a tier 2 relic", yellow "! Fight an elite" with an indented "If you win:" block, a blue "? 50%" line. Greyed choices say why ("Unavailable: Needs a tier 1-3 relic you can give up"). The Vending Machine ("Insert coins" 50% vs "Shake it" sure thing with curse and -3 max HP) is a clean gamble-versus-price read (`-vend.png`, `-event_screen_requires.png`).
2. **The give-up step is honest and gentle.** "Hostile Takeover asks you to give up 2 of your relics. Picked 0 of 2. Nothing is lost until you confirm." plus a "Taking:" box and a disabled confirm that says "Pick 2 more" (`-sacrifice_pick.png`). Nothing about it feels like a trap.
3. **The relic-for-relic and relic-for-curse decisions are real.** Hostile Takeover (+35% damage for two relics), Golden Parachute, Asset Stripping, and the Supply Closet (tier 3 relic, no skip, random curse) each make me stop and think. The Dig at a rest (`-dig_pick.png`: replaces the heal and the removal, skip allowed) is a good new option, the best-explained of the lot ("This rest is spent either way").
Tone: the flavor lines land ("Nobody saw the invoice. Nobody will ask about the deductible." "Somebody buried it. Somebody should have asked first.").

## Answers to the brief

### 3a. Are the trade-offs clear before I click?
Mostly yes (observed on the screens). The costs are lines of their own, in a different colour from the gains, and numbers are concrete (6 HP, 40 gold, 8 HP). Two gaps:
- **A curse is named but never described at the choice.** "Gain the curse Property Damage" and "Gain a random curse" tell a new player nothing about what a curse is or does. It is only explained once it is in a slot or in the remove list ("Cooldown 3 s" with no effect text).
- **"Lose 3 max HP" is shown without the total.** 3 of 30 is 10%, which the player can do, but the other events use "% of HP" in the data and absolute numbers on screen, and that mix is easy to misread.

### 3b. Did any choice feel like a trap I couldn't have read?
- **The Loyalty Pledge (E8)** is not a trap, it is a **no-win toll**: "Pay the dues, lose 50 gold" or "Sign in blood, gain a random curse". Nothing positive on either side. It is readable, but landing on a "?" and getting only a bill feels bad, and the "?" node cannot be avoided once chosen. (Weight 0.5, so rare.)
- **Sign in blood is probably the right pick every time** (theory): see 3c, a curse costs about 1 s per rotation, 50 gold buys a merchant relic discount.
- **The Exit Interview "Demand a rematch"** starts a real elite fight at the cost of nothing but risk; I read that clearly ("! Fight an elite", then rewards). Not a trap.
- **The Internal Audit (E6) "Declare your best asset"** gives up your *highest-tier* relic for a one-merchant discount. I read its text only. The cost is large and the payoff only exists if the route has a merchant, and "at most one on any route, some routes pass none" means you can give a relic for nothing. I would flag that as a possible trap: the choice does not say whether a merchant is ahead. Suggestion: grey it or warn when the rest of the route has no merchant, or make it "at the next merchant, even a later act". (Needs a game-lead call.)

### 3c. Does a curse feel like a real cost, and is swapping past it clear?
- **Visibly yes, mechanically no (theory).** The curse is unmistakable in the HUD: a purple frame, a "Curse" chip, a dud name in the slot (`-curse_hud.png`). The "Next:" line under the slot shows what comes after. But swapping costs a 1.0 s penalty cooldown (`CardSlot` `swap_penalty` 1.0), nothing else, and a curse never has an upside. So a curse is a small tax of about 1 s per rotation on one slot, and only if you notice and press Shift + the slot key. Reply-All Storm and Mandatory Training only hurt if you cast them.
- **The HUD never tells a new player that the swap exists, or the key.** Nothing on the slot says "Shift+Q swaps". The curse is clear, "you can get past it" is not.
- **Where a curse is a real cost:** next to The Micromanager (cannot swap a dead card), which is a nice, surprising interaction. Otherwise a curse is cheaper than 50 gold. I'd expect a good player to take curses freely.
- Suggestion: either make the curse swap cost 2 s, or let the curse's own text say so on the slot ("Swap: Shift+Q"), and show effect text on every curse in the event offer. Default in "Questions" below.

### 3d. Is the Raider an act-ending fight now?
**Not for the Rogue bot, yes for the Wizard bot (bot numbers, not a person).** Human-preset bot, three-act runs, the act 2 mini-boss:
- Rogue: Raider fight 10.0, 13.6, 14.5, 17.6, 19.7 s (lowest HP in that fight 21-27 of 30). A normal combat room takes the same bot 20-25 s. So the Raider is quicker than an ordinary fight for the Rogue, with plenty of relics by then (about 9).
- Wizard: 30.1, 39.6, 42.8, 48.5, 53.3, 64.5 s (lowest HP 10-22). It is a real fight for the Wizard; one Wizard run in the `-StartRoom 20` set was lost at room 18 (act 2).
- Relics at the Raider are the lever: with 180 HP the Raider is no longer free, but a Rogue with the usual 9 relics still deletes it in about 15 s. I did not fight it by hand. The director's own words earlier ("died super fast", the Raider; his earlier view was it was too easy) should be the judge; I'd say the Rogue's Raider is still not an act-ending fight on this evidence.
- Suggestion for game-lead: DF1 already exists to raise difficulty; put the Raider on its list if the director's own Rogue run still flattens it.

### 3e. Run length with decisions (D50 = B, 15-20 min real)
- **Fight time** (observed, sim seconds, `Fight time` in the bot's totals): Rogue 328-436 s (5.5-7.3 min), Wizard 466-707 s (7.8-11.8 min). Three events add no fight time (event rooms are 0.0 s) unless you take a fight.
- **Real time** (my estimate, not observed): add about 30 map choices at 4-6 s (2-3 min), about 20 reward and relic screens at 10-15 s (4-5 min), 3 events at about 20-30 s (1-1.5 min), the merchant 1-2 min, plus pauses and deaths of attention. That puts a Rogue at about 14-17 min and a Wizard at about 17-22 min. Events add about 1-1.5 min, so a Wizard run sits at or just above the top of the target. Within the range, not clearly over.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **A curse is a cheap tax, so the event "costs" for curses are weak (major, theory).** A swap is a 1.0 s penalty; nothing makes a curse hurt otherwise. Sign in blood beats paying 50 gold, and the Supply Closet's "random curse" is almost free next to a tier 3 relic. A player will take every curse offered. Without a bite, curses are decoration, and the Micromanager is the only relic that makes them matter. Suggestion: raise the swap penalty on a curse, or have each curse leave a small lingering cost; or let the Closet's curse land in the slot you rely on. game-lead call.
2. **Nothing teaches the swap (major for new players).** The curse looks bad but the slot never says Shift+key swaps past it. Suggestion: ux, a one-line hint on a slot whose current card is a curse ("Shift+Q swaps"), shown until the first swap.
3. **Curse events don't describe the curse (major/minor).** "Gain a random curse" is opaque to a first-time player, who cannot know it is a dud card or that it is a swap tax. Suggestion: ux, one sentence on the event screen the first time ("A curse is a dud card in one slot; Shift+key swaps past it") or a "Curse" tooltip line; and show effect text on the curse in the remove list ("Property Damage" has none today, only "Cooldown 3 s").
4. **The Loyalty Pledge is all pain (minor).** Two costs and no reward, no leave. It reads fine but feels like a tax, not an event. Suggestion: either allow "Leave" at a cost of nothing for the first time, or give the curse option a small sweetener (gold back), or keep it rare as is.
5. **Rule text repeats the cost (minor, see 4a).**

## Items from item 4, each with a default

### 4a. Sacrifice relic rule text repeating "Give up N relics" under its cost line
Seen in `-sacrifice.png`: "You deal 35% more damage. **Give up 2 relics to take it.**" then, below, "**Cost - Give up 2 relics**". It says the same thing twice and pushes the flavor line down, and the tile at 1280x720 is already tall. **Default: remove the "Give up N relics to take it." sentence from the tile and from the rule text on the offer; keep the orange "Cost" line and the "Taking:" box in the give-up step.** Minor.

### 4b. Curse purple next to relic violet
Seen side by side in the captures. The relic violet is a pale lavender header band with a hexagon; the curse purple is a saturated magenta-purple frame, with a "Curse" chip and an X mark. In one screen they are distinguishable, and the shapes (hexagon versus X) carry the difference. They would clash only if a relic and a curse share a list (pause run-mods list, not seen by me). **Default: leave as is.** Minor.

### 4c. The cluttered 4-enemy Bleed scenario
At the moment of the detonation (`-bleed4_0.png`) two "Bleed detonated" labels and two "32!" damage numbers sit on top of each other and over the "Bleed x2" labels, so it reads as a pile of text. A moment later (the second shot, not kept) it settles: two labels remain and the other two enemies are dimmed, as intended. The slot's "LIVE" glow is clearly visible. **Default: leave the dimming; stagger the detonation labels or show "Bleed detonated" once per cast, not once per target.** Minor, a one-second clutter at the payoff moment (where you most want it clean).

### 4d. The give-up step with the keyboard
Seen in `-sacrifice_pick.png`: the focus ring lands on **Cancel**, not on the relic tiles. In keyboard play (the brief's flow) pressing Enter right away cancels the whole take. Up goes into the relics, Down to Confirm or Cancel. It is safe (nothing is lost) but surprising: a player expects the first Enter to pick something. **Default: start focus on the first relic tile, keep Cancel one Down away.** Minor. Not tested by hand (the focus ring comes from the frame; the Up/Down flow is from the README).

### Also, act 3 telegraphs on sand (`-sand.png`)
The orange golem reads fine on tan and is clearly its own colour. The pink telegraph is a thin, pale outline and is still muted on sand; it is readable at 1280x720 close to the player, weaker further away. **Default: keep, thicken the pink one notch if a hands-on player misses one.** Minor.
