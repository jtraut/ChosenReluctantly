# Playtest: DF1, difficulty bump and readability (2026-11-17)

## Build and setup
HEAD f6f3a69 (DF1 qa PASS), Godot 4.7.2, windowed Vulkan. **I cannot play with a live keyboard and mouse here.** What I did:
- **Bot runs** (human-preset bot, `run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -Seed 1 -Runs 3 -BotPreset human -Route first`), one command per class, three full acts, seeds 1-3 (3 runs per class, not 1). A bot, not a person. Plus `-Class wizard -Acts 1 -StartRoom 10 -BossLog`, 4 runs, for the act 1 boss time.
- **Windowed frame captures** of the real game (`tools/capture_screenshot.ps1`): map, a room, the Internal Audit event, the relic room with sacrifice relics, the Game Over screen, the boss relic screen.

## Does it feel harder in a fair way? (bot numbers)
| Class | Seed | Result | Fell at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rogue | 1 | lost | room 13 (act 2, third fight), arrived at 17/30 HP with only 2 relics |
| Rogue | 2 | lost | room 25 (act 3 elite), arrived at 5.6/30 after a chain of low-HP rooms |
| Rogue | 3 | won | final boss at full HP |
| Wizard | 1 | lost | room 23 (act 3 elite), 4.6 HP lowest at the end |
| Wizard | 2 | lost | room 29 (act 3, one before the boss), HP 2.6 two rooms earlier |
| Wizard | 3 | lost | **final boss**, dead 16.4 s in, lowest 4.3 |

Fight time (sim): Rogue won run 464 s (7.7 min). Wizard lost runs 447-552 s. Real-time estimate (mine, not observed): about 15-18 min for a Rogue win with map choices, rewards and events, inside the target.

**Observed pattern: HP is the resource.** HP carries room to room and only rests (and act starts) refill it, so every one of these deaths is attrition: the previous 2-4 rooms ended at 40-60% HP and the next fight finished the job. That reads as fair in a roguelite (you can watch the bar sink and choose a rest), but I did not see a single bad fight I would call "that killed me". A player will feel "I was losing for a while". Whether each death is readable depends on whether a player can see which enemy caused the slide; I can't judge that by bot.

- **Act 2's opening is not a wall in these samples.** 5 of 6 runs cleared rooms 11-13. The one loss there (Rogue seed 1) had 2 relics going into act 2 (the first-route bot skipped relic rooms and rests), so it is the thin-build case, a fair fail.
- **The Raider is not act-ending.** Mini-boss times: Rogue 24-29 s (lowest HP 18-30), Wizard 29-54 s (lowest 12-22). A real fight for the Wizard only. No run died to it.
- **Act 3 is where runs ended: 4 of 5 losses (rooms 23, 25, 29, boss).** That is the x1.1 act. The spike is "act 3", not one room, which is where a bump should land. But an act 1 or 2 slip costs 8 minutes later.
- **Spongier or fairer?** Damage went up, not enemy HP, so fights are not longer: room times are 15-33 s as before. Not spongier.
- **Wizard is the harder class.** 0/3 versus Rogue 1/3 here. SIM1 qa has both in the 16-18/36 band, so my 3 runs are noise, but Wizard max HP is only 20-22.

### Wizard act 1 boss (qa flagged about 98 s)
Observed, 4 runs, standard deck, no relics (`-Acts 1 -StartRoom 10`): **81-95 s** (median 88.6 s), all won, HP lost 20-34 of 22. The boss log says **the volley is 75% of the damage taken** (17 of 175 pellets hit); slams and smashes almost never hit. So it is **long but not dangerous**. Opinion: 90 s of mostly waiting for cooldowns while dodging one attack drags. In the real three-act runs the Wizard (with a few relics) killed it in 28-34 s, so it only drags for a Wizard with no build yet.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **Difficulty rose without bloat.** Fights stay 15-33 s; the pressure shows as HP attrition and act 3 deaths, not longer rooms.
2. **Event and relic screens stay clear** (Audit line, one-line sacrifice cost, boss relic "real upside and a real cost", `-bossrelic.png`).
3. **Game Over copy** keeps the weary tone, and "Try again" is one key.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **Deaths are slow attrition, hard to read as "my mistake" (medium, bot evidence).** Every loss arrived at the fatal room already at 20-60% HP. Suggestion: make low HP before a rest choice visible on the map screen (the map header shows only gold, no HP).
2. **A thin build in act 2 gets no second chance (medium).** Rogue seed 1 reached act 2 with 2 relics. A human taking relic rooms won't see this, but a new player needs to learn the map's relic and rest nodes matter. Low urgency.
3. **Most losses come in the last third (medium).** Right place for a bump, but a final-boss loss 16 s in (Wizard seed 3) after 8 minutes hurts. Not a change request: a director check.
4. **Wizard starter-deck act 1 boss drags, about 90 s (minor).** Only with no build. If it shows up in the director's run, trim the phases that never hit (slam, smash).
5. **Four of six readability fixes could not be seen with the current tooling (process).** Add the two flags above.
