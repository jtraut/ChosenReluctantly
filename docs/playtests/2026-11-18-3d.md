# Playtest: slice 3d (loadouts), 2026-11-18

## Build and setup
- HEAD c741184, Godot 4.7.2, Windows, real window with the Vulkan renderer. I played through scripted launches and screenshots, not by hand. A focus-and-SendKeys attempt hit the wrong window, so I used the game's own `--press=<t>:<action>` tool (`ui_accept`, `dead_card_swap_N`). That goes through the real input path, but nobody clicked anything. Mouse flows (click to add a card, right-click to remove, hover) were NOT exercised.
- Every launch used scratch `--profile-path` and `--loadouts-path` files in my session scratchpad. I checked the real user folder afterwards: no `profile.json`, `loadouts.json` or run save appeared.
- I wrote one saved loadout per class by hand as JSON, because the editor cannot be driven without a mouse here. So "build a loadout" is judged from the editor captures, not from clicking through it:
  - Rogue "Bleed Brawler": 16 cards, plan Arterial Spray and Exsanguinate, relic Chasing Paper.
  - Wizard "Burn Pedant": 15 cards, plan Hot Desking, relic Ergonomic Chair.
- Captures used: the start screen for both classes, Enter via `--press=1:ui_accept`, a run with the saved deck, a reward screen with a plan card (`--loadout=1 --run-offer=...`), UxCapture `loadout_editor_legal`, `loadout_editor_blocked` and `shop_plan_act2` (both classes), and a curse swap with `--run-curse-first=curse_reply_all_storm`.
- This is a slice playtest of the loadout loop. I did not play a full run or a full room by hand.

## Smoke check result
PASS, no blocker.
- Launches with no console errors or warnings (engine banner and renderer line only), both classes.
- World visible: floor, blue Rogue and purple Wizard, red enemies, shadows, a camera framing the player, HP 30/30 and 22/22, five card slots (Q, E, F, Ctrl, R), dodge pips.
- Input: a scripted Enter starts the run; a scripted `dead_card_swap_2` swaps a slot and shows a cooldown bar. A pink cone hit with a "10" damage number showed up on its own in a capture. I did not move or aim by hand.
- Seen in passing: a Rogue that stands still in room 1 is at 20/30 after about 3.5 s and dead by about 8 s. An idle player, so not a finding by itself, but there is no grace period.

## Answers to the director's questions
1. **Smoke check:** passes.
2. **Editor clarity and the detail strip:** see "Editor". Readable without the docs once you are in it, with "Plan" and the tab names as weak spots. The effect-line-only strip is enough for starter cards, not for reward cards or anything keyword-dependent.
3. **Starting a run:** one Enter from the start screen starts the run with the saved deck (Q Open a Vein, E Everyone Calm, F Warm-Up, Ctrl Walk Through, R Nap Time, with "Next:" lines matching my list). The starter relic shows as a two-letter badge under the dodge pips ("CP", "EC"). See problem 2.
4. **"On your plan" badge:** clear on reward tiles and the merchant tile. I did not capture a mini-boss tile.
5. **2 s curse swap:** see problem 1.
6. **N3 (chip and gold over the start screen):** mildly odd, not distracting, dimmed behind the panel. See problem 5.
7. **New-player blockers:** none. Class pick, start screen, editor and run chain together. Problems 2 and 3 are friction, not blocks.

## Editor
Screens: [legal Rogue](img/2026-11-18-3d-editor-legal-rogue.png), [blocked Wizard](img/2026-11-18-3d-editor-blocked-wizard.png), [start screen](img/2026-11-18-3d-start-last-used-preset.png).
- **Clear without the docs:** the list is grouped by slot with live counters ("Attack: AoE 4/3"). The right side says "Ready to save." in green, or "Can't save yet" with plain reasons ("Movement needs 3 unique cards, deck has 0"). Tick-or-cross chips show where the gap is. The bottom line names every key.
- **Confusing without the docs:**
  - **"Reward" / "Add to plan" / "On plan".** A Reward card (rare or legendary) can't go in your deck, yet its row offers "Add to plan" where Starter cards have a "-". A new player will not know what a plan is, what it does, or that those cards are not usable at the start. The panel just says "Plan: 1". The payoff, the green badge in reward screens, is far from this choice.
  - **Tab names.** Five tabs read "1. Empty" to "5. Empty" even while slot 1 holds a ready-to-save draft.
  - **Empty name field.** The name is the first thing the checker complains about (Wizard capture). A default name such as "Loadout 1" would save a step.
  - **Clipping at 1024x600** (UxCapture fixture): in the blocked capture the Plan box is cut off ("Hot Desking (Movement. Reward)" half hidden) and Save and Delete sit tight under it. The card list starts scrolled partway down in both captures.
- **Card detail strip (effect line, no keywords).** For starter cards it is enough to judge a number: "12 fire damage (area), Burn 2 damage/s for 5 s. Cooldown 3.3 s", plus a flavour line. It is not enough to theorycraft:
  - On the reward screen Arterial Spray shows "If the target has Bleed: 4 physical damage (cone)", the chips "Melee" and "Bleed", and "In slot: Cutting Corners / Everyone Calm Down / ...". The editor strip has no keyword chips and no slot-mates. I did not see a reward card's strip in the editor, so I can't say whether the conditional line is there.
  - Which cards share a slot is exactly what you want to see while building.
  - Verdict: enough to judge a number, not to build around a keyword. For GDD pillar 1 (deep builds) the strip wants keywords and slot-mates, at least for the selected card.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The start screen is quick and obvious.** "Start a run - Playing as Rogue. Pick a deck." lists Preset deck, the saved loadout (cards, plan count, relic name on one line) and "Edit or create a loadout". Last used is preselected and one Enter starts. A returning player is in a room in seconds.
2. **The "can't save" feedback.** Live counters, chips and one sentence per failing rule mean you never wonder why Save is off. It reads like a form, not a puzzle.
3. **"On your plan" is a clean pill.** On the reward tile ([image](img/2026-11-18-3d-reward-on-plan.png)) and the merchant tile ([image](img/2026-11-18-3d-merchant-on-plan.png)) it reads in one glance, green with a tick, and does not fight the rarity tag.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **The 2 s curse swap is a longer wait but not a visible cost.** Observed: swap at 1.0 s with Reply-All Storm in slot E. The E card shows a dim grey bar about 15% full 0.3 s later ([image](img/2026-11-18-3d-curse-swap-0.3s.png)) and about 60% at 1.2 s ([image](img/2026-11-18-3d-curse-swap-1.2s.png)), so roughly 2 s, matching the data. It looks identical to an ordinary 1 s swap apart from bar speed: no "2 s" and no curse mark. And the first enemy only arrives after about 2 s, so a swap at the start of a room costs nothing. Why it matters: the tension of the dead-card swap lives under pressure, and the player cannot see the price before paying it. Proposal: show it on the swap hint, for example "Shift+E swaps (2 s)". I only swapped in a quiet moment; the feel under pressure is for the director to check.
2. **The starter relic is hard to read in the run.** It is a two-letter badge ("CP", "EC") with no name on screen and no cue that it is the player's own pick ([Rogue](img/2026-11-18-3d-run-rogue-loadout-relic.png), [Wizard](img/2026-11-18-3d-run-wizard-loadout-relic.png)). It is the one loadout decision outside the deck. I did not hover or open the pause screen, so I can't say whether a tooltip exists. Proposal: show the relic name for the first seconds of room 1.
3. **"Plan" is the weak concept in the editor** (see Editor). Proposal: one line under the Plan panel, for example "Plan cards are cards you hope to find. They get an On your plan tag in rewards and shops."
4. **The detail strip is thin for build theory** (see Editor). Proposal: keywords and slot-mates on the selected card.
5. **Start screen leftovers and small text.** The act/room chip ("Room 1 of 30 - Combat") and "0 gold" show dimmed top right of the start screen ([image](img/2026-11-18-3d-start-saved-loadout.png)). Clutter, not distraction; "Room 1 of 30" may mislead before a run exists. Also seen in the run: slot names get cut ("Warm-Up," for Warm-Up, Reluctantly; "Flaming" for Flaming Nonsense; "Overhand," for Overhand, Whatever).

## Questions for the director
1. Plan cards: should the editor explain the tag, or is learning it from the first reward screen enough?
2. Should the curse swap price be shown before the swap, or is "found out when it hurts" the intended tone?
3. Should a starter relic announce itself at run start?
4. D53 / qa N1: starter relics can still drop in ordinary offers (a held one is never re-offered). Fine for now?

## Proposed design changes (proposals only)
- Show "(2 s)" on a curse's swap hint.
- A one-line explanation for "Plan" in the editor; a default name for a new draft.
- Keywords and slot-mates in the editor's detail strip for the selected card.
- Show the starter relic's name for the first seconds of a run.

## Not verified
- No mouse use in a window: click-add, right-click remove and hover were not exercised. I never opened the editor in a live window, only UxCapture fixtures (qa's tests cover the model).
- No full room played by hand, so hit-stop, shake and impact are not evaluated here.
- No mini-boss "On your plan" tile captured; qa covers the act 2 merchant, and I saw reward and merchant.
- I did not see a reward card's detail strip in the editor, or check for a relic tooltip.
- The Plan clipping is from a UxCapture fixture at 1024x600; a real window may differ.
- One stray Enter was sent by Windows SendKeys to the foreground window (VS Code) before I fixed the window match. `git status` is clean, but an unsaved newline in an open editor tab is possible; worth a glance.
