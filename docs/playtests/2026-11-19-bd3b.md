# Playtest: BD3b (Wizard depth), 2026-11-19

## Build and setup
- HEAD 772e5ab, Godot 4.7.2 console build, Windows, real window on a Vulkan renderer (RTX 3080).
- **I did not play by hand.** I can't hold movement keys or aim with the mouse from this environment. What I did:
  - **Scripted real-window launches** (`capture_screenshot.ps1`, `--press=` taps on the slot keys) with `--class=wizard --deck=wizard-thesis` and `--deck=wizard-ward-battlemage`, room 1 (`--no-map`) and the boss (`--acts=1 --run-room=10`, with `--relics=reading_list+filing_cabinet` and `--relics=thorned_boundary+exit_strategy`). The player stood still, so these show the HUD and the screen, not fighting feel.
  - **UxCapture states** `bd3b_hud`, `bd3b_hud_fired` and `bd3b_fire_offer` at 1024x600 and 1600x900.
  - **Wave sim** (the bot, headless): 6 whole one-act runs (seeds 1-6, `-NoMap -Plan -BossLog`) each of `wizard-thesis`, `wizard-ward-battlemage` and the plain Wizard starter deck. This is the plan bot, not a human. Its numbers are data, not feel.
- **Everything about feel (hit-stop, shake, "oh nice") is NOT judged here.** "Reads" means in a screenshot. "Feels" is theory from numbers, and I label it.
- Not done: a live fight with Reading List, Thorned Boundary or Hot Streak firing; a plain Wizard run by hand.
- Screenshots (1024x600 and 1600x900) are in `docs/playtests/img/bd3b/`.

## Smoke check result
**PASS, no blocker.**
- Launches with no errors or warnings in the console (engine banner and renderer line only), every launch.
- World visible: green floor, purple Wizard, red enemies, shadows, the camera frames the player, HP 22/22, five slots (Q, E, F, Ctrl, R), LMB/RMB bars, dodge pip.
- Scripted slot presses respond: a Thesis Defense cast showed a "5" damage number and a pink cone; slot cooldown bars run.
- A Wizard standing still in room 1 is dead in about 7 s ("Well. That went badly."). Same as the 3d report: no grace period. An idle player is not a finding by itself.
- Note: with the map on (a normal launch), `--press` taps landed on the map screen and did nothing. Use `--no-map` or `--run-room` for captures.

## What I saw, by question

### Thesis (build Charge, then spend it)
- **Reads well on screen.** "Charge 3/10" with a purple segmented bar sits under the dodge pip. At 6 Charge the Thesis Defense slot wears a pill, "+10/Charge: +60" at 1024x600 and "+10 per Charge, now +60" at 1600x900. At the cap the bar turns yellow, the label says "15/15 MAX" and the pill says "now +150" (+7 base = 157). I could tell what the number means and what it is worth without being told. [HUD 1024x600](img/bd3b/ux-bd3b_hud-1024x600.png), [Charge at cap, 1600x900](img/bd3b/ux-bd3b_hud_fired-1600x900.png).
- **Bot numbers (6 runs, one act):** it spent Charge 16-27 times a run, 3.9-5.0 Charge each (about 47-57 damage with the base), against 6-14 for other single-target cards. On paper a clear "oh nice" hit. The Attack: Single slot did 32-43% of a Thesis run's damage. Thesis boss fights were the fastest of the three decks: 28-58 s, median about 32 s (plain Wizard 37-48 s, about 40 s).
- **Swinginess (theory):** 67 at 6 Charge is 5-10 times a normal hit. Charge decays 4 s after the last gain, and Footnotes and Literature Review give 3 each, so 6 Charge needs two casts in the window and 15 needs five. I think 157 is a rare fantasy number and the realistic spend is 40-70. Not confirmed by hand.
- **Cycle problem (theory from the capture):** Thesis Defense, Footnotes and Citation Needed share the Attack: Single slot and come up in order. In my room 1 capture Thesis was in the slot with Charge 3 by luck; a few seconds later the slot had rolled on to Citation Needed. You can't choose to cast Thesis when Charge is high except by dead-card swap. This is the thing to feel by hand.

### Ward Battlemage versus the plain Wizard (D54)
- **Does it feel like its own build? Not in anything I could measure, and nothing on screen says it is.**
- Bot damage split, 6 runs each (about 1,700 damage a run):

| Deck | Single slot | AoE slot | Light + heavy | Spiteful Ward nova | Boss fight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain Wizard | 34-43% | 22-28% | about 30-35% | none | 37-48 s |
| Thesis | 32-43% | 21-28% | about 30-35% | none | 28-58 s |
| Ward Battlemage | 36-45% | 15-22% | 32-40% | 6 damage in 3 of 6 runs, 0 in the rest | 33-87 s, median about 44 s |

- Spiteful Ward did 18 damage in total over 6 runs (about 10,000 enemy HP). Its 6-damage nova is under 0.2% of a run. In a hand-played run the Wizard is hit more than the kiting bot is, so the real figure is higher, but 6 per hit against 13-20 per cast never becomes the build's damage. This matches the D54 text.
- The Ward deck took more punishment at the boss: its slowest fight was 87 s with a low of 5.8 HP and one clear at 6.7 HP. That is a build that kills slower, not one that shrugs hits off.
- What is different on screen: the "Spiteful Ward 3.4 s" bar under the Charge counter, a "Spiteful Ward x3" label over the player when the nova fires, and Hostile Feedback's "Below 50% HP" pill. Readable, but they say "a ward is up", not "this deck plays differently".
- **Evidence for D54:** option A (ship as is, fix in BAL1) is cheap and safe, but I'd expect you to feel "this is the standard Wizard with a bar on screen" when you play it. Option C (survival identity) fits the numbers best, because a bot that kites will never show a damage payoff. Option B tunes before you have played it, which CLAUDE.md's playtest gate warns against.

### Readability
- **Charge counter and drain line:** the counter is clear. The drain is a thin line under the bar, and I couldn't see it move in stills.
- **Spiteful Ward bar and cue:** clear. The bar ticks down in seconds, "x3" counts hits, and the floating "Spiteful Ward x3" is legible at both sizes.
- **Fire flash:** a moment, not judgeable from stills.
- **Slot pills:** "+0.5/Projectile: +1.5" (Citation Needed), "Below 50% HP", "Needs Burn" and "+10/Charge: +60" all read. A new player can tell the card is conditional and what it is worth right now.
- **"Swap: free" (Filing Cabinet):** shows on Utility and Movement all the time, even on a ready card, and as "Shift+F swaps: free" on a curse slot. It is a standing label, not a prompt, so it reads as clutter. Known item.
- **Relic row:** two-letter badges (RL, FC, TB, ES). I couldn't tell what "TB" was. Full text is on the pause screen only. Known item.
- **1024x600:** everything fits; the status rows (Charge, Ward bar) and the relic badges don't overlap each other or the slots. No clipping seen. 1600x900 is the same, looser.

### The new relics
- **Exit Strategy:** 5-29 damage a run (1-2%), 15 triggers in one bot run. It works. It is a quiet extra, not an "oh nice".
- **Filing Cabinet:** the "Swap: free" pill works. The bot made no swaps with it in two runs, so I can't say I felt it.
- **Reading List, Thorned Boundary, Hot Streak:** seen as badges only. No live fight, no judgment.

### Fire colour cue
- With the Fire chip hidden, **an orange card border (and orange slot outline) marks Fire cards**. In [the 1600x900 shot](img/bd3b/ux-bd3b_hud_fired-1600x900.png) Fine, Burn Then and Hot Head stand out from the tan-framed non-Fire cards at once. So yes, I can still tell Kindling and Hot Head are Fire, as a colour-seeing player.
- It is colour only. There is no icon or word. Known item.
- The `bd3b_fire_offer` shop capture shows "Stand-in effect line, 8 physical damage (cone)" on every card. That is the capture fixture, not the game; I note it so nobody thinks the shop is broken. [Shop shot](img/bd3b/ux-bd3b_fire_offer-1024x600.png).

### Pace and time (fight time is the bot's; the real-time figure is my estimate)
- **Bot fight time**, act 1 (10 rooms, 8 fights, the boss): 175-250 s of sim time per run; boss 28-87 s.
- **Estimated human real time:** rooms are 15-25 s in the bot's hands; a human is slower. About 10-12 minutes of fighting over three acts, plus about 1-1.5 minutes of map, rewards and shop per act: **about 15-18 minutes**, inside the 15-20 target. Theory.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **Charge reads at a glance.** The segmented bar, "15/15 MAX" and the live pill "+10 per Charge, now +60" turn a hidden mechanic into one you can plan around.
2. **The Spiteful Ward bar and its "x3" cue.** You know the ward is up, how long is left, and when it fired.
3. **Conditional pills on slots** ("Below 50% HP", "Needs Burn", "+0.5/Projectile: +1.5") say what a card needs without opening a menu.

## Top 5 problems, ranked
1. **Ward Battlemage doesn't play as its own build (major).** Damage split, kill speed and the nova (18 damage over six runs) match the plain Wizard, and the boss fights run slower. A player who builds it for "the ward blasts back" gets a bar and a label. Suggestion: D54. My lean is C (survival identity with a number you can see, such as damage taken or armor uptime) rather than B.
2. **Thesis may not line up with its own Charge (major, unconfirmed by hand).** Thesis, Footnotes and Citation Needed share one slot and come up in order, and Charge decays in 4 s. High Charge and Thesis in the slot may rarely coincide. If so the spend feels like luck, not skill. Suggestion: the director checks this first in play; a longer decay or a way to hold the card could fix it.
3. **Reading List's 157 is probably out of reach (minor, theory).** 15 Charge needs five Primer casts inside a 4 s decay. If the headline number never happens, the relic over-promises. Suggestion: watch it in play; if so, show a realistic number or add decay time.
4. **"Swap: free" is permanent noise (minor, known).** It labels slots that aren't dead and a curse slot. Hide it while swaps are locked (already on the polish list).
5. **Relic badges are two letters and Fire is colour only (minor, known).** "TB" and "FC" mean nothing in combat, and a colour-blind player has no Fire cue. Both are on the polish list.

Not ranked: the idle Wizard dies in 7 s (carried from 3d).
