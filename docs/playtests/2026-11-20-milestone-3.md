# Milestone 3 playtest (2026-11-20)

**This is the baseline milestone report. There is no earlier milestone report, so there is no "better or worse" comparison, only numbers later milestones can be compared against.**

## Build and setup
- Repo `main` at 02b908a (clean). Windowed watch mode (TL2), `-Scale 4`, 1152x648. A bot played, not a human; every "feel" statement below is read from screenshots and logs, not from hands on the controller (see "Where the bot limits me").
- Shots: `shots\m3` (human preset) and `shots\m3off` (preset off), gitignored. Selected shots: `docs/playtests/img/milestone-3/`.
- Route policy was the default `first` for every run (the bot takes the first node and first offer). That matters a lot for the pillar judgement below.

## Smoke check result: PASS
- Launches, no engine errors or warnings in the console of any of the 9 windowed runs (the only "error/warning" text match was the bot's own settings line "aim_error 25.0 deg").
- World visible: floor, lit isometric camera framing the player, player (blue/purple capsule) and enemies (red/grey capsules), shadows, per-act floor tint (blue act 1-2 rooms, brown act 3).
- HUD sensible: HP 30/30 Rogue, 22/22 Wizard at start, slot cards with key, name, type tags and "Next:" chain, gold, act/room/type box, relic badges.
- Input was exercised only through the bot (movement, aim, attacks, dodge, slots all demonstrably worked: slots fire, cooldown bars fill, damage numbers appear). I did not press keys by hand.
- Blocker: **no**.

## Top 3 things that work
1. **The run structure reads as a real roguelite.** The map screen ([map](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-map-act2.png)) is clear: legend, gold ring for "you are here", white ring for the next pick, relic-room chest size dots, "Every route ends at the mini-boss". Routes really do differ: seed 3 act 1 was combat/event/rest/elite/event; seed 1 was four combats then a merchant and an elite; seed 13 had an elite in room 3. Three acts have three different map shapes and mini-boss/boss identities (Brute, Corporate Raider, Overly Enthusiastic Guardian with ground pounds and a three-phase bar). The boss screens show phase name, armor, resists and the current attack telegraphed in text ([mini-boss](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-act2-miniboss.png), [final boss](img/milestone-3/wizard-s1off-final-boss-half-hp.png)).
2. **Reward screens give you real information.** The card offer ([card reward](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-card-reward.png)) shows slot type, tags, exact numbers, "In slot: ..." (which slot rotation the card would join), Keystone/Payoff markers, rejects left (1 of 3) and a flavour line. The merchant ([rogue merchant](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-merchant.png), [wizard merchant](img/milestone-3/wizard-s1off-merchant.png)) has clear prices, "Only sold here" relics and escalating removal cost. The relic-room cards show tier, class/element and a one-line effect. I could tell what each choice did without being told.
3. **Tone lands.** "Well. That went badly. The world is still cheerful about it. You are not." and "Somehow, you finished. Nobody is more surprised than you." ([lost](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-end-lost.png), [won](img/milestone-3/wizard-s1off-end-won.png)), "Another act. Same hero, same reluctance." ([act screen](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-act-screen.png)), card names like "Nope, Leaping Away", relic text like "Faster hands. Longer hours. No extra pay." It is funny and stays out of the way.

## Top 5 problems, ranked

### 1. Max HP never grows, and act 3 kills a 30 HP / 22 HP hero in one bad fight
What happened: in all three act-3 losses the player started the room at 14-26 HP and died inside one or two rooms (Rogue off seed 13: room 22, lowest HP 4 then dead; Wizard off seed 3: entered room 22 at 14/22 and died; Rogue human seed 3: entered room 24 at 21/30, died, lowest HP 2.4 in the room before). The act-start "heal 30%" is invisible because the bot arrives at act 2 and act 3 at full HP already ("HP 30 of 30" on the act screen). The hero is as fragile in room 24 as in room 1 (Rogue 30 HP, Wizard 22 HP; room 1 already took Rogue to 10 HP on seed 3). Relics and deck bloat are the only growth, and the Wizard's whole life is 22 HP.
Why it matters: the game feels like a flat difficulty line with a late wall, not a power curve. Dying at room 22-24 after ~7 minutes of fights, with 200-600 unspent gold, feels bad. GDD §9a says a first win should take a few tries, so a wall is fine in principle, but the "build" is not what stops the wall.
Suggestion (for game-lead): decide if any HP growth exists in a run (relic, rest option, card) before BAL1. Without it, act 3 is balanced only by enemy numbers.

### 2. Decks end up as bloated starter piles with weak build identity, in the runs I could see
Evidence: Rogue seed 1 won with 28 cards: the original 15 starters plus 13 picks spanning Bleed (Arterial Spray, Exsanguinate, Paper Cuts), Fire (Hot Desking, Wall of Mild Fire, Controlled Demolition) and neutral (Heal By Hurting, Golden Handshake, Second Opinion, Pointy Stick). Wizard seed 1 won with 27 cards: Three Bolts, Reluctantly, Wall of Mild Fire, Literature Review, Hot Desking x2, Office Hours... Both decks are "everything mixed". Slots show "Next: ..." chains of 2-3 cards, so slot rotation is visible, but with 27-28 cards across 5 slots each slot holds 5+ cards and nothing is likely to fire on purpose.
Caveat: the `first` policy takes the first offer and is not build-aware, so a human would draft better. Still, the data shows how little the game pushes a player toward a plan: offers in these runs were often unrelated (Rogue seed 3 act 3 room 21: Momentum / Kindling / Second Opinion).
Why it matters: pillar 1 is explicitly the top priority. In watched runs I could not see a deck that "played differently" from another.
Suggestion: next playtest should use a build-aware bot policy (or a hand run) to test a committed Bleed Rogue and a committed Thesis Wizard. Consider capping deck bloat (e.g. more removal offers, or fewer starters) so the draft decisions matter.

### 3. Real-time estimate runs over the 15-20 minute target
Facts: a win's end screen reported fight time 11:00 (Wizard) and 10:47 (Rogue). Sim time includes ~0.2 s "room" entries for events/rests. Opinion/estimate, not measured: a human also stops at about 30 screens (about 20 card/relic/event/merchant decisions plus ~30 map picks, which are mostly one-option picks in acts 1-2), at maybe 15-30 s each, so I estimate **about 20-28 minutes real time**, above the GDD §9 target. The merchant, relic and map screens are not the issue (they are fast to read); the volume of single-option "choose your path" screens (e.g. act 1 rooms 1, 3, 7, 12: "node:5 (Combat)" is the only offer) is. A one-option map pick is a button press with no decision.
Suggestion: auto-advance when the map has exactly one onward node (still show the map for a beat), which cuts a screen per room from the run.

### 4. Gold piles up unspent; the economy is not a decision in these runs
Facts: every run ended with 200-624 gold (Wizard off seed 1: 524, Rogue off seed 1: 604, Rogue seed 13: 623, Rogue seed 3: 234 after one merchant visit). The `first` policy rarely reaches a merchant, but even a route with one merchant leaves ~400 gold, because gold income (24-75 per room) outpaces what a single merchant can absorb. Rogue seed 3 went into act 2's merchant with 435 gold, spent 385 on relics and a removal in one burst.
Why it matters: spare gold is a missing decision, and the player cannot convert it into a build or HP.
Suggestion: either add a gold sink (buy HP, reroll, more merchant visits) or reduce income. This is partly a bot artefact; confirm with a route-aware bot.

### 5. Crowded fights are readable but cluttered by relic tooltips and number spam
[Rogue crowded](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-crowded-fight.png): 7 red capsules around a blue player; two relic tooltips ("Fight Club Membership x2", "x3") float over the action, damage numbers (3, 3, 4, +2, +5) stack on top of each other, and the cone attack hides the enemy under it. The [Wizard crowded fight](img/milestone-3/wizard-s1off-crowded-fight.png) is cleaner (hit numbers, enemy HP bars, one tooltip), but the grey and the red enemies and blue ones look similar at a glance and I cannot tell which are elite or ranged without telegraphs. Two-letter relic badges (AV, FC, LE, SI, TB, HS) are unreadable in combat (already a known issue). The Wizard end screen and Rogue end screen are otherwise clear.
Suggestion: suppress relic proc tooltips during fights or move them to the badge row; separate enemy types by shape, not only colour.

## Other things I noticed (lower rank)
- **Same seed, same map for both classes.** Wizard and Rogue on seed 3 had identical act 1 routes and nearly identical early offers (Pointy Stick / Heat Sink first in both). Good for tests, but it means seed 3 does not feel like a new run when you switch class.
- **Events always went the same way** in these runs only because the bot picked the first option; I cannot judge event variety except that I saw: Offer a relic, Give up a relic, Insert coins, Declare your best asset, Pay the dues, Answer honestly, Settle in, Take a break, Ask for help with your deck. Locked options are shown greyed with "[locked]" in the log. "Give up a relic" with only one relic offered a single card, a forced swap with no real choice ([screenshot](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-event-offer-a-relic.png)).
- **Max HP changed oddly:** Rogue seed 1 ended at 27/27 and Wizard seed 1 at 20/20, from 30 and 22. Presumably a relic or curse; the HUD does not say why. I did not investigate (not diagnosing).
- **Rest on a low-HP bot:** Rogue seed 3 picked "heal" at 26/30 and "remove" at 30/30 (rest rooms with a full HP bar). The bot's choice, not a player problem, but "Remove a card" is the only useful rest option at full HP, so the rest node is effectively a free removal on most routes.
- **Known log nits** (already listed in STATE): "HP at end" stale on loss (Rogue seed 3 said 20.8 for a player who died at 0/30); CROWD lines use act-local rooms ("room 9" in act 2); BOSS lines say "room 10" in act 2 and act 3.

## Pillars

### Deep theorycrafting (pillar 1)
- **Evidence for:** the cards carry real build language (Primer, Keystone, Payoff, Bleed/Burn, "In slot: ..." lines, Next-chain), and the relic pool has build relics (Salt in the Wound, Serrated Excuse, Bleeding Edge, Kindling Jar, Hot Streak). The merchant and relic-swap event create trade-offs.
- **Evidence against:** all decks I saw ended as 20-28 card piles of the 15 starters plus mixed picks, with offers that often ignored the build. I could not observe a deck that felt "built". Treat this as untested rather than failed: I need a build-aware run.
- Verdict: **not shown by this milestone's tooling.** Proposal below.

### No two runs alike (pillar 2)
- Three seeds across both classes gave different act 1 routes, different elite/merchant/relic-room placement, different mini-boss reward offers, different relic sets: Rogue seed 3 ended with Fight Club Membership / Lingering Embers / Salt in the Wound (Bleed + Fire), Rogue seed 1 with Pyre Insurance / Burnout Syndrome / Heavy Sigh / Blood Oath / Synergy Workshop, Rogue seed 13 with Pyre Insurance / Fight Club / Per My Last Email / Bleeding Edge. Wizard seed 1 ended with Corporate Buyout / Exit Strategy / The Micromanager / cc: All Staff / Brain Drain / Kindling Jar. Maps differ by act and by seed.
- Run shape is the part that varies; the deck core does not (starters dominate), and the same boss trio (Brute, Corporate Raider, Guardian) appears every run. Verdict: **good on route and relic variety, thin on deck variety and boss variety.**

### ARPG plus deck-builder blend (pillar 3)
- The HUD makes slots central: each card slot shows key, name, tags, cooldown bar, Next-chain; the Wizard's `Hot Desking` and Rogue's `Blaze and Bail` dash-with-burn show that a slot card changes how you move. The bot swapped cards through slot rotation naturally ("Next: Hot Head" etc.).
- I could not verify that a human feels the blend, because I did not control the character. From the screens, fights are one-room, 18-56 s encounters that the player wins or loses by HP and positioning; I cannot say if slot choices (what is in each slot) change the fight. **Open: a hand test of two decks in the same room.**

## Difficulty and pacing per act (sim seconds from the logs)
- **Act 1 (rooms 1-10):** 181-263 s per act, room fights 18-56 s. Rogue lost 20 HP in room 1 on seed 3 (to 10/30), which is a hard open for a first fight. Wizard human lost twice in room 2 (lowest HP 4.0 in room 2 on seed 3). Mini-boss Brute 170 HP: 13-22 s to half HP.
- **Act 2 (11-20):** 151-205 s. Elite wall room 14 took Rogue to 9.6-10/30 on seed 3. Corporate Raider mini-boss 198 HP: 9-17 s to half.
- **Act 3 (21-30):** all three off/human act-3 deaths are on `a3_crossfire` (room 22) or `a3_horde` (room 24), the second and fourth rooms of the act, before the boss. The two wins show lowest-HP dips to 3.8 (Rogue room 13) and 12.7 (room 28). The final Guardian fight itself was not the wall; the act-3 early rooms are.
- **Where runs die (data):** human Wizard: rooms 2, 2, 9; human Rogue: 24; off Rogue: 24, 22; off Wizard: 22. Won: 2 of 6 off-preset runs started (Wizard seed 1, Rogue seed 1). Compare STATE baselines (SIM1 human Rogue 19/36, Wizard 20/36; off 35/36, 31/36): my small sample (off, 2 of 4 wins on the `first` route) is below the batch off rate, which fits a bot on one fixed route.

## Fun, flow and clarity
- Flow in the run: fights are short (about 20-45 s) and punctuated by clear reward screens, which is a good rhythm. A late-act room 3 minutes of nothing never occurred; the rest/event/merchant rooms last 0.1-0.2 s sim time because the screens carry them.
- Clarity: the boss bar tells you armor, resist and current move ("Ground pound"). The pink ring and cone telegraphs on the [final boss](img/milestone-3/rogue-s1off-final-boss.png) are readable. A new player gets slot info on the card face.
- Dead moments: none seen. Overwhelming moments: Rogue 7-8 enemy crowds in acts 1-2 are the only cluttered fights.

## Where the bot limits me
- I cannot judge hit feel (hit-stop, shake, knockback), dodge satisfaction, aim comfort, or whether the 25-degree human aim error is "fair". Screens are still frames; I did not watch motion.
- The human preset presses slots at random, so "do slots matter in fights" cannot be read from bot behaviour. The preset also loses early often (Wizard 2 of 3 human runs died by room 9), which says "weak bot" more than "hard game".
- `first` route, first offer: the bot does not draft toward a plan, so pillar 1 is under-tested.
- `-Scale 4` drops fixed-fps, so none of these runs repeat from their seed; the seed controls map and offers but not fight outcomes.
- Real-time length is my estimate (decision screens times human reading), not measured. The only measured numbers are the sim/fight times above.

## Questions for the director
1. Should a run have any HP growth (max HP up from relics, rests, elites, a level)? Today a Rogue is 30 HP and a Wizard 22 HP from room 1 to room 30, and every act-3 loss I saw was one bad room.
2. Is the real-time target 15-20 minutes including a human's thinking, with "fight time" about 11 minutes, or should fights be even shorter? I estimate 20-28 minutes real, which is over.
3. Single-option map picks (about 1 in 3 map screens): OK to auto-advance, or keep for pacing and flavour?
4. Do you want leftover gold to matter (more shops, HP for gold, reroll), or should income come down?

## Proposals for game-lead (design changes, not decisions)
1. **Single-path auto-advance** on the map when only one onward node exists (keep a short "route ahead" glimpse). Saves about 10-15 screens per run.
2. **Run-wide HP source:** one new common relic or rest option "Max HP +4", or a per-act +max HP at the act screen, so act-3 rooms are not fought at the same 30 HP as room 1. Needed before BAL1 balance.
3. **Build-aware bot or hand playtest before BAL1:** add a `plan` route/draft policy to the watch bot (commit to a status, e.g. Bleed Rogue, Thesis Wizard) so a future playtest can judge pillar 1. Also needed to test `--deck=wizard-thesis` and `--deck=wizard-ward-battlemage` with the new Wizard decks.
4. **Gold sink or lower income:** reduce per-room gold, or add a second merchant offer (buy 4 HP, reroll).
5. **Relic tooltip clutter:** move combat relic proc popups to the badge strip.
6. **Boss variety:** the same three bosses every run caps the "no two runs alike" pillar; ask whether a second boss per slot is on the roadmap.

## Suggested next playtest focus
