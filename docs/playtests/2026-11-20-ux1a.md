# Playtest: UX1a combat HUD readability (2026-11-20)

## Build and setup
Commit 628cd1f (UX1a 1abef8c, qa PASS e732d2e). Scripted captures through `tools\capture_screenshot.ps1` at 1152x648 (Godot 4.7.2, windowed, RTX 3080). I did not hand-play. The bot stood still, so the Rogue died in its crowd; that is my script, not a game finding.
- Rogue: `--run-seed=1 --no-map --run-room=5 --relics=fight_club_membership+pyre_insurance+heavy_sigh+spite_knuckles`, auto attack taps.
- Boss: scenario `ux1a_boss_statuses` (Burn and Bleed on boss, 20 relics).
- Wizard: `--run-seed=2 --no-map --run-room=4`, slot taps.

## Smoke check: PASS. Blocker: NO.
Launched three times with no engine errors or warnings in the output I filtered (error/warn/refused: none). Floor, player, enemies and lighting visible; camera frames the player; HP 30/30, 22/22 and 9999 are sensible; scripted attack, aim and slot input responded. Dodge and manual movement were not exercised. Note: not a full console read, I grepped for error and warn.

## What got better
- **Field is cleaner.** Before ([rogue-s3-crowded-fight.png](img/milestone-3/rogue-s3-crowded-fight.png)) two "Fight Club Membership x2 / x3" name boxes sat on top of the fight and hid the enemies. Now the field shows only enemy bars and small numbers. ([x3](img/ux1a/rogue-crowd-x3.png), [x10](img/ux1a/rogue-crowd-x10.png))
- **Badges are readable.** "FC", "PI", "HS", "SK" are big and legible at 1152x648; the lit FC badge gets a cream frame and an `x10` strip under the letters, and the letters stay clear (before, `x6` covered "FC"). 20 chips fit in 5x4 with no clipping ([boss shot](img/ux1a/boss-arm-res.png)).
- **Armor and resist numbers read as words.** `[Arm 0.5]` and `<Res 1>` sit side by side with a gap and are understandable without a legend, helped by the boss bar line "Armor 50% - Resists all elements 50%".
- **Fire pill and swap pills.** "Fire" is a clear orange pill top-right on Hot Head, Fine, Burn Then and Flaming Nonsense ([wizard](img/ux1a/wizard-fire-pills.png)). The ready cards no longer say "Swap: free", so each card now shows type tags and "Next:" only. Much calmer.

## What is still unclear
- **Which relic fired.** The lit badge is a cream frame, and a name tag appears under the row ("Fight Club Membership" while FC is lit). In a still frame I can tell something fired, and which one, only because of the tag. In a real fight my eyes are on the enemies, not the top-left corner. With one proc I could find it; in a burst of several relics I would likely miss it. Needs a hand-play look.
- **Boss banner collides with the armor number: confirmed.** In [boss-banner-collision.png](img/ux1a/boss-banner-collision.png) "Ground pound" sits on top of `[Arm 0.5]` and the wind-up bar touches `<Res 1>`. Already queued for UX1b.
- **Early overprint: not reproduced.** I saw no stacked or garbled numbers in my frames (the boss shot 1.5 s in was clean). Not enough evidence to deny qa's overprint note.
- **Crowd numbers.** In the x10 shot the white damage digits (1, 3) are small and sit across enemy heads and bars; hard to tell whose number is whose. The mark over the first enemy's bar (red triangle) is unexplained to a new player.
- **Cards that are a tag-less swap.** With the free-swap line gone, a ready card shows no hint that it can be swapped. I did not check whether anything else teaches it.

## Director-facing questions
1. Is a cream-frame pulse plus a name tag under the row enough for "which relic fired", or do you want the badge to flash a stronger colour per relic?
2. Is dropping "Swap: free" from ready cards OK for new players, or should the first run show it once?
3. The red triangle over enemy bars: should it get a legend or word, as with Arm and Res?
