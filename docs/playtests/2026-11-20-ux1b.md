# Playtest: UX1b run-screen readability (2026-11-20)

## Build and setup
HEAD 3234c44 (UX1b qa PASS 6386562). Scripted captures through `tools\capture_screenshot.ps1`, windowed, 1280x720, Godot 4.7.2. I did not hand-play and did not run a watch-mode run (see "Not checked"). Shots in `docs/playtests/img/ux1b-playtest/`.
- Crowd: `--scenario=ux1b_crowd`, 6 timed shots.
- Boss: `ux1a_boss_statuses` as Wizard and as Rogue (`-AtBossAttack smash`).
- Map beat: preset `map_mini_boss`. Act screen: `--run-room=10` with `--run-auto-choose=relic:burnout_syndrome+skip`.
- Wizard real run: `--run-seed=2 --no-map --run-room=4`, scripted dodge and slot taps.

## Smoke check: PASS. Blockers: none.
Both classes launched with no error, warning or "refused" lines in the output I filtered (grep only, not a full console read). Floor, player, enemies and lighting are visible and the camera frames the player. HP is sensible (Rogue 30/30, Wizard 22/22). Scripted dodge and slots 1 and 2 responded as the Wizard: a dodge charge was spent and the slot bars show cooldown. Manual mouse and keyboard play was not exercised.

## What reads well
- **Teal Rogue and ivory Wizard.** Both are easy to find in a crowd and in a boss fight. The Rogue in [crowd-live_2](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_2.png) is the one bright cool shape among red and orange enemies. The Wizard in [wizard-run](img/ux1b-playtest/wizard-run.png) stands out from the red enemies.
- **Crowd digits.** With 7 enemies burning and bleeding, the numbers now sit around the pile and not on heads or bars. Each type has its own lane: white "1" and orange "2" repeat as a pair, so I read them as "bleed tick" and "burn tick". The pile's heads and bars stay clear. Big merged "12" values are readable. ([crowd-live_2](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_2.png), [crowd-live_4](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_4.png))
- **First-time status names.** "Bleed" and "Burn" show in pink above the pile, stacked and legible, and they are not on top of anything ([crowd-live_2](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_2.png)). On the boss, "Bleed x2" sits at the head and clear of `[Arm 1]` and `<Res 1>`.
- **Arm and Res.** `[Arm 1]` and `<Res 1>` sit on separate lines with a gap in both boss shots and do not overprint ([wizard-boss_1](img/ux1b-playtest/wizard-boss_1.png), [rogue-boss-banner_1](img/ux1b-playtest/rogue-boss-banner_1.png)).
- **Map beat.** The map shows the whole map, a ringed lone node, "Only one way on. Taking it in a second." under the title, and "Any key skips the wait." at the bottom. It reads as a calm beat and not a missing screen ([map-beat](img/ux1b-playtest/map-beat.png)). In the capture it was gone about 1.5 s after the room cleared and the fight screen followed, so the beat does end by itself.

## What is confusing
- **Whose number is whose (qa N7).** Honest answer: in a crowd I can tell which damage type, not which enemy. The ring gives lane and colour, not an owner. For burn and bleed ticks that is fine, because all enemies tick the same. For a big single hit, the number is not tied to the target I hit. That is a trade the brief accepted ("reads as belonging to its enemy" is only partly met).
- **Mid-fade touching (N7).** In [crowd-live_2](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_2.png) a faded grey "1" sits right under an orange "2" at the left and right of the pile, and they touch. It does not bother me in a still frame, and in a live game it would last a fraction of a second. I would not spend time on it. The pile itself still hides some of each enemy.
- **Pile is very dense.** At 7 enemies the HP bars and the triangle marks overlap each other, so the bars cannot be read one by one. That is a crowd problem and not new, but the digits moving off the pile makes it more visible.
- **The triangle still has no word on it.** It flips between red and orange in the crowd shots ([crowd-live_2](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_2.png), [crowd-live_4](img/ux1b-playtest/crowd-live_4.png)), and I could not tell why. I did not find a word or label for it in my frames. The brief says UX1b item 3 named it with a first-time label; I only saw the "Bleed"/"Burn" names and may have missed the triangle's label. Left for the director's eyes.
- **Wizard vs a hit boss (qa N3).** The Wizard is ivory and the boss body is a pale lavender in the idle shot, so colour alone is close ([wizard-boss_1](img/ux1b-playtest/wizard-boss_1.png)). They are still easy to tell apart, because the Wizard is a third of the boss's size and has a blue dodge ring under it. When the boss is hit-flashed or stunned it turns cream ([rogue-boss-banner_1](img/ux1b-playtest/rogue-boss-banner_1.png)), which is closer still. I think it is OK because of the size gap, but a Wizard standing beside a small stunned elite would be the risky case, and I did not capture it.
- **Rogue flashes pink-red when hit.** In [rogue-boss-banner_1](img/ux1b-playtest/rogue-boss-banner_1.png) the Rogue is red during the wind-up hit and looks like an enemy colour for that moment. Brief, but notable next to "distinct from every enemy colour".
- **Boss attack banner not seen.** I took shots during a `smash` wind-up and only the pink cone showed; no banner text appeared in the frames. I cannot confirm or deny the banner fix from my shots.

## Not checked
- **Act screen heal line.** I could not get a hurt-HP act screen: there is no launch flag for starting HP, and my run was at full HP. At 30 of 30 the screen shows no heal line, which matches "hidden at full HP" ([act-screen_1](img/ux1b-playtest/act-screen_1.png)). The line's wording ("+N HP (act heal 30%)") I only read in `data/run.json`. Needs a hurt-run look.
- **Key skip on the map beat** and **watch mode not pausing on the map:** I had no way to send a key during a timed capture, and did not run watch mode.
- **Starter relic label, "Bleed" on a real run's first bleed, crowded fight in a real run:** not done.
- **Tooling note:** `-AtBossAttack ground_pound` is refused (the ids are smash, slam, volley and so on). `-After` on a preset appears to merge with the preset's own times in the log. The `act_transition` preset (`--run-auto-choose=relic_skip+skip`) stalls on the boss relic screen, so it never reaches the Act screen; `relic:<id>` works. Suggest fixing the preset.

## Suggestions
1. Add a capture flag for starting HP so the act heal line can be shot.
2. Let the capture tool send a key at time T during the map beat, to prove the skip.
3. If a number cannot be tied to an enemy, tint or flash the hit enemy's outline for a moment, instead of moving digits further away.
4. Give the hit-flash on the Rogue and the boss a tint that stays clear of the other's colour.
