# UX1c smoke check, 2026-11-23

**Verdict: NO BLOCKER.**

## Build and setup
main at 87b922a (ux 0371dd0, qa PASS 91dfbe4). Windowed Godot 4.7.2 (RTX 3080), one instance at a time, via `tools\capture_screenshot.ps1` at 1152x648: `ux1b_crowd` (Wizard), `ux1c_strip`, `ux1c_hit` (Rogue and Wizard). These are scripted captures of still frames, not hand play. I did not drive the real controls by hand, and I did not capture the pause relic list myself: I judged item 1 from ux-designer's `docs/playtests/img/ux1c/relic-after-1152x648.png` and `hit-after-1152x648.png`. Observed facts and opinions are marked.

## Smoke check
- All captures launched, saved PNGs and exited with no errors or warnings in the console (observed).
- World, player, enemies, lighting and camera framing are fine. HUD shows HP 9999/9999 (scenario value), slot bars, dodge pips and five card plates with sensible text (observed).
- Controls in motion (movement, aim, dodge, slots 1 to 5) not hand-tested here. The scenarios did cast cards on a timer (log: "cast fire_fine_burn_then ... ok=true").

## Items
1. **Performance Review readout (from ux-designer's capture).** The pause list shows the rule ("gain 1 max HP (and heal 1) each elite room you clear") and a yellow line "Now: +1 max HP". Clear: rule, then running total. Yellow stands out from the white text. Fine as is. Only pause shows it; a player who never pauses never sees it (known, by decision).
2. **Violet hit flash.** In the ux-designer's hit capture the capsule goes violet at the same moment as a "5" damage number and the HP drop, and next to the pink enemy swing cone. I read it as "I got hit" (opinion). Violet is distinct from the enemy reds and the orange Fire and Burn tones. Wizard: his normal body is near-white, so violet is a strong contrast. His Heavy `#a070ff` appears on a cast (projectile or impact), not on his body, so I do not expect confusion (opinion, theory). **Not verified:** in my own runs I did not catch a flash frame (10+ frames at 0.05 to 0.9 s, Rogue and Wizard; the player stayed teal or white, only a heal "+9969" showed). The flash lasts 0.15 s, and the capture times seem to miss it. I did not see a Wizard flash or a Wizard Heavy impact side by side. A human check in motion is still owed.
3. **Crowd HP bars (my Wizard capture of a 7 pile).** Bars stack in a staircase above the pile, each beside its red pip, with "Bleed / Burn" text above. They read as one group belonging to the pile and none overlap. Observed: the tower is about 100 px tall, and the status name text sits right on top of the highest bar, close but not touching at 1152x648. The damage numbers float in a loose column well clear. Jitter and popping when enemies move were not tested (static frames only). Opinion: with fewer, taller enemies or a pile near the top of the screen the text could clip; check in a real fight.
4. **Card strip over an enemy (`ux1c_strip`).** Seven red enemies sit behind the top edge of the E and F plates. Card text (Fine, Burn Then / Kindling / tags) reads fine over them. Plates are clearly darker than the floor. Ready versus cooling: in this frame E and F are both orange-framed and E's name text is dimmed and its bar is grey-blue where ready cards have a green bar. That is the cue I use, not the plate tone (the plates look almost the same shade). So a ready card reads by its bright text and green bar, and a cooling card reads by dim text. It works, but it is subtle (opinion).

## Top things that work
- Hit flash colour is distinct from every enemy and element hue.
- The stacked bars keep the pile readable.
- Dense plates fix the text-over-enemy problem.

## Problems (ranked, all minor)
1. Ready versus cooling now relies on text dimness and the bar colour, since plate tone barely differs. Suggest a slightly stronger cue on cooling, for example a dimmer frame, if players miss it.
2. Violet flash not seen in motion by me; the 0.15 s flash is easy to miss on a short capture. Suggest a human look at the Wizard (Heavy cast, then take a hit).
3. Bar tower plus status text is tight at 1152x648 for a pile of 7. Watch for clipping at the top of the screen.
4. Performance Review "Now" line appears only on pause.
5. Capture scenario `ux1c_hit` is hard to time for the flash (tool note). A `--press`-style hook or a longer flash hold for capture would help.

## Eyes items 68, 69, 70
- **68 (violet): keep.** Nothing found that argues for the fallback. Not verified live on the Wizard.
- **69 (stacked bars): keep.**
- **70 (denser plates): keep.** Optional: ask for a stronger cooling cue.
