## Checks
**1. Steering**
- No stacking, metric not vacuous: PASS. The dev's test passes alone with steering on (>= 60% spread) and the "off" case falls below it as intended (in my diagnostic, 12 chasers with steering off pile up at 2.4 to 3.5 m and only 6 to 8 of 12 ever get to swing; with steering on, 11 to 12 do). Cap crowd: no pile, 11 to 12 of 12 start a wind-up in 12 s (a full ring around the player cannot hold all 12).
- Deterministic with a fixed seed: **PARTIAL.** Positions come from a seeded RNG, but the swirl side is `get_instance_id() % 2` and the on-top direction is `get_instance_id() % 628`. Instance ids depend on everything allocated before, so the same seed behaves differently in a full suite vs alone. This is why the speed test fails only in the suite, and why the failure count changed between runs (11, 5, 0). F1.
- No per-frame allocation: PASS in the steering code (static array walked in place, scalar math, no arrays or dictionaries built per frame).
- Roster hygiene: PASS. My tests: free(), queue_free() and freeing the whole Main all return the roster to baseline; 5 spawn-and-free cycles of 12 leave no duplicates or freed entries; every entry is valid and in the tree. Latent issue F2: `_ready` appends once and `_exit_tree` erases, so an enemy removed from the tree and re-added is no longer in the roster (pinned by `test_pin_reparented_enemy_drops_out_of_roster`). Nothing in `src/` reparents an enemy today.
- Speed clamp: **FAIL, S5d-1.** The chase branch is clamped to `move_speed` (clamp applied before knockback is added). The in-range, waiting-out-cooldown branch is not: `velocity += slide * move_speed * strength` with strength 1.5. Measured with a seeded 12-chaser crowd: max flat speed 7.48 (seed 4242), 5.58 and 6.05 to 6.17 in other runs, against a move_speed of 4. It happens in the first second after an attack (cooldown about 1.18 s left, distance about 1.8 m).
- No steering during a wind-up: PASS. `_physics_process` runs the telegraph branch before `_chase_and_attack`; velocity there is knockback only. The dev's test pins position and facing for 10 frames. Telegraphs still match hits (the attack resolves from where the wind-up started). The post-attack slide happens after the hit, so it cannot move a telegraph.
- Walls: PASS. 12 enemies (chasers and golems) crowding a player in the arena corner for 600 frames: all finite, all within +-19.6, none fell. Steering feeds `move_and_slide`, so walls still block.
- Jitter and soft lock at the cap: PASS. After 420 frames of settling, heading reversals between consecutive frames stay under 5 percent of samples; 11 to 12 of 12 attack. No stall over 10 s in any sim.

**2. Stats to data**
- Every value equals the old scene value: PASS, checked by hand against `git show 5e6ea32 -- scenes src` (not just the dev's test). Chaser: speed 4.0, range 1.5, damage 5, cooldown 1.2 set in the scene; the rest were Enemy.gd defaults (preferred_range 6, telegraph 0.45, projectile 10/20, physical, not ranged) and match data. Shooter: 3.0, ranged, preferred 7.0, damage 4, cooldown 1.6, projectile 9/18; attack_range default 1.6 and telegraph default 0.45 match. Golem: 2.0, range 2.0, damage 9, cooldown 1.8, **telegraph 0.7**; rest defaults match. My test also asserts no stat key remains in any enemy scene.
- Validator: **FAIL, S5d-2.** `validate_stats()` is only called from tests. `validate()` and `validate_enemies()` never call it, so a missing, misspelled or wrongly typed stat in `data/enemies.json` passes validation. With a missing block, `get_config()` then dereferences null at `parsed["enemies"][id]["stats"]` instead of printing a clear message. The function itself works (the dev's direct tests pass); it is just not wired in. Fix: in `validate_enemies`, append `validate_stats(enemy_id, entry_dict.get("stats"))`.
- Golem 0.7 s wind-up: PASS (D6 kept).
