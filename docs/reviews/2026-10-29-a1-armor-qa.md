Caveats the sim cannot remove:
- Single fights are noisy: standard boss fights range 48.2-86.7 s over the 8 seeds. The 7% gaps between fire, physical and standard are inside that spread. Only the lightheavy gap is outside noise.

That is the sim's room limit, not a game soft lock, but note the final boss takes a light and heavy only deck 3 minutes or more.
- Elite time includes escorts and travel, so the elite ratios are coarse.
- Minimums are skipped through the tool-only `RunSession.skip_deck_minimums`, so these are not legal decks.

## 7. Findings

**Blocking: none.**

Non-blocking:
- **N1 (latent, ux).** F3 `resist_summary` and the boss bar's `resists_uniformly` judge "all elements" by the table's own keys. With one element in the game a fire-only golem reads "all elements 50%". When a second element ships it will keep saying "all" for a fire-only table. Compare against `TierRules.element_ids()` instead. Also the `target_line` doc comment says "(50% off physical)", the format does not print that.
- **N2 (ux, fix before the playtest).** At 1024x768 the widened boss bar (480 -> 560) overlaps the player HUD: its left edge (x 235) and the "Phase 1 of 3" text sit over the player's HP, LMB and RMB bars (they end at x 252); the left diamond lands on the HP bar. At 1280x720 and up there is no overlap. Repro: `capture_screenshot.ps1 -Scenario boss -After 3.0 -TrueSize 1024x768`.
- **N3 (data safety).** The damage type is only checked as a non-empty string. A typo such as "Physical" or "Fire" falls through both channels and is mitigated by neither. All 27 shipped types are valid (`physical`, `fire`; my sweep test covers `/data` and fails on an unknown type), but a validator in the card and boss schema against `non_elemental_types` plus the element ids would close it.
- **N4.** Resistances of non-boss enemies (the golem's fire 0.5) live in the `.tscn`, not in `enemies.json`. Pre-existing, but it means the "armor optional per enemy in data" has no resistance twin.
- **N5.** `armor_reduction(INF, ...)` is NaN (`INF / (INF + K)`). Not reachable from data (validators cap at 1000) but a modifier stack could in theory; untested and unguarded.
- **N6.** The sim's `lightheavy` family hits the 240 s room limit more often after armor (4 of 8 vs 1 of 8, God mode). Bot capability, but if the director wants a pure light and heavy build to be viable, the final boss takes about 3.3 minutes for it.
- The boss capsule overlaps the bar's text line in the `boss` scenario at all sizes (scenario placement; the boss stands under the bar). Pre-existing.
