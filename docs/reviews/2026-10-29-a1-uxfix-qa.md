## Validators
- `pip_scale`: fail-loud in `UiConfig.validate_hp_bars` (number >= 1), with bad values in slice5_qa_test. Consistent with the other hp_bars keys. My test also checks that removing the key is reported.
- `boss_bar_gap`, `boss_bar_stack_below`, `boss_bar_stack_extra` (hud block): **no validator and not in `DEFAULT_HUD`**. No hud key has a validator, but the others have defaults. `HudLayout.boss_bar_stacked_width` reads `cfg["boss_bar_stack_below"]` directly, and `boss_bar_rect` reads `boss_bar_stack_extra` directly, so deleting a key errors at use time, not at load. `boss_bar_gap` silently falls back to 8. Non-blocking. I added `a1_uxfix_qa_test.gd` to pin that the three keys exist, are positive numbers, and that the stack threshold is below the authored width. Suggested for ux-designer: add the three keys to `DEFAULT_HUD` (or a hud validator).
