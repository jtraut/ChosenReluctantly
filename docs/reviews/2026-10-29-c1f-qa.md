## 4. Capabilities (all covered by existing tests, run in the full suite)

- chance (0, 1 and partial; seed repeatable; chance 0 or 1 draws no RNG), hits and hit_interval on cone, aoe and projectile, `hit_interval` required when hits > 1.
- Bleed: physical, ignores resistance and the cap, refreshes without stacking and keeps tick phase. Regeneration: heals per tick, stops at full and when dead.
- Leech: fraction, per-hit cap, counts HP actually removed (no overkill), uses resisted damage, DoT ticks are credited to the applier, a freed applier does not break the tick. All damage sources go through `Hurtbox.receive_damage` -> `Health.take_damage(source)`; I checked every `receive_damage` call site (cone, aoe, projectile, zone tick, player dash path) passes the caster. New qa tests confirm real HP gain from a cone hit, a projectile hit, a Fire Wall zone tick, Lunge path damage and a Bleed tick.
- Zone: pooled, capped at 6 (oldest ends), ends when its caster dies or is freed, safe with no manager or an orphan caster, goes away with the world on reload, ticks are `KIND_DOT` (no hit-stop).
- Dash: path damage hits each enemy once, a fast dash does not skip one, `direction_mode` forward and away_from_aim.
- Pass-through: restored on end, death and reload; overlapping grants end with the last; the deferred restore when the player is inside an enemy is covered (`c1e_cues_test > test_the_ghost_look_lasts_until_the_player_is_clear_of_enemies`, `c1c_solid_enemies_test` corner case). I read the `Player.gd` logic: a stale timer firing after `_end_all_pass_through` is a no-op (count is checked), so no double restore.
- aoe `delay` (locks the centre, cancelled if the caster dies) and `max_range` clamp.
- Solid-enemies flag, both values: masks, walking into an enemy, dodge not passing through, crowd cannot shove the player through walls (true and false), separation steering still spreads, backward leap and dodge into an enemy stay on the floor, spawn never pops the player at the origin.
