## 3. Bot sim (`-Run -Seed 1 -Runs 12 -Scale 8`)

The per-run line now shows HP at each room end, deck size and per-slot counts, picks, rejects used, removals and rest heals. Rejects are used (1-3 per run) and removals happen (1 removal in 9 of 12 runs; rest heals in 2). Final decks 16-20 cards; every slot stays at 3 or more uniques. Mean room-end HP is 72-90% of max, so attrition is mild. Room 8 (placeholder boss) ends at HP 1-4 in many runs, and 5 of the 6 losses are in room 8 (the sixth is room 2). A scale-8 run does not repeat from its seed (the tool says so), so the won/lost split is indicative only.
Pick rates have OUTLIERs (for example `fire_flaming_nonsense` 0.12x, `rogue_overhand_whatever` 2.08x). They follow ownership: the bot only casts what it holds, so reward-only cards that were not picked rank low. Not a card defect; the detector is doing its job.
