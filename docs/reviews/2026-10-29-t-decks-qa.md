## Sim re-measure (the A1 follow-up)

Method: `tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Run -God -Seed 1 -Runs 12 -Deck <preset>` (seeds 1-12; god mode so every seed reaches the boss; `--fixed-fps 60`; same bot and seeds in every family). Boss fight is spawn to death as the sim prints it. LightHeavyOnly used `-RoomTimeout 900`; all 12 fights finished (0 timeouts). All 5 sets: WAVE SIM OK, 12 of 12 bosses died. To read the deck at the boss I added a one-line print to `tools/wave_sim.gd`, ran, and reverted it with `git checkout`; it is not committed.

| Family | Boss fight median (s) | Mean (s) | Range (s) | Median multiple of standard | Per-seed multiple, min to max | Deck at boss, mean fire / rogue / neutral (fire range) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| standard (starting deck) | 63.1 | 62.6 | 48.2 - 78.3 | 1.00 | - | 4.5 / 7.7 / 6.0 (3-6) |
| mixed-rogue | 63.1 | 62.6 | 48.2 - 78.3 | 1.00 | 1.00 - 1.00 | identical to standard (runs are identical: good identity check) |
| fire-heavy-rogue | 64.3 | 63.1 | 44.8 - 83.2 | **1.02x** (mean ratio 1.04) | 0.57 - 1.62 | 6.3 / 6.3 / 5.6 (6-7) |
| physical-rogue | 67.3 | 67.9 | 52.6 - 84.9 | **1.07x** (mean ratio 1.11) | 0.77 - 1.46 | 4.2 / 8.6 / 6.2 (3-5) |
| standard + LightHeavyOnly | 249.2 | 245.9 | 195.0 - 293.0 | 3.95x | 3.14 - 5.53 | same as standard |

**Headline:** over 12 seeds, fire-heavy is **1.02x** and physical **1.07x** of standard (medians). Both are inside 70-130%. This settles the earlier disagreement: A1 qa (0.93x / 1.04x, old bypass families) and the playtest (0.80x / 0.97x, 4 seeds) differed by noise. With legal decks no fire discount is visible. Single seeds swing 0.57x to 1.62x, so 4 seeds could not show a 20% difference.

**Dilution:** large for physical. Start Fire counts are 7 (fire-heavy), 2 (physical) and 4 (mixed); at the boss they are 6.3, 4.2 and 4.5. Physical ends with about the same Fire as standard, because the reward policy picks unowned cards and Fire is mostly what the Rogue does not own. Fire-heavy keeps most of its contrast (6.3 vs 4.5). So this does not test "pure physical vs pure fire". An undiluted comparison needs a sim mode that skips rewards or starts at the boss; neither exists (small sim feature, game-lead to decide).

**LightHeavyOnly** is far outside 70-130% (3.95x standard), but it is a bot policy, not a deck: it never casts the two attack cards. It shows that cards beat basic attacks against the boss (GDD section 3). For information, not a defect.

### Non-blocking (for gameplay-dev)
- **N1 (pre-existing flake, not from T-decks):** `run_reload_cycle.ps1` sometimes fails with `FAIL: reward pick: damage immunity leaked into a new room` (check at tools/reload_cycle.gd line 248, in `_check_run_fresh`). Repro: start `tools\run_wave_sim.ps1 -Seed 1 -Seconds 60 -Runs 2` in the background and run `tools\run_reload_cycle.ps1 -Cycles 5` alongside. On the checkout from before T-decks (e7b0029, in a worktree) it failed 2 of 3 runs under that load; on HEAD it failed 1 of 1 under load and 1 of 12 with no extra load. So T-decks did not cause it. Likely timing-dependent dodge or hit immunity still on when the room reloads. It makes the required soak unreliable and needs an owner. I did not edit or weaken the check.
