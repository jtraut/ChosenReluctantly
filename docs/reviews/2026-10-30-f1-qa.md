## Edge case: dodge inside the post-dash ghost window
**Reproduced** (`test_edge_case_dodge_inside_the_post_dash_ghost_window_passes_through`, which pins current behaviour): after a dash ends inside a body, a dodge started in the window travels more than 2 m through a second enemy. After the window closes (stepping clear, or the 3 s cap) the dodge is blocked again, pinned by `test_the_ghost_window_closes_and_the_dodge_is_blocked_again`.

**Severity: low. Recommendation: accept, and reword GDD section 3 to mention it.**
- The window exists only while the player's body actually overlaps an enemy (the clear margin is -0.05 m, so merely touching or standing adjacent does not count). It ends the moment the player is clear, and hard-caps at 3 s.
- Entering it needs a movement card ending with the player's centre inside a body. The cards have cooldowns, the dodge has its own 0.8 s cooldown and is invulnerable anyway, so the extra is some positional freedom, not a way to chain through crowds. Dodging cannot renew the window; only another movement card starts a new one.
- It is the same mechanic Shadow Step already had. No normal-play exploit found.
- If the director wants section 3 literally true, the smallest fix (gameplay-dev's) is to block a dodge start while `_restore_pending` is true. That costs the player a dodge exactly when stuck in a crowd, which feels worse. Not recommended.

## Findings
Blocking: none.

Non-blocking:
1. **Concentric launch quirk (pre-existing, not caused by F1, now reachable by more cards).** If the buff or the 3 s cap ends while an enemy's centre is exactly concentric with the player (offset 0.0), solid collision resumes with zero separation and depenetration launches the player upward (y about 93 to 135 m within 5 s). Reproduced with both the Rogue dash and a plain `grant_pass_through(0.3)` (Shadow Step's mechanism). Any offset of 0.1 m or more is fine (max y 0.001). Exact equality is essentially unreachable in play, so low priority. Suggested hardening for gameplay-dev: nudge the player out of the body when restoring, or hold the ghost until clear, and/or clamp y. No regression test added since it would fail today; probe for when the fix lands: enemy at exactly the dash end (4.5 m for Ugh, Fine, Moving), wait past `PASS_THROUGH_RESTORE_CAP`, assert max y below 0.5.
2. Stale comment `src/player/Player.gd` line 798: "press 1, press Ctrl, release 1 must still end the hold" should say Shift.
