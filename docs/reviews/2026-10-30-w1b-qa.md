## Flake: `w1a_test:482`
**Verdict: test timing, fixed (test only), root cause not proven.** The failing assert is "the auto-fire bolt landed within 90 physics ticks", after teleporting an idle chaser into range. 90 ticks is 1.5 game seconds only while `Engine.time_scale` is 1; the bolt needs about 0.4 s to cross 9 m, so any slow time scale or hit-stop leak (the README documents a related timer bug) shrinks the margin to nothing. I could not reproduce it: `w1a_test.gd` alone passed 3/3 plus 2/2 more after the change, and the full suite passed once. I did not apply CPU load (the environment declined it). Fix: both bolt tests (`test_the_wizard_bolt_does_not_reach_past...` and the reticle test, same pattern) now poll until damage lands or 4 s of real time passes, and the failure message prints `time_scale`, `can_act()` and `is_busy()`, so a recurrence points at the cause. This does not weaken the checks: the assertions are the same, only the wait is bounded on wall time. If it recurs with `time_scale` below 1, that is a game-side leak of hit-stop or slow-mo across tests and is a gameplay-dev bug.

## Non-blocking findings
- **N1. The non-kiting boss time is not robust at the 70% floor.** Pooled over 24 seeds the Wizard's non-kiting boss time is 73.1% (+- 2.8, one standard error) of the Rogue's with the default bot and 70.7% (+- 2.3) with `-DodgePellets`. One of the four cells (seeds 13-24, pellet-dodging) is below it at 69.8%. A 12-seed mean carries about 4 points of noise (Rogue boss times alone run 48-89 s), so "inside 70-130%" is a coin flip for the DodgePellets cell and passes with about 1 standard error of room on the default bot. The kiting policies are comfortably inside. Do not read the 72% in the DECISIONS table as a safe margin. This is a balance call for game-lead (for example one notch more Wizard damage); I have not changed any value.
- **N2. The pellet-blind kiting bot still dies in the boss room.** Seeds 13-24: 3 of 12 died in the boss room, 93% of the boss-room damage was volley (31.9 HP per run, against 26 HP). Seeds 1-12 had no deaths, which is why the tuning entry says "no deaths". With `-DodgePellets`, 0 deaths in 48 Wizard runs. So the P-dodge playtest's "cliff" is mostly a bot-blind-to-pellets artefact (as gameplay-dev found), but it is not zero for a player who does not dodge pellets: with 26 HP two full volleys kill. Not a one-shot, as the brief wanted. A human who does not read the Fan of regrets telegraph will die in the boss room; a playtester should look at whether the telegraph is readable.
- **N3. Tooling: `-DodgePellets` is opt-in**, so the headline default-bot numbers still over-punish the Wizard (kiting boss-room damage 20-35 HP per run default against 4-5 with dodging). game-lead has already decided to keep it opt-in for baseline comparability; flagging only that the two bots give different verdicts for the kiting Wizard (3 deaths against 0).
- **N4. Wizard light 80% to 50% and heavy 8.5 to 5.5 are design-facing numbers** the director has not seen as such (see the test-edit review). Not a GDD violation: GDD §3 only requires cards to beat the basic attacks.
- **N5. Untracked generated files left in the tree:** `tests/unit/w1b_content_test.gd.uid` and `tools/ux_capture.gd.uid` (other `.uid` files are tracked), and `docs/playtests/img/*.import`. I committed only my own `w1b_qa_test.gd.uid`. Whoever owns them should add or ignore them.
- **N6. `wall_crossing` has no automated check** of what it shows (Burn on the walkers, Golems taking less); it is a scenario for eyes, which was the brief. Verified by capture only.
