## 1. Switch off (checked by reading the diff, by the existing h2_test.gd cases, and by qa runs)

- Space casts Movement with hold-to-preview and cast-on-release; F casts Utility; Shift+Space and Shift+F swap without previewing or casting. Covered by `h2_test.gd` with real key events through `Input.parse_input_event`. qa added `h2_qa_test.gd::test_space_and_f_key_events_match_their_card_actions_and_never_the_dodge_action`: the Space and F events match `slot_4`/`slot_3` (and their Shift forms the swap actions) and never the `dodge` action.
- No dodge ever happens and charges are frozen: `Player._handle_dodge_input` and `_tick_cooldowns` return early on the switch, and `get_dodge_state()["available"]` is false. A press of the `dodge` action (which has no key) does nothing, and a spent pool stays spent over 90 frames (`h2_test`).
- Bots do not dodge: `-BossLog` on the shipped copy, Rogue seeds 1-6, prints `dodges pressed per run 0.0`; the same command on the dodge-on copy prints `18.7`. The wave sim gates every dodge press on `get_dodge_state()["available"]`, which is false while off, so no tool change was needed.
- Pips hidden, no "?" anywhere: `DodgePips` is hidden by `_process` polling `PlayerConfig.dodge_enabled()`, and `Hud._ready` sets the first state. The only callers of `InputLabels.for_action(&"dodge")` are DodgePips. No other UI string, card text or tutorial names the dodge (grep of src/ui and data). Flavor text on one card ("Slow enough to dodge") is a joke, not a prompt.
- Space never confirms game over, class pick, a reward or a run prompt: class pick, reward and run prompts already swallowed Space; GameOverScreen now swallows it in `_input` and ignores presses for 0.4 s (`is_armed`, real-time ticks). Tests cover each. Hint reads "Enter". The Try again dim timer uses `create_timer(..., ignore_time_scale)`, which TestClock documents as able to fire early after a hit-stop; here it only affects the cosmetic dimming, not the arm check, so non-blocking.
- **Pause accepting Space on its focused Resume button, without casting: acceptable, and my recommendation is to keep it.** Reasons: (a) pause and settings are menus the player opens on purpose, so a deliberate Space is not an accident; Space-as-ui_accept is the Godot default and removing it from `ui_accept` would change every screen; (b) the press is consumed while the tree is paused, the Player never sees it, and `test_space_casts_movement_after_resuming_from_the_pause_screen` proves no cast on that press and a normal cast right after, including past the post-resume block ticks; (c) the one cost is that a player mashing Space when the pause opens (Escape pressed mid-fight) could close it again at once. That is mild and Escape is the pause key. If the playtester sees it, the fix is to arm the pause screen as Game Over is armed; I would not do it pre-emptively.

## 6. The `w1a_test.gd` real-time flake family (fixed by qa, test-only)

Two failure modes, both in the Wizard bolt tests:
1. `test_the_wizard_bolt_does_not_reach_past_its_12_m_range...` (line 485): a 4 s wall-clock deadline. Under full-suite load with a hit-stop `time_scale` of 0.25 the game advanced too little in 4 real seconds, so auto-fire had not landed yet.
2. `test_the_wizard_bolt_flies_to_the_reticle...`: asserted total HP lost equals exactly 1.5. Auto-fire (not disabled there) can land two bolts before the loop observes the first, giving 3.0.

Fix (tests/unit/w1a_test.gd, nothing weakened):
- Both loops are now bounded in physics ticks (`MAX_WAIT_TICKS` = 1800), not wall-clock time. A tick count is independent of machine load, 1800 ticks is 30 game-seconds at full speed and 7.5 at 0.25, and the loop still ends at the first hit, so the normal case costs about 30 ticks.
- The bolt-damage check now records the first hit from `health.damaged` and asserts that hit is exactly 1.5 (stricter than before, and immune to a second bolt), and asserts the total lost is a whole multiple of 1.5.
- The failure message of the range test now reports ticks.

Verification: `w1a_test.gd` passes 30/30 twice while 12 Wizard run sims loaded the machine, and in the full suite. I could not reproduce the original failure on demand (it needs a hit-stop time_scale left over from an earlier test under heavy load), so "fails before the fix" is not demonstrated; the change removes both identified causes by construction.

Other wall-clock waits remain in the same style (non-blocking, not changed here): `attack_meter_test.gd:79` (6 s), `player_attack_test.gd:86` (2 s), `slice1_qa_test.gd:69` (4 s), `slice4_qa_test.gd:320` (4 s). The 2 s one is the most likely to be the next flake. Suggest converting them to tick bounds the same way.
