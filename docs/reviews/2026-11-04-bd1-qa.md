## 1. Review of the tests and content (unit tests, validation)

The gameplay-dev suites cover every required item well: tag derivation (19 cases, canonical order, conditional effects counted, keyword validation), each condition type true and false (the cast history window edges, zone ownership, per-target `target_has_status` in cone, aoe, projectile and zone paths), primed buffs (charges, snapshot so a card never eats its own prime, expiry through the controller clock, own-slot rule for `slot_cooldown_skip`), `status_damage_mult` (per status, only the applier, missing applier), aoe `anchor` (hits where the caster stood, validation, rejected with `max_range`), the 4 decks (legal, both lists, class pools, core cards), and the damage rule on every new Attack card with no `rule_exception`.

Gaps I found and filled in `tests/unit/bd1_qa_test.gd` (10 cases, all pass):

- **Zone per-tick re-check.** The dev test only covers the placement tick. Mine places an 8 s zone on an unmarked target (no hit), marks the target for 0.8 s (hit), lets the mark expire (hits stop).
- **Projectile that fails its condition is spent.** A marked target 3 m behind an unmarked one takes nothing; a control shot with no condition does hit the front target, so the zeros are not a missed shot.
- **D39 = A through a real `Player`.** A real dodge through the input path spends a charge, does not enter the cast history as Movement, does not make `rogue_drive_by` live and does not consume a Movement prime. The control casts a real Movement card and it does all three. The dev test only called `notify_dodge()` directly.
- **Card numbers vs BUILD_DEPTH §5.** 40 numbers across the 8 cards (radii, damage, cooldown modifiers, durations, knockback, windows, `anchor`) are pinned. Every one matches §5. The brief's open numbers (Drive-By cone 1.8 / 45 degrees, Momentum speed 16, Slow Burn range 8, status 3 s, and so on) are in DECISIONS and not pinned, as they are not in §5.
- **Conditional payoffs are extra, never the only damage.** Each of Fan, Drive-By, Heat Sink and Well, Actually has an unconditional damaging effect plus a conditional one.
- **`status_damage_mult` edge values** (0, 1, 1000, -1, -5): no NaN, no INF, no negative damage. Note: a multiplier at or below -1 is accepted by the stat stack and damage clamps to 0; data can reach it through a `buff` with a large negative value. Today no card does that. A BD2 relic that grants it should be validated (non-blocking note for gameplay-dev).
- **`keywords.json` failure paths** (empty `rule_text`, missing `display_name`, collision with a derived tag, a malformed file). The dev test only checked the shipped file. These pass, with fixtures in `tests/fixtures/bd1_keywords/`. A fifth case failed and found a real bug, see carry-over 1.

## 2. The slice6 flake (did BD1 make it worse?)

To compare fairly I ran that suite alone, 6 times at HEAD, then 6 times in a git worktree at 81ff8e4 (PL3 close, before any BD1 commit), same machine, nothing else running:

|---|---|
| 81ff8e4 (PL3 close) | **1** of 6 |
| d26e242 (BD1) | **2** of 6 (plus 1 of 1 in the first full run) |

The flake **exists before BD1** and I cannot show BD1 made it worse: 1 of 6 against 2 of 6 is within noise, and gameplay-dev's 2 of 3 is also in range. The failure text is the same each time: 7 of 10 enemies end 0.1 to 3.5 cm apart in x or z between the two runs, so the miss is larger than the 1 cm `PHYSICS_NOISE` tolerance the test allows, which means the two "identical" runs really diverge a little. At a failure rate of 20 to 35 percent this gate is unreliable for the whole project. I did not change the test (no weakening); it needs a root cause (owner below).

## 4. Specific items from the brief

- **Dodge-pressed leak between suites.** I could not find the leaking suite. Every suite that presses the dodge action (`h2_test`, `h3_test`, `p_dodge_test`, `slice6_qa_test`, `player_death_test`, `pl3_qa_test`) pairs each press with a release, and each calls `DodgeSwitch.reset()` or releases itself. gameplay-dev's release in `DodgeSwitch.reset()` is therefore a sound catch-all for every suite that uses the helper. The likeliest historical cause is an assertion failing between a press and its release. Nothing is left to fix; I consider this closed unless it recurs.
- **D39 = A.** `data/player.json` has `dodge.counts_as_movement_cast: false` (the code default is the same). A real dodge neither satisfies `cast_within movement` nor consumes a Movement prime (new test). Flipping the switch makes `rogue_drive_by` live (dev test).
- **Primed.** A card never eats its own prime (dev tests at the modifier and controller level) and a charge expires on time through the controller clock (dev test).
- **Projectile and zone edge cases.** A projectile that fails its condition is spent (code: `_spent` is set before the check) and my test shows it does not reach a marked target behind. Zone ticks re-check per tick (code and my test).
- **Known gaps listed in STATE.**
  - CastPreview does not read conditions. It only draws geometry, and the conditional effects share the unconditional effect's area in every shipped card, so nothing is drawn wrong today.
  - A buff aura outlives a spent charge. No shipped card combines an aura cue with `consume_on_cast`, so it is latent.
  - A delayed aoe with no `anchor` and no `max_range` telegraphs at the cast spot but hits at the caster. No shipped card is affected (`fire_fireball` has `max_range`; Parting Gift uses `anchor`). It is pinned by a test.
  All three are latent; none blocks.
- **New autoload.** `CombatEvents` relays only a clock and a cast history; reload soak asserts the history is empty after a reload and passes.
- **Performance.** The history is a ring buffer capped at 32 entries; no per-frame allocation seen in the slot-synergy view beyond small arrays. No concern.

## Carry-overs and non-blocking issues

1. **gameplay-dev: `JsonDataTable.get_entry` crashes on a non-object entry, and `KeywordDatabase.validate_table`'s "must be an object" check is unreachable.** `get_entry` is declared `-> Dictionary` but returns whatever the file holds. Repro: point `KeywordDatabase._table` at a file containing `{ "Primer": "just a string" }` and call `validate_table()`. Result: engine error `Trying to return a value of type "String" from a function whose return type is "Dictionary"` at `JsonDataTable.gd:18`, instead of the clean `keyword 'Primer' must be an object` message. It is loud (not silent), so non-blocking, but it breaks the "bad data fails with a clear message" rule, and the same pattern is used by every `JsonDataTable` consumer. Note: do not fix it by returning `{}` for non-objects, because other tables would then accept the bad entry silently. Test to add with the fix (fixture `{ "Primer": "just a string" }`):
   ```gdscript
   func test_a_keyword_entry_that_is_not_an_object_is_reported_not_crashed_on() -> void:
   	var problems := _problems_with("not_an_object.json")
   	assert_array(problems).is_not_empty()
   	assert_str(problems[0]).contains("Primer")
   ```
   I left it out of the suite because it fails today; `_problems_with` is in `bd1_qa_test.gd`.
2. **gameplay-dev: the `slice6` flake (section 2).** About 1 run in 3 in isolation, present at the PL3 close. Positions diverge by up to 3.5 cm, above the 1 cm tolerance. It needs a root cause, or a game-lead decision to quarantine; it will keep tripping full-suite runs.
3. **gameplay-dev: tooling for the BUILD_DEPTH §2 checks.** (a) damage by source per run (light, heavy, each slot, each status) so the fingerprint check can be run; (b) a way to swap one card in a preset from the command line, so removal pain doesn't need temporary preset files; (c) a 1-in-12 boss time-out in the standard Wizard boss runs skews the median; report timed-out runs separately.
4. **game-lead: balance flags (not mine to change).** Removal pain: 1 of 4 keystones passes (Controlled Burn +34%; Kindling +6%, Departure Lounge -5%, Hit and Run **-23%**, so removing Drive-By helps). Parity: Controlled Burn is 59% of the standard Wizard's boss time, below the 70% floor. Clear rates are fine. BUILD_DEPTH §2 says these tune in BD3, so this is a heads-up, plus a question on whether Drive-By's unconditional 6 damage is intended to be this weak.
5. **gameplay-dev: the no-card Rogue soft lock on seed 7 room 1**, pre-existing at the PL3 close (1 enemy alive, the bot never finishes it). The wave sim reports it as a SOFT LOCK failure, so `-Run -LightHeavyOnly -Class rogue -Seed 7` exits 1; the no-card gate should be read over seeds, not on seed 7.
6. **ux: "Cooldown 3 s" wraps with an orphan "s"** on Fan the Flames' reward tile at 720p and 1024x600.
7. **qa (me, deferred): tighten `bd1b_content_test`'s Fan cooldown check** to the first line (it is a test I do not own, so I left it; it is a one-line change for gameplay-dev or me next pass). Also the w1b tag-based skip, and the raw-newline string in `m3c_qa_test`.
8. **gameplay-dev: a `status_damage_mult` at or below -1 is accepted.** It clamps to zero damage, no healing. Add a validator floor if BD2 relics can grant it.
9. **Side effect to watch (already in DECISIONS):** the 8 new cards are in the pool-fill for the arena player, the bot sim and fresh random slots. Standard hashes moved for that reason only (verified above). 3d's availability field is where this gets controlled.
