## 1. The failing test on clean main: a test bug, fixed test-only

`pl3_tooling_test > test_a_scenario_as_the_wizard_has_the_wizards_deck_hp_and_dodge` set `main.player.character_id` before `add_child`. `Main.player` is `@onready` and nil until the node is in the tree, so it died on a nil base. The real tooling is fine: `Main._choose_character` reads `get_node_or_null("Player")`, not the onready var. Fix (test only): fetch the Player by path before `add_child`. It now passes. It still sets the deck itself, so by itself it proves little about the real path. The real evidence for the `--class` scenario path is the windowed capture below: the Wizard card row (Overqualified Bolt, Personal Space Enforced, Borrowed Focus, Sideways Blink, Eventual Healing) and the Wizard's dodge pip show in `boss_volley` with `--class=wizard`.

## 2. The slice6 flake

It has a 1 cm tolerance since M3a (`PHYSICS_NOISE`, engine pair-ordering noise). It did not fire in my 4 full runs, so I have no failing case to show a fix against, and I did not change it blind. The ux hand-off saw it fire next to the failing `pl3_tooling_test`, which left 80 orphan nodes in the physics world; that failure is plausible as the trigger (orphans change the physics server's handle history, the M3a root cause). With that test fixed the orphans are gone and the flake has not come back. Status: not reproduced in 4 runs; keep watching. If it fires again, the failure text (the per-enemy positions) is what is needed.

## 3. Coverage added: `tests/unit/pl3_qa_test.gd` (26 cases) and the `pl3_tooling_test` fix

- **Knockback.** 3, 6, 9 and 14 m/s hits each travel `v^2 / (2 * KNOCKBACK_DECAY)` within 15 percent (a 9 m/s smash: 2.25 m). A real dodge after a hit zeroes `_applied_knockback`, ends with no leftover velocity, and walking resumes. A 9 m/s hit into the wall stays inside the arena rect and measured **0.000 m pull-back** (closest approach 0.401 m equals the final distance; the new subtraction does not bounce the player off the wall). A killing hit with knockback stays finite and inside. Enemy.gd has no `_applied_knockback` and a chaser's knockback still moves it a sensible distance (the fix is Player-only). The existing `pl3_knockback_test` covers the 40 m/s wall case and the walking-speed-after case.
- **`--press` and `--at-boss-attack`.** `ScriptedInput` presses and then releases (on a private action, see below), sorts out-of-order presses, `CaptureArgs` validation (already in `pl3_tooling_test`). The anchor test: another attack does not anchor, `volley` does and zeroes the elapsed time, a boss added later is watched too. The 90 s error exit is a source check in the suite (it would kill the runner if driven) and a real run above.
- **Ward aura.** Cast through the real `buff` template: armor modifier +25, at least 3 pool effects appear (burst, ring, bubble), the effects are still up before the buff ends and gone with it (pool count returns to the baseline, armor modifier removed). A non-armor buff adds exactly 2 fewer effects (no aura). Auto attacks are off in these cases so stray VFX cannot flake the counts.
- **Card projectile look.** `ball_scale` 1 and 3 give the same collision radius and a 3x drawn ball. `validate_look` rejects 9 bad shapes (empty, wrong types, 0, negative, string, NaN, bad hex, a string, null); every shipped card with a `look` validates.
- **Damage numbers under the boss bar.** With the real Main, a real Boss and the real bar showing: a number projected inside the bar block ends below it; one already below is left alone; with no bar nothing moves; **with `camera_rig.shake(0.6, 3.0)` running for 40 frames the number never goes above the bar block** (checked every frame). Verified in the suite, not on screen: the windowed capture happened before a hit, so no live numbers were in the picture.
- **Volley gap tint against `Boss.in_threat`** (a live boss in WINDUP, the real `_lock_dir`). Every gap segment, sampled at its near end, middle and far end across its whole width, is outside every lane: 0 hits in 27+ samples; the lane centres are inside (control). No segment starts before 7 m (the computed first gap is about 7.9 m). At 6 m, 8 m and 11 m a sweep of the real `in_threat` across the first gap gives: 6 m no tint and no free gap (`-0.01 m`), 8 m a 0.41 m free gap and a tint, 11 m a tint where the free gap is wider. `TemplateVfx.boss_volley_gaps` draws exactly as many parts as `VolleyGaps.segments` and nothing for a zero aim, zero duration or no pool. VolleyProbe independently agrees: at 8 m a Wizard placed in the gap takes 0 HP, at 6 m it takes 10 HP (2 pellets) and at 4 m 10 HP, centre lane 5 HP each time.
- **Reward and removal screens.** Digits 0 to 9 and keypad digits change neither focus nor choice on the reward screen and on the removal grid (a full-deck remove menu, 7 digit keys pushed through the viewport); the hint no longer says "1-"; the right arrow moves the highlight; a real Enter (through `Input.parse_input_event`) picks the focused option; a real left click at a button's centre picks it. Reward options show a key cap label "F" after Recovery is rebound to F and the old "Key X" wording is gone. The helper builds a genuine three-card offer (one per slot type including Recovery) and asserts it has one, so none of this is vacuous.
- **Card strip and dodge pulse.** See finding 4 below. `dodge_pulse_duration` 0.6 and `dodge_pulse_grow` 7 are finite, positive and under 3 s; `boss_bar_gap` is not negative.

Test hygiene found while writing these (test side, not the game): some earlier suite leaves the `dodge` action held, so `Input.is_action_just_pressed("dodge")` is false for the rest of the run. My first versions of two cases used the real action and failed in the full suite but passed alone. They now release the action first (dodge case) or use a private action (scripted input case). Whichever suite leaks it is worth finding (I did not; it is a test-order problem, not a game one).

## Non-blocking issues

1. **ux-designer / note: the card strip is 3 px over at the supported minimum.** At 1024x600 the real slot rows need 115 px against a nominal 111.7 px (they spill about 3.3 px). The strip is anchored so the widget grows and nothing is clipped. I compared with da418aa's parent in a worktree: 108 px against 105 px, also 3 px over, so this is **pre-existing** (rounding of the 2 px row separation and the row heights at a scale of 0.83) and PL3's +8 px keeps it the same. At 720 p and up it is exact. Below 600 p it is up to 3 px (measured 0.7 at 480, 2.5 at 540). The slot also intrudes 3 px into the bottom safe margin at 600 p. The "Next: ..." text lines on screen at 720 p fit (capture above); I did not test clipping at 600 p. Pinned at 4 px in `test_card_strip_rows_fit_their_slot_at_small_and_large_windows` so it cannot grow unnoticed.
2. **gameplay-dev: `--scenario=X --class=bogus` prints the "unknown --class" error twice and carries on as the Rogue, exit 0.** The run's `--class` exits 2 with "Launch refused". It does print an ERROR, so it is not silent, but a capture could proceed with the wrong class.
3. **gameplay-dev: a scenario with its own `"character"` plus `--class=<other>` gives a mixed player.** Four shipped scenarios set `"character": "wizard"`. `--scenario=wizard_bolt --class=rogue` shows a Rogue card row on a Wizard body (purple) firing Wizard bolts: `_build` applies the scenario character, then swaps the deck and heavy to the launch class. Capture-only, but worth a one-line rule (the launch class wins, or the combination is refused).
4. **gameplay-dev: `-StartRoom 99` silently clamps to the boss room.** No message. Harmless.
5. **Design, for game-lead (D36 is already open):** the gap tint starts at a clear half width of 0.2 m, so at 8 m it marks a gap 0.41 m wide in which a Wizard standing still takes 0 HP and a Wizard 0.1 m off takes a pellet. It is correct (matches `in_threat`) but very narrow to stand in. Whether the threshold is too small is a feel call for the playtester.
6. **tech-writer: README is stale for PL3.** New flags `--press`, `--at-boss-attack`, scenario `--class`, `-StartRoom`, `tools/VolleyProbe.tscn`; the reward screen text ("Keys 1-N highlight", "Key Q now: ...") and the test count (1244 / 108 suites; now 1289 / 111). The capture script already documents `-Actions`, `-AtBossAttack` and `-Class` in its header.
7. **tech-writer: the knockback fix changes the feel of every shove** (a 9 m/s hit now moves 2.25 m, not 19.6 m); the changelog should carry old and new values. Director eyes item already raised by game-lead.
