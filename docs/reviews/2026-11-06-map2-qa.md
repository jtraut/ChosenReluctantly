## What I tested, and how

`tests/unit/map2_qa_test.gd` (22 cases). Written from the brief and the public behaviour. The dev's suites (`map2a_*`, `map2b_*`, `map2_ux_test`) were read and run green; I did not duplicate their cases.

1. **Random shop sessions** (160 seeds, both classes, caps 1, 2, 3 and 20, random gold, random starting relics, a given-up relic, random option ids including hostile ones like `buy_relic:nope` and `merchant_leave:::`; 45 steps each; seeded Fisher-Yates, no global RNG). Checked after every step: gold never negative; `gold_spent_merchant` moves exactly as gold does; relic count never above the cap; no relic both owned and given up; the removal price is always 50 + 25 x merchant removals; a refused action leaves the state byte-identical; a removal leaves the deck legal; a save at every step restores the same screen and an equal state; stock never holds a duplicate, an owned or given-up relic, or a boss-tier one. The run really exercised the rare paths: 223 relic buys, 93 swaps at the cap, 110 removals, 43 rerolls.
2. **Stock over 300 seeds per class:** exactly 3 relics (always at least one shop-only) and 3 cards, all distinct, relics from the class's own pools and tiers 1-2 only (act 1 odds), every eligible relic shows up for each class. A fixture proves the **fallback**: with no shop-only relic in the catalog the stock is 3 normal relics; with all eligible relics owned the shop opens with 0 relics and still sells cards; with 2 of 3 shop-only relics owned the last is still given the slot.
3. **Boss-tier relics are never sold:** a boss fixture that claims the `merchant` source is never stocked in 120 seeds, and one forced into the stock by hand is refused with nothing charged.
4. **Determinism:** same seed and node give a byte-identical visit across 80 seeds; another node differs; the same reroll sequence gives the same stock; the reroll count is limited to what the relic grants.
5. **Reroll:** sold relics and cards stay shown as sold through 3 rerolls, no owned or given-up relic is restocked, no duplicates.
6. **Prices from data:** with card price 77, removal 11 + 3 per removal and relic prices 7/8/9 injected into the context, every charge follows; the shipped numbers (40, 50 + 25n, and 30/45 for the seven new relics by tier) are pinned. Exact gold buys, one gold short refuses, double buys refuse, buying with no visit open refuses.
7. **Removal guard:** the starter deck blocks every card (reason `none_removable`, nothing charged, deck untouched); with a full deck I removed cards until the guard stopped it, and the prices paid were exactly 50, 75, 100, ...; a rest-room removal does not raise the merchant price; the count persists across visits.
8. **The swap at the cap:** buying opens the swap, nothing is charged; the screen offers exactly the owned relics plus Cancel; cancel keeps gold, relics and given-up list byte for byte; a bad give-up id is refused; a real swap charges once, keeps the relic count, records the given-up relic, and the given-up relic is not restocked by a reroll. A hand of boss-tier relics cannot be given up (`none_giveable`, nothing charged).
9. **Request for Quotation:** across the seeds where it is stocked: no reroll option before owning it, the reroll is refused before, bought and rerolled in the same visit, free, once only, and a new visit grants it again.
10. **Saves mid-merchant:** shop, removal list and swap restore the same screen for both classes (model level). **Scene level** (the real `RunController`, three scene spawns on a restored state): a save in the **removal list** reopens the removal list, a save in the **swap** reopens the swap with the same incoming relic and giveable list, gold unchanged; Cancel and a full swap then work after the reload and the live `RelicManager` follows the swap. (`open_for_room` resets `pending`, so this was the path most likely to land on the shop by mistake; it does not.) Damaged saves (every merchant key given null, a string, a negative number, an array and an object; a merchant pending with no visit; a pre-MAP2 save) load without a crash, drop the choice rather than show an empty shop, and clamp negative counters.
11. **The 7 relics.** The dev's behaviour tests are real-path (real status manager, real hurtboxes, real zone manager). Mine add: documented numbers pinned; **Severance Package on a dense cluster** (16 bleeders in a 4x4 block, 1.2 m apart, 3 HP each): the chain fires 8 bursts for 16 enemies, ends after 6 rounds, nothing left alive, bursts never exceed the enemy count; **ASAP Notice on a real Movement cast** (`CardSlot.use_card` through `StatModifiers`): cooldown x 0.85 on Movement only, Utility unchanged, and restored when the relic is given up; **Liquidate Assets through the real controller**: buying it plus a card (85 gold spent) gives `damage_mult` 1.01 after a reload, and the steps are pinned (0 gold = 0%, 99 = 1%, 100 = 2%). **Real sim runs with a trigger counter I added to the sim** (`relic triggers fired:` line; `-Run -Relics ...` with `-NoMap`, 2 runs each, all won): Wizard run 1 and 2: Grading Curve fired 47 and 45 times and dealt 237 and 164 HP of damage (10-13% of the run), As Per the Attached Document fired 37 and 30 times, Paperwork Fire fired 3 times in run 2 and 0 in run 1 (a Wizard bot casts few card projectiles that hit; it is every 5th hit, so 0 to 3 is plausible, but I have no run that shows it more than three times). Rogue with `-Deck physical-rogue` (the preset that holds Slash, Then Bleed): Severance Package fired 4 and 9 times and dealt 6 and 13 HP through `relic:severance_package`, As Per the Attached Document fired 20 and 21 times. ASAP Notice and Liquidate Assets are stat modifiers, so they show no trigger line; they are covered by my real Movement cast and the controller test above.
12. **D40, my own sweep:** see Evidence. The path-extremes walk is my own depth-first code over the raw nodes and edges (min and max rests, max merchants, dead ends), plus the MAP1 independent checker on top, which now also checks `min_rest` and the merchant estimate.
13. **The offers with the full 13-relic catalog:** because `bd2_qa_test` and `bd2b_flow_test` now cut the catalog to the original 6, I added a property run of the elite and relic-room offers (200 seeds, both classes, random caps, owned relics, random take, skip, swap and cancel) with the full catalog: offers hold at most 3 distinct relics, never an owned, given-up, boss-tier, shop-only or other-class relic; the cap holds; all four new pool relics were offered.
14. **A pin that the merchant is fully ON** (`test_the_shipped_data_has_the_merchant_fully_on`): weight 8, `relic_route` [6, 8], estimate [30, 30, 45], prices 30/45/65 with no boss price, `min_rest` 1, rest weight 25, `max_attempts` 1000. ## Carry-overs and non-blocking issues

Each has an owner and a slice.

1. **`--run-node` and `--run-gold` ignore bad values quietly, and none of the new launch paths has a test** (gameplay-dev, MAP3 housekeeping). Real launches (headless, `--quit-after`): `--run-node=abc` and `--run-gold=xyz` exit 0 with no message (ignored); `--run-node=-3` and `--run-gold=-5` are silently clamped to 0; `--run-node=999` prints an engine ERROR ("RunController: --run-node=999 is not a node of this run's map") and still exits 0. Every other launch flag refuses with "Launch refused: ..." and exit 2. They are capture-only flags, so it is not a play bug. `--merchant`, `--run-node` and `--run-gold` read `OS.get_cmdline_user_args()` inside `_apply_launch_args`, so they cannot be unit tested without a subprocess. Suggested fix: a pure `resolve(args)` like `MapLaunch.resolve`, which returns values and errors, then a test of it.
2. **Stock mix: Request for Quotation is in about 79% of shops, Liquidate Assets about 20%** (game-lead to decide; BD3 or MAP3 retune). Over 300 seeds per class: RFQ stocked 237 (Rogue) / 230 (Wizard), Liquidate Assets 61 / 66, As Per the Attached Document 72 / 72, against 17-100 for the normal relics (Lingering Embers 17 / 24, Heavy Sigh 32 / 18, Severance Package 22, Grading Curve 30). Cause: the shop-only slot is a weighted draw over the three shop-only relics, and the weight is the tier weight (RFQ tier 1 = 80, the other two tier 2 = 20 each), and RFQ can also fill a normal slot. Not a bug; the free reroll relic is on screen far more than the others. I changed nothing.
3. **Balance observations for game-lead** (not design changes): Rogue `safe` route fell to 10/12 (was 12/12 at MAP1, 11/12 at MAP2-A); Wizard `first` route boss median is 70.9 s (64.8 s at MAP2-A, 60.6 s at MAP1) with 11/12 won. Both are small samples (12 runs). The `relic` routes now win 12/12 for both classes and the Wizard `safe` route is 12/12 too. Nothing I ran isolates why the Rogue safe route lost 2 (the safe bot visited the merchant in 5 of 12 runs, and the rest weight and new relics also moved); the new relics widen offers (`-NoMap` moved 57.3 -> 57.5 and 62.5 -> 63.3, as announced). For the playtester's "is a relic route a real alternative?": by these numbers the relic route wins as often as the safe route for the Wizard (12/12 both) and more often for the Rogue (12/12 against 10/12), with 6 relics against 1.
4. **Liquidate Assets' live stat is refreshed only by a relic purchase** (gameplay-dev, optional, MAP3). `RunController.choose` calls `_sync_relics` for `relic_pick`, `relic_swap` and `merchant_relic` only, so spending on a card or a removal does not re-scale a held Liquidate Assets until the next room is built (which rebuilds from `RunState` and is correct, tested after a reload). No observable effect today, because the merchant room has no combat. It would matter if a later node let you spend gold and then fight in the same room.
5. **`Merchant.items()` marks a relic buyable at the cap even when nothing can be given up** (all owned relics boss-tier); the click is refused with `none_giveable` and nothing is charged (tested). Cannot happen with shipped data (no boss-tier relics exist yet). MAP3 adds boss relics, so make `items()` say so then (gameplay-dev, MAP3).
6. **The option parser is lenient:** `merchant_leave:::` is accepted as Leave (it splits at the first colon and ignores the rest). Harmless; recorded because an option id is user-reachable only through the `--run-auto-choose` debug flag.
7. **Reroll can show the same items again:** the reroll redraws unsold slots from the same pool without excluding the currently shown unsold ones, so a free reroll can return some of what was already there. I did not measure how often. Design call for game-lead; no rule is broken.
8. **STATE.md line 111** still says "The merchant stays off (weight 0) until ux's step"; it is on since `8994863` (game-lead).
