### What my suite (`tests/unit/map3_qa_test.gd`, 9 cases) covers beyond gameplay-dev's and ux's

- **Save across the act boundary:** act 2 with 0, 1 and 4 rooms walked writes, reads back as an equal RunState (`equals`), has the same reachable next nodes, keeps `past_acts` and the act-1 path, and `RunSave.summary()` returns act 2, acts_total 3, room 11/12/15 of 30. A resumed act-2 run then makes the same offers and ends in the same state as one that never stopped.
- **Hostile files:** every top-level field of a valid act-2 save replaced in turn with 14 wrong-typed or absurd values (null, strings, empty and nested arrays and objects, -1, 0, 1e30, true, 2.5), (26 fields by 14 values, 364 files). The loader never script-errors; any file that does load is a consistent live map run at a boundary with non-negative gold and a max HP. Files truncated at every 397th byte, and a binary-garbage file, are all ignored, with `has_save`/`summary`/`load_save` agreeing. `act` and `acts_total` edits (act 3 of 2, act 0, acts_total 10, wrong past-act count, act 2.5) never load as an act with the wrong history. Unknown cards and max HP 0 are refused.
- **Determinism:** the same seed gives byte-identical maps for acts 1, 2, 3 on four seeds, the acts differ from each other, and two identical act-2 runs make the same offers.
- **Exhausted pool in acts 2 and 3:** with every relic given up, an act 2 and act 3 elite, an act 2 and act 3 relic room, and an act 2 mini-boss (which draws act 3's tier 1/2-only odds) all fall back to one card offer of real cards, never an empty or stuck relic screen. These are real map nodes reached through `jump_to_node`, not a poked-up state.

## Bugs found (non-blocking)

1. **`RunState.from_dict` / `RunSave.read_state` accept out-of-range `hp` and `rejects_remaining`** (owner: gameplay-dev, severity low: needs a hand-edited file, never produced by the game). Reproduction: take a valid save, set `run.hp` to 0, -1 or 9999 (max 30), or `run.rejects_remaining` to -5 or 100000, and it loads as written. `hp` -1 is also the "not set yet" marker, so it would read as a free full heal; hp 0 may open the room dead; negative rejects may break the reject-pip UI; 100000 rejects is a free unlimited reject. I did not run the game on such a file to see which of these actually happen. Probe: `test_hp_and_reject_values_in_a_loaded_save_are_sane` in `map3_qa_test.gd`, **skipped** (`do_skip`) with the reason, so the suite stays green; gameplay-dev should clamp or refuse in `RunSave._state_problem` (hp in (0, max_hp], rejects in 0..`RunConfig.rejects_per_run()`) and remove the skip. That is the "fails before, passes after" test. Suggested slice: MAP3b housekeeping (or BD3a sitting A if it touches RunState).

## Carry-overs and non-blocking notes

- **Paused-tree leak (the ux carry-over):** root cause is `GamePause.reset()`, which clears `_paused` but never touches `get_tree().paused` (owner: gameplay-dev, non-blocking). Suites that open ClassPickScreen (`enabled_override = true` or Continue) only released the tree through the screen's own `_exit_tree`. I could not make any of them leak: with the guard removed from `w1a_ux`, `w1a_qa`, `pl2_ux` and `h2` and a probe suite run right after each, the tree was unpaused. Still, I added `get_tree().paused = false` to `after_test` of the 11 suites that touch the class pick (`h2`, `map1_qa`, `map1b_flow`, `map2_ux`, `map2b_merchant_flow`, `pl2_ux`, `w1a_qa`, `w1a_ux`, `map3a_housekeeping`, `map3a_save`, `map3b_acts`; `map1_ux` and `map3_ux` already had it). Test-only, one line each, full suite green. gameplay-dev could make `GamePause.reset()` also unpause when the tree is paused.
- **Bots end every act at 100% HP median on every route**, so "no free heal between acts" is not testable by the bot (rests and in-room play top it up). Information for the playtester and game-lead, not a defect.
- **Relic and gold targets are still MAP3b's:** relic route median relics 11 (MAP3b target 14-17), safe route 5 (target 6-8), unspent gold 444 (relic) to 680-902 (first, safe). Not gated in MAP3.
- **Rogue `safe` route wins 8/12** on 3 acts (Wizard 10/12). The MAP3 gate is the `first` route only; recorded as a baseline.
- **Accepted by ux, confirmed:** the HUD plate reads "Act 1 of 3" behind the Act 2 screen until the next room; resuming at an act's first map shows no act screen (the act-start map is a save point and Continue lands on the held map; covered by `test_continue_at_an_acts_first_map_screen_does_not_reopen_the_class_pick`).
- **A quit at a reward, rest or merchant screen resumes from the room's start (a won fight replays).** That is the documented design (a save is only at a map screen or a room start), and offers and gold are seeded by node, so a replay is not an exploit. I note it for the playtester because a player may read it as lost progress.
- **Perf:** `Boss._begin_windup` duplicates the attack dictionary on every wind-up when `damage_scale` is not 1. Act 3's damage multiplier ships at 1.0, so it is inert today; a one-per-attack allocation is not a trap.
- Test `.uid` files: **153 test `.uid` files are tracked in git**, so I am committing the stray ones (`map3a_housekeeping_test.gd.uid`, `map3b_acts_test.gd.uid`, plus the newly generated `map3_ux_test.gd.uid` and `map3_qa_test.gd.uid`).
