### 7. Save and Continue in the mini-boss reward

- Real behaviour: the game saves only at a map screen or a room start (`RunSave.is_boundary`), and `RunSave` refuses a save "with a choice open". So a quit inside the reward resumes at the mini-boss room's start and replays the fight. Neither the boss relic nor the card taken before quitting is kept.
- Tested with the shipped data and the real RunMain (`test_quit_at_the_boss_relic_offer_and_at_the_card_offer_then_continue...`):
  - the open boss offer and the open rare card offer both round-trip through `RunState.to_json` / `from_dict` with the same offer and `offer_rarity`;
  - quit at the card offer (after picking a boss relic), Continue: the state is the room start with no relic held; winning the room again gives the identical boss offer, and after the same pick the identical card offer.
- This matches MAP3's save boundaries. Note for game-lead: a player who quits after taking the boss relic loses it and must re-fight the mini-boss; the offers cannot be re-rolled by quitting (same seed, same stream).

### 9. Worst-case detonate (Exsanguinate at the stack hard cap)

Measured with the real `StatusEffectManager` and `Health` (`test_worst_case_exsanguinate_detonate_at_the_bleed_hard_cap`):
- Stat-raised cap (Blood Oath +9, Serrated Excuse +4, Bad Influence +4, Thousand Memos +4) is clamped to **10** by `stacking.hard_cap`.
- Bleed refreshed to the longest shipped duration (4 s) with both Bleed speed-ups (Synergy Workshop, Bad Influence: tick rate x2.0 summed): 16 ticks left.
- Detonate = 16 ticks x 1 x 10 stacks x 3 = **480** raw. Plus the 21 direct damage of the same cast.
- The detonate is a card hit, so `Health.take_damage` also applies `card_damage_mult_vs_status.bleed` (Blood Oath +25%, Salt in the Wound +25%, Synergy Workshop +15%, Hemorrhage Clause +20% = x1.85) and `damage_mult` (The Micromanager +25%, Action Item +25% = x1.5, before Liquidate Assets). Composed: **1332** before armor (480 x 1.85 x 1.5, exact).
- For scale: max health is 195 / 170 / 120 for the three bosses (armor 50 / 22 / 10 reduces it). The composed number is far above any health in the game; even the plain 480 is. It needs 10 stacks (several stack-raising pieces) in a 4 s window. Flag for game-lead and the playtester; I did not change anything.
