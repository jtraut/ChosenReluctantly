No bug found.
3. **A curse cast fires `card_cast` and can satisfy a payoff (report):** confirmed. A curse is cast like any card: `card_cast` fires and the cast history records the slot. What it can trigger today, found by scanning shipped data (the tripwire test `test_what_a_curse_cast_can_trigger_is_exactly_the_known_list` pins the list):
   - payoff card: **Peer Review** (Wizard, `cast_within` Utility 4 s) is satisfied by casting the Utility curse **Mandatory Training Module**;
   - relic triggers: **Faculty Meeting** (Wizard tier 3: every Utility cast releases an 8 damage nova) and **The Ivory Tower** (Wizard boss relic: 6 damage nova per Utility cast) fire on that same cast; **The Non-Compete Clause** (every cast) fires on any curse cast;
   - the other two curses (Property Damage, Reply-All Storm) sit in attack slots that no shipped payoff or relic keys on; Per My Last Email needs the Primer tag (no curse has it).
   - Is it worth exploiting? Mild for a Wizard holding Faculty Meeting or the Ivory Tower: Mandatory Training is a free extra Utility cast, so each cast is an 8 (or 6) damage nova for the price of the curse's own cost (Movement cooldown +3 s for 8 s). That turns the curse from a pure cost into a small trade. It needs the curse to be in the Utility slot and the relic to be held. I flag it to game-lead as a design call; qa changed nothing. Casting Peer Review's condition this way is the same Utility cast the Wizard already makes.
4. **Multi-give-up with all picks made, rest greyed, then moving focus:** done (`map4_qa_ui_test`). Focus is never lost over 18 mixed arrow presses; Confirm and Cancel stay reachable; a greyed tile can be read (focused) and Enter on it logs nothing; Enter on a picked tile unpicks it, keeps the focus on it and re-enables the other tiles.
5. **`RunSession.clear()` resets `--run-discount` and `--run-curses`:** **it does not.** `clear()` only sets `current = null`. `start_curses` and `start_discount` are "sticky for the launch" like `start_relics` and `debug_deck`, by the doc comment on them, so a "Try again" keeps them. ## Findings

Blocking: none.

Non-blocking:

1. **Brief versus code, `RunSession.clear()`** (section 6, item 5). Not reset; documented as sticky. Game-lead's call.
2. **Event curse exploit** (section 6, item 3). Utility curse plus Faculty Meeting, The Ivory Tower or Peer Review. Design call.
3. **Map generator headroom shrank.** Worst attempts for a three-act map, per act (limit 1000 attempts): act 1 278, act 2 312, act 3 **568**; MAP3 qa measured 79 / 86 / 126. 52% of act 3 seeds need over 60 attempts. 0 failures in seeds 1-1000 per act, and in seeds 50000-50999 (act 1 only, worst 309). Act 3's extended sweep over seeds 1001-4000: 0 failures in 3000 seeds, **worst 751 of 1000** (53% need over 60 attempts), so the worst case grows with the sample (568 in 1000 seeds, 751 in 4000) and the headroom is about 1.3x, not comfortable. At roughly 100-180 ms per map the worst seed costs a hitch of well under a second at the start of act 3, which is fine. Recommendation to game-lead: before DF1 or 3d touch map weights, raise `max_attempts` (data/map.json, 1000) or trim the `multi_act.types` event weight (28), because a seed past 1000 gives no map. A seed that hits 1000 gives no map (loud error, straight run), so DF1 or any later map-weight change should re-sweep. The cost is about 100 ms per act map on average (1000 maps in 100-180 s).
4. **Event bot coverage.** The shipped policies take the first available choice, so 9 of 17 non-leave choices are never exercised in the `first` runs (E1b, E2b, E3b, E4b, E5b, E6b, E7a, E7c, E8b unseen in 144 runs; the `relic` and `safe` runs add the cost-free and relic picks). qa closed it with the forced-choice sweep test; a sim mode that takes a random choice would be the permanent fix (sim tooling, gameplay-dev or qa later).
5. **Two medians are on the lower edge of their ranges:** `relic` 14 (range 14-17) and `safe` 5.0 on two of four cells (relaxed range 5-8). Fine today; DF1 and 3d should watch them.
6. **Human Rogue 21/36 is on its floor** (21). Within noise, DF1 re-gates.
