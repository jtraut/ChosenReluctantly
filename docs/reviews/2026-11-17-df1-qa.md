## Blockers

1. **`k1_key_hint_scan_test::test_no_json_value_hardcodes_a_slot_or_input_key_hint` fails (1 failure).** ux's new `data/run.json` `event.remove_curse_line` reads "... swap past it (Shift + the slot key)." The K1 rule forbids a key hint hardcoded in JSON. Repro: `godot --path . -s --remote-debug tcp://127.0.0.1:0 res://addons/gdUnit4/bin/GdUnitCmdTool.gd -a res://tests/unit/k1_key_hint_scan_test.gd -c`. The scan names the file and string. Fix: reword without a key, or build the key text from `InputLabels` as the slot hint does (`slot_swap_key`). Not weakened by me.
2. **`mb1b_test::test_no_one_duelist_sequence_does_more_than_ten_and_three_in_a_row_stay_under_the_note` fails (6 failures).** Sitting B's act 2 damage 1.05 scales the act 2 duelist: its hardest sequence is now 10.5 (was 10), and three in a row 30.45 (limit under 30); the per-hit list is `[7.35, 9.45, 10.5]` against `[7, 9, 10]`. Sitting B reported "full suite not run", so this was never seen. The MB1 note ("a full-HP Wizard (22 HP) survives any two sequences, at most 20") still holds at 21 for two, but with 1 HP to spare, and the three-in-a-row design cap now breaks. **This is a design decision for game-lead**: either the note's limits are scaled by the act damage (then the test changes, with a reason), or the duelist is exempt from the act multiplier, or the multiplier is lowered. qa does not change the test or the value. Repro: run `tests/unit/mb1b_test.gd`.
3. **Act-change map failure ends in a dead screen.** Sitting A made it `run_lost`, but nothing in `src` listens to `run_lost`: the Game Over screen is driven only by `Main._on_player_died` -> `game_over_ready`. With the player alive, the run is inactive (`_active=false`, `set_process(false)`), `outcome` "lost", and neither a win nor a Game Over screen appears: the player stares at the cleared room with no way on or out (the pause menu may still work). qa test `df1_qa_test.gd::test_an_act_change_map_failure_shows_the_game_over_screen_with_the_player_alive` fails with that message, and is **red on purpose** until the fix (as the SIM1 precedent). Rare (0 failures in 3000 seeds at `max_attempts` 2000, worst 751), but the task defined this fix as "a loss". Fix: have Main or `GameOverScreen` listen to `run_lost` (call `_announce_game_over`), and test it.
