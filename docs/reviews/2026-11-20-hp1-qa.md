### Attribution (SIM1 human Rogue, pooled 36; off Wizard, pooled 36)
Method: a throwaway copy of the working tree under the scratchpad (nothing in the repo was edited, so `git status` has no data or source changes). Two variants, same seeds and command:
- **zeroed:** both relics stay in the pools with their data unchanged, but `RunMaxHp.gain` returns at once in the copy (a test-only override; gives no max HP and no heal).
- **removed:** the two JSON files deleted from the copy's `data/relics`.

| Run | Rogue human | Wizard off |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-HP1 baseline (reported) | 19 | 31 |
| removed (my run) | 19 (6/3/10) | 31 (12/10/9) |
| zeroed (my run) | 24 (11/5/8) | 33 (12/12/9) |
| HP1 as built | 25 (11/5/9) | 34 (12/12/10) |

Reading:
- **Removed reproduces the baseline exactly (19 and 31): the HP1 code changes by themselves move nothing.** No drift from the new `_push_state_hp_to_player` calls.
- **Zeroed minus removed (+5 Rogue, +2 Wizard) is the offer reshuffle:** two extra neutral relics in the pools change which relics are offered and which random draws follow. The relics are inert in this variant, so this is pool dilution and a shifted random stream, not a power effect.
- **Built minus zeroed (+1 each) is the max HP itself.**
- So about 5 of the 6 extra human Rogue clears are the reshuffle, and the max HP is about 1. Noise caveat: 36 runs at a 0.6 clear rate have a binomial SD of about 2.9, so even the +5 is under 2 SD and the max HP effect (+1) cannot be told apart from zero at this sample size. The reasonable reading for BAL1 is that the Rogue ceiling trip is mostly seed noise, not the relics' strength. It would take a larger sample (more seeds) to settle; I did not run one.

### Schema
Verified by the build's tests and by reading the diff:
- `RelicSchema` rejects a non-additive or `per_run_stat` max_hp modifier, a value that is not a whole number from 1 to 20, a `gain_max_hp` amount of 0, and `gain_max_hp` on any event other than `room_cleared`.
- `CardSchema._validate_effect` rejects `gain_max_hp` outside a trigger.
- `EffectTemplates` rejects it inside a card buff's triggers and validates `amount`.
- A card cannot carry the effect (`test_a_card_cannot_use_the_gain_max_hp_effect`).
