## Non-blocking issues
1. **Bare `--watch-shots` (no `=`) is silently ignored** and runs the normal headless sim (exit 0, nothing watched). `WatchMode.ARG_PREFIX` is `--watch-shots=`, so `is_bare` only catches `--watch-shots=`. Unreachable via the ps1 script; only affects a hand-typed Godot command. Fix: also match the exact arg `--watch-shots`.
2. **"HP at end" on a lost run is stale**: log and seed-5 RESULT said `HP at end 17.4` (seed 26: `22.0`) for a player who died at 0 (`RunSession.current.hp` is not updated on death). The end-screen shot shows 0/22. Misleading in the text log; use the player's last HP or 0 on a loss.
3. **Crowd lines use the act-local room index**: in act 2 and 3 the log says `CROWD ... in room 1` / `room 3` while ROOM/CHOICE lines say room 11/13 (`int(room["index"])`). Also the BOSS lines say `in room 10` in act 3 (room 30). Cosmetic, but confusing when matching a shot to a room.
4. `crowded-fight.png` is overwritten per bigger crowd, so each CROWD log line (4, 5, 6...) points at the same file; fine, but the file shows the final biggest crowd only (it was 6 to 7 enemies, readable).
5. The `-StartRoom 10` shortcut gives a bot at preset off with act-1 skipped; I used it only to reach the boss and act-end paths cheaply.

## Gaps for the Milestone 3 playtest

- I could not verify real-time `-Scale 1` behaviour (frame cost, the 12 s death wait) by a full run.
