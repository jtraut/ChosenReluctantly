3. **Legible at 1152x648, 20 relics fit: met.** The boss captures show 20 chips in 5 columns by 4 rows, 40 px chips, font 18, no clipping, at 1152x648, 1280x720 and 1366x768 (ux's captures, which I opened at 1152x648). `chip_layout` is tested at six sizes.

5. **Swap pills: met.** `ConditionCue.pill_lines(..., swap_ready, swaps_locked)`: ready drops "Swap: free" only, locked drops both swap lines (curse and free), and a ready curse card keeps its curse line. `Hud` passes `swap_locked()`, and the widget redraws on a change. The test covers cooling, ready, ready-curse and locked.
6. **Fire word pill keyed on pool data: met.** `ConditionCue.element_mark_text` reads `PoolDatabase.get_definition(pool).kind == "element"` and `tag_for_pool`; no card ids. Test: Kindling and Hot Head give "Fire"; a neutral, a curse and `{}` give "". Captures show "Fire" top-right on Fine Burn Then, Kindling and (run capture) Ember, Whatever. The class-pool Drive-By shows none.
