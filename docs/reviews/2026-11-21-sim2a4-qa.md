- `test_the_starter_guard_alone_turns_a_removal_into_back`: for each starter of both classes, with a second non-starter card of the same slot added (so last-of-slot cannot be the reason), the same offer gives `remove:<starter>` with no starters known and `back` with the starters known. Asserts at least one case ran.
- `test_an_extra_copy_of_a_starter_can_go`: a second copy is removable under the rule.
Card ids come from data. With these the guard is covered; no further strengthening needed before PASS.
